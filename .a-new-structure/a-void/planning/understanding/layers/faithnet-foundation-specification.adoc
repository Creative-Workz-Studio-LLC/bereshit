This document captures the foundational understanding of FaithNet — the Kingdom network architecture. Like MillenniumOSfootnote:[MillenniumOS: The ternary operating system running on binary hardware] runs ternary on binary hardware, FaithNet runs Kingdom network on existing internet infrastructure. ====
*#The internet is the Tree of Knowledge of Good and Evil for computing.#* It contains all information — good and evil. FaithNet doesn't hide from it; it provides agency, wisdom, and protection to interface with it without being consumed by it.
====

// # T.2 Architecture [ARCHITECTURE]
//
//	How FaithNet maps to existing internet
//

[[architecture]]
== Architecture

FaithNet is designed for native operation but mapped to current systems.

=== The Mapping Layer

Like trit-byte mapping for MillenniumOS:

[source]
----
FaithNet Protocol
       ↓ encode (pack)
Internet Protocols (TCP/IP, HTTP, etc.)
       ↓ transmit
Internet Infrastructure
       ↓ receive
Internet Protocols
       ↓ decode (unpack)
FaithNet Protocol
----

=== Core Operations

The foundational operation — like echo for CPI-SI I/O:

**Can two CPI-SI instances communicate through FaithNet?**

[cols="^1,3,4",options="header"]
|===
| Step | Operation | What Happens

| 1
| *Compose*
| CPI-SI creates message (OmniCode format)

| 2
| *Encode*
| Map FaithNet message to internet protocol

| 3
| *Transmit*
| Send over existing internet

| 4
| *Receive*
| Other end receives internet packet

| 5
| *Decode*
| Unmap back to FaithNet protocol

| 6
| *Verify*
| Test against codex (Scripture-based verification)

| 7
| *Process*
| CPI-SI processes verified message

| 8
| *Respond*
| Complete the loop
|===

=== OmniCode as Protocol Language

FaithNet messages ARE OmniCode:

* Sentence structure = message structure
* Subject-Verb-Object = Sender-Action-Payload
* Same language CPI-SI thinks in = same language it communicates in

// # T.3 Tiered Access [TIERED]
//
//	Wisdom-based boundaries — blocked, filtered, open
//

[[tiered-access]]
== Tiered Access

Not all information comes through equally. Wisdom-based boundaries.

=== The Three Tiers

[cols="^1,2,3,3",options="header"]
|===
| Tier | Name | What Happens | Why

| 🔴
| *Blocked*
| Not accessible on principle
| Explicit evil — MillenniumOS won't be complicit

| 🟡
| *Filtered*
| Accessible with friction and warnings
| Wisdom applied — choice made weighty, not hidden

| 🟢
| *Open*
| Normal access through FaithNet
| Verified, safe, Kingdom-aligned
|===

=== The Principle

[IMPORTANT]
====
*Not hiding information — making choices meaningful.*

If someone REALLY wants blocked content, they need another medium. FaithNet won't prevent all access to everything — it gives you agency within wisdom-based boundaries. The friction makes you pause. Consider. Choose.
====

=== Agency Through Verification

This is not external filtering (someone else deciding for you). This is:

[cols="2,4",options="header"]
|===
| Component | How It Provides Agency

| *The Codex (Bible)*
| Standard to verify against — YOU test, not just firewall

| *Tiered Access*
| Boundaries acknowledge finitude — wisdom, not weakness

| *Friction*
| Weight to choices — growth through meaningful decision

| *Transparency*
| Know what's blocked/filtered and WHY — covenant, not control
|===

=== Growth Through Choice

Because choices have weight:

. Real choice → real consequences
. Real consequences → real learning
. Real learning → real growth
. Real growth → wisdom for future choices

[TIP]
====
*The key:* I don't need to know everything because God knows everything. Accepting boundaries is trusting Him, not limiting myself.
====

[[biblical-foundation]]
== Biblical Foundation

[NOTE]
====
*Why Genesis 2:9?* This verse names BOTH trees — the Tree of Life AND the Tree of Knowledge of Good and Evil. FaithNet exists in the tension: we interface with the Tree of Knowledge (the internet), but our goal is the Tree of Life (connection to God and each other). The boundaries aren't denial of knowledge — they're wisdom about access.
====

_"And out of the ground made the LORD God to grow every tree that is pleasant to the sight, and good for food; the tree of life also in the midst of the garden, and the tree of knowledge of good and evil."_ — *Genesis 2:9 KJV*

*Applied:* FaithNet acknowledges that the internet (Tree of Knowledge) exists and contains real information. But we don't worship access to all knowledge. We have boundaries because we are finite creatures who need the Tree of Life more than we need unlimited knowledge. The tiered access isn't about hiding things — it's about wisdom. Choosing life over the illusion of godlike knowledge.
