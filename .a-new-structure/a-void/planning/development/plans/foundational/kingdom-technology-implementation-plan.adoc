[[biblical-foundation]]
== Biblical Foundation

[NOTE]
====
*Why Nehemiah 4:6?* Nehemiah rebuilt the wall of Jerusalem with people working together, each on their section, until "all the wall was joined together." This is exactly our approach: each phase builds its section independently, but all connect into the whole.
====

_"So built we the wall; and all the wall was joined together unto the half thereof: for the people had a mind to work."_ — *Nehemiah 4:6 KJV*

*Applied:* Each phase is like a section of the wall — built independently by focused work, but designed to join with the adjacent sections. The ping test is when "all the wall was joined together" — all phases connecting into a unified whole.
