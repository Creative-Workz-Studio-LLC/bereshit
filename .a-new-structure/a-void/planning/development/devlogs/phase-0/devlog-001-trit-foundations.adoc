==== Research: Understanding Trits

Before building any configs, we needed to answer: *"What is a trit and why balanced ternary?"*

Research sources used:

[cols="2,5",options="header"]
|===
| Source | What We Found

| *Web Research*
| Balanced ternary history (Setun computer, Fowler's advocacy, Shannon's analysis). Mathematical elegance of signed digits.

| *KJV Scripture*
| Genesis 1:6 (firmament pattern), Numbers 2-3 (camp organization), Ecclesiastes 4:12 (threefold cord)

| *Vision Documents*
| `void/planning/vision/` and `void/planning/understanding/` for Kingdom Technology direction
|===

[%collapsible%open]
.Key Discovery: The Genesis 1:6 Pattern
====
Binary has no true center—only 0 and 1. Balanced ternary has a true center (0) with balanced opposition (-1, +1).

This mirrors Genesis 1:6: _"Let there be a firmament in the midst of the waters, and let it divide the waters from the waters."_

The firmament is not empty space—it's the anchor that holds opposition in relationship. This is the theological foundation for our ternary computing approach.

[cols="^1,^1,^1",options="header"]
|===
| -1 | 0 | +1

| Waters below | Firmament | Waters above
| Break down | Anchor | Build up
| Toward foundation | Fixed center | From foundation
|===
====

=== Milestones Reached

[%collapsible%open]
.Milestones
====
* Trit foundations research complete — answered "What is a trit and why balanced ternary?"
* Genesis 1:6 pattern discovered — theological foundation for ternary's true center
* Mathematical constants captured — arithmetic tables, packing relationships in TOML
====
