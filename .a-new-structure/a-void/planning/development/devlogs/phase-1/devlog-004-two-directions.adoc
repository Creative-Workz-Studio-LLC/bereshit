=== Work Completed

==== Correction: Genesis 1:1 Order

While updating the Phase 1 implementation plan, we noticed imprecise language. The Biblical Foundation section claimed implementation "mirrors" Genesis 1:1 order. This was wrong.

[%collapsible%open]
.The Problem: Inversion, Not Mirror
====
Genesis 1:1 reveals: *TIME → SPACE → MATTER*

* "In the beginning" = TIME (sequence, causality)
* "the heavens" = SPACE (position, relationships)
* "the earth" = MATTER (substance, identity)

But we implement: *MATTER → SPACE → TIME*

* Start with concrete (matter) — what we can touch and measure
* Understand relationships (space) — how things connect
* Grasp meaning (time) — purpose, sequence, causality

These are *inverse*, not mirrors. The order is completely reversed.
====

==== Discovery: Isaiah 55:9 Connection

The inversion led to Isaiah 55:9:

_"For as the heavens are higher than the earth, so are my ways higher than your ways, and my thoughts than your thoughts."_

God's ways are higher because He starts from the foundation of existence itself. We start from where we are—concrete reality—and build toward understanding.

==== Discovery: Bible as Meeting Point

If God creates top-down (TIME→SPACE→MATTER) and we implement bottom-up (MATTER→SPACE→TIME), where do these directions meet?

*The Bible is the rail where revelation and understanding converge:*

* God reveals truth in words we can read (reaching down into matter)
* We study Scripture to understand eternal truth (reaching up toward time's origin)
* The rail holds both directions together
