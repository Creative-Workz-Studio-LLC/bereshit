== Document Purpose

This document details the *services CWS provides to creatives*—artists, developers, writers, makers, and anyone bringing creative work into the world. These services support the publishing house model while preserving creator ownership and autonomy.

[IMPORTANT]
====
*Remember:* You keep your IP. All services are provided in exchange for revenue share, not ownership. See <<../02-publishing-house-model/02-02-EXTERNAL-CREATIVES.adoc#,External Creatives>> for the foundational principles.
====

== The Creative Partnership Promise

When you partner with CWS as a creative:

=== Your IP Stays Yours

[IMPORTANT]
====
*Non-negotiable:* We never take ownership of your intellectual property. You created it; you own it. This is fundamental.
====

=== You Choose Support Level

[cols="1,2",options="header"]
|===
|Support Level |You Get

|*Full Support*
|Maximum services, higher CWS revenue share

|*Moderate Support*
|Balanced services, balanced split

|*Light Support*
|Advisory services, you keep more

|*Infrastructure Only*
|Tools and access, minimal CWS share
|===

=== You Maintain Creative Control

* We advise, you decide
* Your vision drives the work
* We support your direction
* Final decisions are yours

=== You're Not Locked In

* Leave with your IP intact
* No punitive exit terms
* Relationship is voluntary
* Autonomy always preserved

=== You Join a Network

* Community, not just transaction
* Connections with other creators
* Access to faith-based businesses
* Part of Kingdom building
