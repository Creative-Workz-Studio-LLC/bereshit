[.lead]
This document details the *services {company-short} provides to creatives*—artists, developers, writers, makers, and anyone bringing creative work into the world. These services support the publishing house model while preserving creator ownership and autonomy.

[sidebar]
.icon:star[] Why This Document Matters
****
Creatives are the heart of {company-short}'s mission. Every artist, developer, writer, and maker carries a unique gift from God. Our services exist to *amplify these gifts* without taking ownership or creating dependency. This document is your complete guide to what {company-short} offers—so you can make an informed decision about partnership.
****

[IMPORTANT]
.icon:key[] Your IP Stays Yours
====
*Non-Negotiable:* We never take ownership of your intellectual property. All services are provided in exchange for revenue share, not ownership. See <<../02-publishing-house-model/02-02-EXTERNAL-CREATIVES.adoc#,External Creatives>> for the foundational principles.
====
