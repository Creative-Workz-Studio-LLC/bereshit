== icon:code[] Accessible Technology

=== icon:book-open[] Philosophy

Technology should be understandable by regular people, not just experts.

[quote, Habakkuk 2:2]
____
Write the vision, and make it plain upon tables, that he may run that readeth it.
____

The church shouldn't need computer science degrees to use or understand Kingdom technology. What we build must be *plain*—clear enough that those who read it can act.
