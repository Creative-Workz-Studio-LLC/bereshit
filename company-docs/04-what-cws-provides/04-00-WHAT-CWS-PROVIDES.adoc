== Chapter Purpose

This chapter documents *what services CWS provides*—the ways we serve our three primary audiences beyond the products we build. While <<../03-what-cws-builds/03-00-WHAT-CWS-BUILDS.adoc#,Chapter 03>> established what we build, this chapter explains how we serve through services, support, and resources.

[NOTE]
====
*Products vs. Services:* Chapter 03 covers what we *build* (products, infrastructure). This chapter covers what we *provide* (services, support, resources). Both serve the Kingdom; they're complementary.
====

== Service Philosophy Summary

Before diving into specific services, understand our foundational approach:

=== We Serve, Not Extract

[IMPORTANT]
====
CWS exists to serve the Kingdom, not to build an empire. Every service aims to strengthen recipients, not create dependency on CWS.
====

=== Five Core Principles

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Principle |What It Means

|*Teach, Don't Just Deliver*
|Every engagement transfers knowledge; clients understand more afterward

|*Build Autonomy, Not Dependency*
|Success is clients who can function without us

|*Values Before Revenue*
|We decline work that doesn't align, even if profitable

|*Community Over Transaction*
|Engagements build relationships, not just deliverables

|*Preparation Orientation*
|Everything makes recipients more prepared for what's coming
|===
