== Creative Engagement Process

=== Phase 1: Initial Conversation

==== Purpose

Understanding your work and goals; introducing CWS model.

==== What Happens

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Activity |Description

|*Your Introduction*
|Share about your creative work and goals

|*CWS Introduction*
|Learn about the publishing house model

|*Q&A*
|Questions in both directions

|*Fit Assessment*
|Initial sense of whether partnership makes sense
|===

==== What We Discuss

* What you create and why
* Who your work serves
* Where you are in your journey
* What support you might need
* How CWS works

==== Outcomes

* Mutual understanding of fit potential
* Decision to continue exploring or part graciously
* Next steps if continuing

=== Phase 2: Values Alignment

==== Purpose

Ensuring genuine Kingdom alignment.

==== What Happens

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Activity |Description

|*Values Discussion*
|Deeper conversation about values and mission

|*Work Review*
|Looking at your work in light of values

|*Alignment Assessment*
|Evaluating fit with CWS values

|*Honest Conversation*
|Addressing any concerns or questions
|===

==== What We Evaluate

* Kingdom orientation of your work
* Consistency with <<../07-core-values/07-00-CORE-VALUES.adoc#,CWS Core Values>>
* Relationship potential
* Long-term alignment

==== Outcomes

* Confirmed alignment (proceed) or
* Identified concerns (address or part) or
* Determined not a fit (part graciously)

=== Phase 3: Support Level Selection

==== Purpose

Choosing the right level of CWS involvement.

==== What Happens

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Activity |Description

|*Needs Assessment*
|Understanding what support you actually need

|*Options Review*
|Explaining support level options

|*Your Decision*
|You choose what's right for you

|*Terms Discussion*
|Discussing corresponding revenue terms
|===

==== Support Levels

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
|Level |What You Get |Revenue Implication

|*Full Support*
|Maximum services
|Higher CWS share

|*Moderate Support*
|Balanced services
|Balanced split

|*Light Support*
|Advisory focus
|Lower CWS share

|*Infrastructure Only*
|Tools and access
|Minimal CWS share
|===

==== Key Principle

[IMPORTANT]
====
*You Choose:* CWS doesn't push for higher support levels. You select based on your needs. We succeed when you succeed at any level.
====

=== Phase 4: Agreement

==== Purpose

Formalizing the partnership.

==== What Happens

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Activity |Description

|*Terms Finalization*
|Confirming specific terms

|*Agreement Preparation*
|Creating partnership document

|*Review Period*
|Time to review and ask questions

|*Signing*
|Formal commitment by both parties
|===

==== Agreement Elements

* Scope of works covered
* Services CWS will provide
* Revenue terms
* Duration and renewal
* IP clarity (yours stays yours)
* Exit provisions

=== Phase 5: Onboarding

==== Purpose

Getting you set up for success.

==== What Happens

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Activity |Description

|*Welcome*
|Introduction to CWS team

|*Systems Setup*
|Access to tools and platforms

|*Planning*
|Developing initial work plan

|*Network Introduction*
|Meeting relevant community members

|*Launch Prep*
|Preparing for first activities
|===

==== Onboarding Deliverables

* Access to relevant CWS systems
* Initial work plan
* Key contact relationships
* Understanding of processes
* Clear next steps

=== Phase 6: Ongoing Support

==== Purpose

Continuous relationship, not one-time transaction.

==== What Happens

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Activity |Description

|*Regular Check-ins*
|Scheduled touchpoints

|*Responsive Support*
|Help when you need it

|*Performance Review*
|Understanding what's working

|*Relationship Development*
|Deepening partnership over time

|*Evolution*
|Adapting as needs change
|===

==== Ongoing Relationship

* Support continues as long as partnership exists
* Relationship adapts to your changing needs
* You're part of community, not just client list
* Success is mutual and ongoing

'''

== Business Engagement Process

=== Phase 1: Assessment

==== Purpose

Understanding your current state and needs.

==== What Happens

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Activity |Description

|*Initial Discussion*
|Learning about your organization and needs

|*Current State Review*
|Understanding existing technology and processes

|*Needs Identification*
|Clarifying what you're trying to accomplish

|*Fit Assessment*
|Evaluating whether CWS can help
|===

==== Assessment Areas

* Current technology infrastructure
* Pain points and challenges
* Goals and objectives
* Timeline and constraints
* Budget considerations

==== Outcomes

* Clear understanding of your situation
* Identified opportunities for CWS help
* Decision to continue or not

=== Phase 2: Values Alignment

==== Purpose

Confirming genuine Kingdom alignment.

==== What Happens

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Activity |Description

|*Values Discussion*
|Understanding your organization's values

|*Mission Alignment*
|Comparing missions and purposes

|*Honest Assessment*
|Evaluating genuine fit

|*Concern Resolution*
|Addressing any alignment questions
|===

==== What We Evaluate

* Kingdom orientation
* Values consistency
* Mission compatibility
* Relationship potential

=== Phase 3: Planning

==== Purpose

Designing the engagement approach.

==== What Happens

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Activity |Description

|*Requirements Clarification*
|Detailed understanding of needs

|*Approach Design*
|Planning how to meet those needs

|*Timeline Development*
|Realistic scheduling

|*Resource Planning*
|Identifying what's needed

|*Proposal*
|Formal engagement proposal
|===

==== Plan Elements

* Specific objectives
* Approach and methodology
* Timeline and milestones
* Resources required
* Investment needed
* Success criteria

=== Phase 4: Implementation

==== Purpose

Executing the plan with knowledge transfer.

==== What Happens

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Activity |Description

|*Kickoff*
|Launching the engagement

|*Execution*
|Doing the work

|*Training*
|Teaching your team throughout

|*Documentation*
|Creating lasting knowledge resources

|*Milestone Reviews*
|Checking progress and adjusting
|===

==== Implementation Principles

* Knowledge transfer throughout
* Your team involved, not just observing
* Documentation as we go
* Regular communication
* Flexibility to adjust

=== Phase 5: Network Integration

==== Purpose

Connecting you to the broader community.

==== What Happens

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Activity |Description

|*Network Orientation*
|Understanding the trade network

|*Introductions*
|Meeting relevant network members

|*Integration*
|Becoming active participant

|*Relationship Building*
|Developing network relationships
|===

==== Network Benefits

* Connections with other Kingdom businesses
* Barter and trade opportunities
* Mutual support relationships
* Community beyond vendor relationship

=== Phase 6: Ongoing Relationship

==== Purpose

Continued partnership beyond initial engagement.

==== What Happens

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Activity |Description

|*Support*
|Ongoing assistance as needed

|*Relationship*
|Continued connection

|*Collaboration*
|Working together on new needs

|*Community*
|Active network participation
|===

==== Relationship Character

* Not transactional—genuine partnership
* Mutual support, not just service delivery
* Evolves with your needs
* Part of Kingdom community

'''

== Common Elements

=== Values Alignment (Both Paths)

[IMPORTANT]
====
Values alignment is required for both creative and business engagements. This isn't a checkbox—it's fundamental to how we work.
====

=== Knowledge Transfer (Both Paths)

Every engagement includes knowledge transfer:

* Training is part of the work
* Documentation accompanies deliverables
* Your capability increases
* You understand what was done and why

=== Network Integration (Both Paths)

Both creatives and businesses join the community:

* Connections with others
* Mutual support relationships
* Part of something bigger
* Ongoing community, not just transaction

=== Ongoing Relationship (Both Paths)

Engagements don't end at project completion:

* Continued relationship
* Evolving partnership
* Community membership
* Mutual support

'''
