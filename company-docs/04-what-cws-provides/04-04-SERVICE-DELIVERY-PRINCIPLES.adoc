== Document Purpose

This document establishes the *principles governing how CWS delivers all services*. These principles ensure every service engagement reflects Kingdom values and serves recipients in ways that honor God.

[NOTE]
====
*Principles Shape Practice:* How we serve matters as much as what we serve. These principles guide every engagement, ensuring consistency and Kingdom alignment.
====

'''

== Principle 1: Teach, Don't Just Deliver

=== The Principle

[IMPORTANT]
====
Every service engagement should transfer knowledge. Clients should understand more after working with us, not less.
====

=== What This Means

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Aspect |Application

|*Knowledge Transfer*
|Clients learn, not just receive deliverables

|*Explanation*
|We explain what we're doing and why

|*Documentation*
|Written knowledge accompanies work

|*Training*
|Teaching is part of every engagement

|*Questions Welcomed*
|We encourage understanding, not blind trust
|===

=== In Practice

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
|Situation |Just Delivering |Teaching

|*Technology Deployment*
|Install and leave
|Install, train team, document

|*Marketing Campaign*
|Run campaign
|Run campaign, explain strategy, teach approach

|*Consultation*
|Give recommendations
|Give recommendations with reasoning, teach frameworks

|*Problem Solving*
|Fix the problem
|Fix problem, explain cause, teach prevention
|===

=== Why This Matters

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Reason |Explanation

|*Empowerment*
|Knowledgeable clients are stronger

|*Independence*
|Understanding enables independence

|*Multiplication*
|Clients can teach others

|*Kingdom Building*
|Equipped saints serve better

|*Sustainability*
|Knowledge survives personnel changes
|===

'''

== Principle 2: Build Autonomy, Not Dependency

=== The Principle

[IMPORTANT]
====
Our goal is clients who can function without us, not clients locked into our services. Success is independence.
====

=== What This Means

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Aspect |Application

|*Independence Goal*
|We work toward client self-sufficiency

|*No Lock-in*
|No artificial dependencies on CWS

|*Capability Building*
|Increase client capability, not just deliver services

|*Exit Path*
|Clients can leave successfully at any time

|*Honest Assessment*
|Tell clients when they don't need us anymore
|===

=== In Practice

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
|Situation |Creating Dependency |Building Autonomy

|*Technology*
|Proprietary systems only CWS can maintain
|Open systems with knowledge transfer

|*Marketing*
|Only CWS has audience relationships
|Build client's own audience assets

|*Consultation*
|Ongoing reliance on CWS advice
|Teach decision-making frameworks

|*Infrastructure*
|CWS-controlled hosting required
|Self-hosting capability enabled
|===

=== Why This Matters

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Reason |Explanation

|*Kingdom Pattern*
|Building up the body, not building empire

|*Client Strength*
|Strong clients serve the Kingdom better

|*Resilience*
|Network of capable organizations, not hub-and-spoke

|*Trust*
|Demonstrates genuine service orientation

|*Mark Preparation*
|Clients must function independently if needed
|===

'''

== Principle 3: Values Before Revenue

=== The Principle

[IMPORTANT]
====
We turn down work that doesn't align with Kingdom values, even if profitable. Alignment matters more than money.
====

=== What This Means

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Aspect |Application

|*Alignment Required*
|Values alignment is prerequisite, not optional

|*Revenue Secondary*
|Profitability doesn't override values

|*Decline When Necessary*
|We say no to misaligned opportunities

|*Long-term Perspective*
|Kingdom outcomes over short-term revenue

|*Trust Protection*
|Reputation worth more than any single engagement
|===

=== In Practice

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
|Situation |Revenue First |Values First

|*Lucrative but Misaligned*
|Take the money
|Decline the engagement

|*Values Conflict Mid-Engagement*
|Continue for revenue
|Address conflict, exit if necessary

|*Pressure to Compromise*
|Adjust to client demands
|Maintain principles, explain why

|*Opportunity Cost*
|Take any paying work
|Reserve capacity for aligned work
|===

=== Alignment Areas

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Area |What We Evaluate

|*Mission Alignment*
|Does this serve Kingdom purposes?

|*Values Consistency*
|Does this align with CWS core values?

|*Content Alignment*
|Is the content consistent with Scripture?

|*Relationship Quality*
|Is this a healthy working relationship?

|*Long-term Impact*
|What are the Kingdom implications?
|===

'''

== Principle 4: Community Over Transaction

=== The Principle

[IMPORTANT]
====
Engagements build relationships, not just deliverables. Every client joins a network, not just a vendor relationship.
====

=== What This Means

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Aspect |Application

|*Relationship Focus*
|Building genuine relationships, not just completing transactions

|*Network Inclusion*
|Clients become part of community

|*Ongoing Connection*
|Relationship continues beyond project completion

|*Mutual Support*
|Reciprocal support, not one-way service

|*Community Benefit*
|Individual engagements strengthen whole community
|===

=== In Practice

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
|Situation |Transactional |Community

|*Project End*
|Invoice paid, relationship ends
|Project done, relationship continues

|*Client Need Beyond Scope*
|Not our problem
|Connect to resources, help where able

|*Community Introduction*
|Not offered
|Connect to relevant network members

|*Future Opportunities*
|Sell more services
|Share opportunities, support growth
|===

=== Community Elements

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Element |How It Works

|*Network Access*
|Connection to other Kingdom organizations

|*Mutual Introductions*
|Connecting clients with each other

|*Resource Sharing*
|Facilitating resource exchange

|*Collective Learning*
|Sharing insights across community

|*Crisis Support*
|Community rallying when members struggle
|===

'''

== Principle 5: Preparation Orientation

=== The Principle

[IMPORTANT]
====
Everything we provide should make recipients more prepared for what's coming, not more dependent on current systems.
====

=== What This Means

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Aspect |Application

|*Future Focus*
|Prepare for coming challenges, not just current needs

|*Independence Building*
|Reduce reliance on systems that may require marks

|*Resilience*
|Build capability to weather disruption

|*Alternative Infrastructure*
|Provide paths outside beast-system

|*Community Strength*
|Build networks for mutual support
|===

=== In Practice

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
|Situation |Status Quo |Preparation

|*Technology Choice*
|Best current option
|Best option that doesn't create beast-system dependency

|*Business Advice*
|Optimize current operations
|Build resilience and alternatives

|*Network Building*
|Commercial relationships
|Mutual support relationships

|*Infrastructure*
|Cloud services
|Self-hosting capability
|===

=== Preparation Areas

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Area |What We Prepare

|*Communication*
|Ability to connect when platforms restricted

|*Commerce*
|Ability to transact without beast-system

|*Community*
|Relationships for mutual support

|*Capability*
|Skills and resources to rebuild

|*Knowledge*
|Preserved information for future builders
|===

'''

== Applying the Principles

=== Decision Framework

When making service delivery decisions:

[cols="1,2",options="header"]
|===
|Question |Principle Check

|*Will they learn from this?*
|Teach, Don't Just Deliver

|*Will they be more independent after?*
|Build Autonomy, Not Dependency

|*Does this align with Kingdom values?*
|Values Before Revenue

|*Does this build relationship beyond project?*
|Community Over Transaction

|*Does this prepare them for challenges ahead?*
|Preparation Orientation
|===

=== Principle Priorities

When principles seem to conflict:

. *Values Before Revenue* — Always first; never compromise for money
. *Preparation Orientation* — Kingdom future over present convenience
. *Build Autonomy* — Their strength over our recurring revenue
. *Community Over Transaction* — Relationship over efficiency
. *Teach, Don't Just Deliver* — Even when teaching takes more time

=== Anti-Patterns

Watch for violations of principles:

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
|Anti-Pattern |Violation |Correction

|Deliver without explaining
|Teach, Don't Just Deliver
|Add training and documentation

|Create dependency for recurring revenue
|Build Autonomy
|Focus on client capability building

|Take misaligned work for profit
|Values Before Revenue
|Decline or address alignment

|Transact and move on
|Community Over Transaction
|Build ongoing relationship

|Optimize for current systems
|Preparation Orientation
|Build toward independence
|===
