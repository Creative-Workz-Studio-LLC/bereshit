[.lead]
This document establishes the *principles governing how {company-short} delivers all services*. These principles ensure every service engagement reflects Kingdom values and serves recipients in ways that honor God.

[sidebar]
.icon:star[] Why This Document Matters
****
*How* we serve matters as much as *what* we serve. A vendor can deliver products; Kingdom servants deliver transformation. These five principles distinguish {company-short} service from worldly business-as-usual. They ensure every engagement—whether with a creative, business, or the broader body—strengthens recipients and honors the Lord we serve.
****

[NOTE]
.icon:lightbulb[] Principles Shape Practice
====
How we serve matters as much as what we serve. These principles guide every engagement, ensuring consistency and Kingdom alignment across all service delivery.
====

'''

== icon:bullseye[] Document Purpose

This document articulates the five principles that govern all {company-short} service delivery, providing decision frameworks for applying these principles in practice and identifying anti-patterns that violate them.

== icon:chalkboard-teacher[] Principle 1: Teach, Don't Just Deliver

=== icon:bullseye[] The Principle

[IMPORTANT]
.icon:graduation-cap[] Knowledge Transfer
====
Every service engagement should transfer knowledge. Clients should understand *more* after working with us, not less. Delivering without teaching creates dependency; teaching alongside delivery creates capability.
====

=== icon:question-circle[] What This Means

[cols="^1,2,4",options="header",frame=all,grid=all]
|===
|icon:tag[] |Aspect |Application

|icon:exchange-alt[role=blue]
|*Knowledge Transfer*
|Clients learn, not just receive deliverables—capability grows

|icon:comment[role=green]
|*Explanation*
|We explain what we're doing and why—no mysterious black boxes

|icon:book[role=purple]
|*Documentation*
|Written knowledge accompanies work—reference for the future

|icon:user-graduate[role=orange]
|*Training*
|Teaching is part of every engagement—not an optional add-on

|icon:question-circle[role=teal]
|*Questions Welcomed*
|We encourage understanding, not blind trust—questions honor us
|===

=== icon:exchange-alt[] In Practice

[cols="^1,2,3,3",options="header",frame=all,grid=all]
|===
|icon:tag[] |Situation |Just Delivering |Teaching

|icon:server[role=blue]
|*Technology Deployment*
|Install and leave
|Install, train team, document—transfer ownership

|icon:bullhorn[role=green]
|*Marketing Campaign*
|Run campaign
|Run campaign, explain strategy, teach approach—empower

|icon:comments[role=purple]
|*Consultation*
|Give recommendations
|Give recommendations with reasoning, teach frameworks—equip

|icon:wrench[role=orange]
|*Problem Solving*
|Fix the problem
|Fix problem, explain cause, teach prevention—prevent recurrence
|===

=== icon:lightbulb[] Why This Matters

[cols="^1,2,4",options="header",frame=all,grid=all]
|===
|icon:tag[] |Reason |Explanation

|icon:fist-raised[role=blue]
|*Empowerment*
|Knowledgeable clients are stronger—Kingdom is strengthened

|icon:user-graduate[role=green]
|*Independence*
|Understanding enables independence—not dependent on us

|icon:users[role=purple]
|*Multiplication*
|Clients can teach others—knowledge spreads through the body

|icon:crown[role=gold]
|*Kingdom Building*
|Equipped saints serve better—collective capability grows

|icon:hourglass-half[role=orange]
|*Sustainability*
|Knowledge survives personnel changes—not fragile
|===

== icon:user-graduate[] Principle 2: Build Autonomy, Not Dependency

=== icon:bullseye[] The Principle

[IMPORTANT]
.icon:door-open[] Independence Goal
====
Our goal is clients who can function *without* us, not clients locked into our services. Success is independence. We measure ourselves by client strength, not client need.
====

=== icon:question-circle[] What This Means

[cols="^1,2,4",options="header",frame=all,grid=all]
|===
|icon:tag[] |Aspect |Application

|icon:flag-checkered[role=blue]
|*Independence Goal*
|We work toward client self-sufficiency—that's winning

|icon:lock-open[role=green]
|*No Lock-in*
|No artificial dependencies on {company-short}—you're free

|icon:chart-line[role=purple]
|*Capability Building*
|Increase client capability, not just deliver services—growth

|icon:door-open[role=orange]
|*Exit Path*
|Clients can leave successfully at any time—no traps

|icon:comment[role=teal]
|*Honest Assessment*
|Tell clients when they don't need us anymore—serve, don't sell
|===

=== icon:exchange-alt[] In Practice

[cols="^1,2,3,3",options="header",frame=all,grid=all]
|===
|icon:tag[] |Situation |Creating Dependency |Building Autonomy

|icon:server[role=blue]
|*Technology*
|Proprietary systems only {company-short} can maintain
|Open systems with knowledge transfer—you own it

|icon:bullhorn[role=green]
|*Marketing*
|Only {company-short} has audience relationships
|Build client's own audience assets—they're yours

|icon:comments[role=purple]
|*Consultation*
|Ongoing reliance on {company-short} advice
|Teach decision-making frameworks—you can decide

|icon:cloud[role=orange]
|*Infrastructure*
|{company-short}-controlled hosting required
|Self-hosting capability enabled—independence possible
|===

=== icon:lightbulb[] Why This Matters

[cols="^1,2,4",options="header",frame=all,grid=all]
|===
|icon:tag[] |Reason |Explanation

|icon:crown[role=gold]
|*Kingdom Pattern*
|Building up the body, not building empire—Scripture's model

|icon:fist-raised[role=blue]
|*Client Strength*
|Strong clients serve the Kingdom better—multiplication

|icon:network-wired[role=green]
|*Resilience*
|Network of capable organizations, not hub-and-spoke—survives

|icon:handshake[role=purple]
|*Trust*
|Demonstrates genuine service orientation—reputation built

|icon:shield-alt[role=red]
|*Mark Preparation*
|Clients must function independently if needed—survival
|===

== icon:balance-scale[] Principle 3: Values Before Revenue

=== icon:bullseye[] The Principle

[IMPORTANT]
.icon:crown[] Kingdom Priority
====
We turn down work that doesn't align with Kingdom values, *even if profitable*. Alignment matters more than money. Revenue serves mission; mission doesn't serve revenue.
====

=== icon:question-circle[] What This Means

[cols="^1,2,4",options="header",frame=all,grid=all]
|===
|icon:tag[] |Aspect |Application

|icon:check-circle[role=blue]
|*Alignment Required*
|Values alignment is prerequisite, not optional—non-negotiable

|icon:dollar-sign[role=green]
|*Revenue Secondary*
|Profitability doesn't override values—clear hierarchy

|icon:times-circle[role=purple]
|*Decline When Necessary*
|We say no to misaligned opportunities—courage required

|icon:clock[role=orange]
|*Long-term Perspective*
|Kingdom outcomes over short-term revenue—eternal view

|icon:shield-alt[role=teal]
|*Trust Protection*
|Reputation worth more than any single engagement—stewardship
|===

=== icon:exchange-alt[] In Practice

[cols="^1,2,3,3",options="header",frame=all,grid=all]
|===
|icon:tag[] |Situation |Revenue First |Values First

|icon:dollar-sign[role=green]
|*Lucrative but Misaligned*
|Take the money
|Decline the engagement—integrity preserved

|icon:exclamation-triangle[role=orange]
|*Values Conflict Mid-Engagement*
|Continue for revenue
|Address conflict, exit if necessary—values maintained

|icon:compress-arrows-alt[role=purple]
|*Pressure to Compromise*
|Adjust to client demands
|Maintain principles, explain why—stand firm

|icon:search[role=blue]
|*Opportunity Cost*
|Take any paying work
|Reserve capacity for aligned work—strategic
|===

|*Content Alignment*
|Is the content consistent with Scripture?

|Build Autonomy, Not Dependency

|*Does this align with Kingdom values?*
|Values Before Revenue

|icon:heart[role=purple]
|*Does this build relationship beyond project?*
|Community Over Transaction

|icon:shield-alt[role=red]
|*Does this prepare them for challenges ahead?*
|Preparation Orientation
|===

=== icon:sort-amount-up[] Principle Priorities

When principles seem to conflict, apply in this order:

[cols="^1,2,4",options="header",frame=all,grid=all]
|===
|icon:tag[] |Priority |Reasoning

|icon:balance-scale[role=gold]
|*1. Values Before Revenue*

|icon:shield-alt[role=red]
|*2. Preparation Orientation*
|Kingdom future over present convenience

|icon:user-graduate[role=blue]
|*3. Build Autonomy*
|Their strength over our recurring revenue

|icon:users[role=green]
|*4. Community Over Transaction*
|Relationship over efficiency

|icon:chalkboard-teacher[role=purple]
