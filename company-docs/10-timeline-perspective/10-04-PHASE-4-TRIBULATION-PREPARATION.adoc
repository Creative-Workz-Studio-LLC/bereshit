== icon:exclamation-triangle[] Phase 4 Risks

=== icon:eye-slash[] Denial

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Ignoring What's Coming
****
*What It Is:*

Refusing to acknowledge the reality of what Scripture clearly describes — pretending tribulation won't happen or won't affect us.

*Why It's Dangerous:*

Denial prevents preparation. Those who refuse to prepare will have nothing when restriction comes. Scripture is clear; we have no excuse for surprise.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] "It won't happen in our lifetime"
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Spiritualizing away literal prophecy
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Assuming someone else will prepare
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Dismissing preparation as paranoia
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Normalcy bias overriding Scripture
****

=== icon:ghost[] Fear-Driven Preparation

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Anxious Hoarding
****
*What It Is:*

Preparing from fear rather than faithfulness — hoarding, isolating, or trusting in preparations rather than the Lord.

*Why It's Dangerous:*

Fear-driven preparation is self-focused, not Kingdom-focused. It hoards rather than shares, isolates rather than connects. It trusts in barns rather than in God (Luke 12:16-21).

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Anxiety dominating preparation
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Accumulating for self, not serving others
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Preparation as substitute for faith
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Isolating rather than building community
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Fear of the future rather than trust in God
****

=== icon:handshake-alt-slash[] Compromise Under Pressure

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Taking the Mark
****
*What It Is:*

Ultimately, the risk is that under pressure, believers compromise — taking the mark to survive economically rather than trusting God.

*Why It's Dangerous:*

Scripture is absolutely clear about the consequences (Revelation 14:9-11). This is why preparation matters — alternatives make faithfulness possible when pressure comes.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Rationalizing small compromises now
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Valuing economic comfort over faithfulness
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Not taking preparation seriously
* icon:times-circle[role=red] "I'll deal with it when it comes"
* icon:times-circle[role=red] No practical alternatives built
****

'''
