== icon:exclamation-triangle[] Transition Risks

=== icon:coins[] Premature Transition

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Jumping Too Soon
****
*What It Is:*

Transitioning to full-time {company-short} before revenue can sustain it — letting enthusiasm outpace wisdom.

*Why It's Dangerous:*

Premature transition endangers family, creates desperation pressure on {company-short} decisions, and may force retreat to employment at a worse position. It's better to transition six months "late" than six months early.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] "Close enough" reasoning on revenue
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Impatience driving timeline
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Ignoring warning signs
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Comparing to others who transitioned
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Rationalizing gaps in sustainability
****

=== icon:hourglass-half[] Delayed Transition

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Waiting Too Long
****
*What It Is:*

Failing to transition when conditions are ready — letting fear or comfort keep you from stepping out in faith.

*Why It's Dangerous:*

If the Lord has prepared the way and we don't walk through it, we miss the opportunity. Comfort at Guardian Angel Settlement Association could become a trap preventing obedience.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Moving goalposts on "readiness"
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Fear disguised as wisdom
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Comfort at current job exceeding calling to mission
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Always finding "one more thing" before transition
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Ignoring counsel that says "it's time"
****

=== icon:heart-broken[] Transition That Harms

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Collateral Damage
****
*What It Is:*

Transitioning in ways that harm relationships — burning bridges at Guardian Angel Settlement Association, straining family, or breaking commitments.

*Why It's Dangerous:*

How we transition matters as much as when. Transition that harms others violates values and damages witness. Kingdom transitions leave people better, not worse.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Resentment toward current employer
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Family feeling neglected or anxious
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Leaving commitments unfulfilled
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Burning bridges "because we're leaving anyway"
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Transition consuming everything else
****

=== icon:praying-hands[] Transition Wisdom

[IMPORTANT]
====
*Transition requires wisdom, not just math.* Numbers may say "go" while the Lord says "wait." We seek confirmation through prayer, counsel, and circumstances — not just spreadsheets.
====

== icon:question-circle[] The Transition Decision Test

=== icon:clipboard-check[] Decision Framework

When evaluating transition readiness, ask:

[cols="^1,4,2",options="header",frame=all,grid=all]
|===
|icon:tag[] |Question |Good Answer

|icon:coins[role=blue]
|*Is revenue proven, not projected?*
|Multiple months of demonstrated sustainability

|icon:sync[role=green]
|*Are revenue streams diversified?*
|Not dependent on single client or product

|icon:home[role=purple]
|*Is family protected?*
|Basic needs covered; family at peace with decision

|icon:praying-hands[role=orange]
|*Do we have confirmation beyond numbers?*
|Prayer, counsel, circumstances align

|icon:handshake[role=gold]
|*Can we transition well?*
|Leave Guardian Angel Settlement Association with integrity and good relationships

|===

=== icon:balance-scale[] How to Apply This Test

[sidebar]
.icon:lightbulb[] Practical Application
****
*The Three Witnesses:*

1. icon:chart-line[role=blue] *Numbers* — Revenue genuinely sustains; not wishful math
2. icon:praying-hands[role=green] *Peace* — Confirmation in prayer; settled spirit
3. icon:users[role=purple] *Counsel* — Wise advisors affirm; board supports

*All three should align.* If numbers say go but peace is absent, wait. If peace says go but numbers aren't there, pray for provision. If counsel says wait despite everything, listen carefully.
****
