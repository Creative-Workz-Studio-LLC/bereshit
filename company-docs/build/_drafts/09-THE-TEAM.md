## Network Structure

Beyond the core team, CWS operates within a network:

### Creative Partners
Creatives publishing through CWS with revenue split arrangements. They're not employees but partners in the publishing house.

### Faith-Based Business Partners
Organizations using CWS infrastructure or participating in the trade network. Connected but independent.

### Trade Network Members
Businesses participating in mark-free commerce facilitation. Mutual support without hierarchy.

### Community Connections
Nonprofits, churches, and organizations we serve without commercial relationship.

## Team Culture

### How We Work

| Principle | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| Async-friendly | Accommodating different schedules and locations |
| Documentation-heavy | Writing things down so others can follow |
| Trust-based | Extending trust, not requiring constant proof |
| Mission-aligned | Every task connects to purpose |
| Learning-oriented | Always teaching and being taught |

### What We Value in Team Members

| Quality | Why It Matters |
|---------|----------------|
| Kingdom alignment | Values must match for true partnership |
| Self-direction | We don't micromanage |
| Excellence | Building as unto the Lord |
| Humility | None of us is the center |
| Persistence | This is long-term work |

### What We Don't Want

| Quality | Why We Avoid It |
|---------|-----------------|
| Empire building | We're connective tissue, not center |
| Extraction mindset | We serve, not extract |
| Short-term thinking | We're building for the Millennium |
| Values compromise | Alignment isn't negotiable |

## Team Development

### Onboarding Future Members

1. **Values alignment check** — Do they share Kingdom values?
2. **Mission understanding** — Do they grasp the full scope?
3. **Documentation immersion** — Read the foundational docs
4. **Relationship building** — Get to know the existing team
5. **Contribution start** — Begin with appropriate scope
6. **Growth path** — Expand responsibility as trust builds

### Developing Current Members

| Focus | Method |
|-------|--------|
| Skills | Learning through doing, resources as needed |
| Understanding | Deepening grasp of vision and architecture |
| Relationships | Building trust across the network |
| Capacity | Expanding what we can handle together |
