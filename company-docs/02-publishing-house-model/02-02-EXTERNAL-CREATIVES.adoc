== Document Purpose

This document establishes how Creative Workz Studio works with *external creatives*—individuals and teams who partner with CWS to publish their work. This is *Pillar One* of the publishing house model and often the most visible aspect of CWS operations.

[IMPORTANT]
====
*The Core Principle:* Creators keep their intellectual property. This is non-negotiable and fundamental to everything in this document.
====

'''

== The Non-Negotiable: IP Retention

=== The Principle

[.lead]
*CWS does not take intellectual property rights from creators.*

When a creative partners with CWS to publish their work:

* The creator *retains full ownership* of their intellectual property
* CWS provides services in exchange for a *revenue share*
* The creator maintains *autonomy* over their creation
* The creator can *leave the partnership* with their IP intact

=== Why This Is Non-Negotiable

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Reason |Explanation

|*Kingdom Alignment*
|Creators received gifts from God; extracting ownership violates stewardship principles

|*Creator Empowerment*
|Creators can build their own legacy, not just contribute to CWS's portfolio

|*Trust Foundation*
|IP retention establishes trust that enables genuine partnership

|*Differentiation*
|This distinguishes CWS from extractive publishers

|*Long-term Relationships*
|Creators stay because they want to, not because they're locked in
|===

=== What IP Retention Means

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
|Aspect |What It Means |What It Does NOT Mean

|*Ownership*
|Creator owns all rights to their work
|CWS gains no ownership stake

|*Control*
|Creator decides how work is used
|CWS cannot use work without permission

|*Modifications*
|Creator controls changes to work
|CWS cannot alter work unilaterally

|*Exit*
|Creator can end partnership with IP intact
|CWS cannot hold IP hostage

|*Future Works*
|No automatic claims on future creations
|Each work is separately considered
|===

== What CWS Does NOT Do

=== Non-Practices

Equally important is what CWS *refuses* to do:

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|We Do NOT |Because

|*Take IP ownership*
|This violates our core principle

|*Require exclusivity* (by default)
|Creators should be free to work with others

|*Lock creators into long contracts*
|Relationships should be voluntary, not coerced

|*Dictate creative direction*
|Creators know their work best

|*Penalize working with others*
|We're partners, not gatekeepers

|*Publish anything for payment*
|We maintain quality and values standards

|*Make decisions for creators*
|We advise; creators decide
|===

=== Exclusivity Considerations

While CWS does not require exclusivity by default:

* *Mutual agreements* — If both parties agree to exclusivity for specific reasons, it can be arranged
* *Project-specific* — Some projects may warrant exclusive arrangements
* *Time-limited* — Any exclusivity should have clear time boundaries
* *Creator-initiated* — Creator should propose exclusivity, not CWS demand it

== Creator Autonomy

=== The Autonomy Principle

Creators maintain autonomy in their partnership with CWS:

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Autonomy Dimension |What It Means

|*Creative Direction*
|Creator decides what to create and how

|*Business Decisions*
|Creator makes their own business choices

|*Partnership Terms*
|Creator chooses support level and terms

|*Exit Freedom*
|Creator can leave partnership at will

|*Other Relationships*
|Creator can work with others simultaneously
|===

=== CWS Role in Autonomy

CWS supports creator autonomy through:

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Role |How We Support

|*Advisor*
|We provide guidance when asked, not mandates

|*Facilitator*
|We enable creator goals, not substitute our own

|*Resource Provider*
|We give creators tools; they decide how to use them

|*Connector*
|We make introductions; creators develop relationships

|*Supporter*
|We're available when needed, not controlling
|===

=== Boundaries of Autonomy

Creator autonomy operates within reasonable boundaries:

[cols="1,2",options="header"]
|===
|Boundary |Rationale

|*Quality Standards*
|CWS has standards for what we publish; creators choose whether to meet them

|*Values Alignment*
|Published work should align with Kingdom values

|*Legal Compliance*
|Work must comply with applicable laws

|*Agreement Terms*
|Both parties honor agreed commitments
|===

== Quality Standards

=== Why Standards Matter

CWS maintains quality standards because:

* *Audience Trust* — People trust CWS-published work meets certain standards
* *Creator Benefit* — Association with quality enhances creator reputation
* *Kingdom Witness* — Excellence honors God
* *Sustainability* — Quality work generates sustainable revenue

=== What We Look For

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Standard |Description

|*Craft Excellence*
|Work demonstrates skill and care in execution

|*Audience Value*
|Work genuinely serves its intended audience

|*Values Alignment*
|Work consistent with Kingdom values

|*Market Viability*
|Work has reasonable potential to reach audience

|*Creator Commitment*
|Creator is committed to seeing work succeed
|===

=== What We Don't Require

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Not Required |Why

|*Perfection*
|Excellence is the goal; perfection is not achievable

|*Mainstream Appeal*
|Niche work that serves its audience well is valuable

|*Proven Track Record*
|New creators deserve opportunity

|*Large Audience*
|Quality over quantity in audience reach

|*Commercial Focus*
|Ministry-focused work matters too
|===
