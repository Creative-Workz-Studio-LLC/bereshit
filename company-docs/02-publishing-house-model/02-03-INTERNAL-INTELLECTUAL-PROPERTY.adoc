== icon:compass[] Document Purpose

[.lead]
This document establishes {company-short}'s approach to *internal intellectual properties*—creative works and technology that {company-short} itself owns. This is *Pillar Two* of the publishing house model and represents {company-short}'s own creative output alongside supporting external creatives.

[sidebar]
.icon:question-circle[] Why This Document Matters
****
Understanding our internal IP portfolio is essential for:

* icon:server[] *Enabling Mission* — Technology IPs power our publishing infrastructure
* icon:star[] *Demonstrating Standards* — Internal work shows the quality we expect
* icon:coins[] *Sustaining Operations* — Owned IPs provide stable revenue foundation
* icon:crown[] *Kingdom Service* — Some needed works must come from within
****

[NOTE]
.icon:info-circle[] Context
====
While {company-short}'s primary focus is serving external creatives (Pillar One), internal IPs enable that service and represent {company-short}'s own creative contribution to the Kingdom.
====
