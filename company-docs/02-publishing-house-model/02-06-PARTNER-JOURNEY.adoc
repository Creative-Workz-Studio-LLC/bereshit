== Document Purpose

This document provides a practical guide for potential partners interested in working with Creative Workz Studio. It explains the journey from initial interest through active partnership and beyond.

[NOTE]
====
This document is intended for potential partners considering a publishing relationship with CWS. If you're reading this, welcome—we're glad you're exploring partnership.
====

== Before You Begin

=== Key Things to Know

Before exploring partnership, understand these fundamentals:

[IMPORTANT]
====
*Non-Negotiables:*

. *You keep your IP* — CWS never takes intellectual property rights from creators
. *Values alignment matters* — Partnership requires shared commitment to Kingdom values
. *Quality standards exist* — CWS maintains standards for what we publish
. *You choose your support level* — And corresponding revenue split
====

=== Is CWS Right for You?

CWS partnership may be a good fit if you:

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Characteristic |Why It Fits

|*Kingdom-oriented*
|Your work serves Kingdom purposes, not just profit

|*Values-aligned*
|You share the values described in <<../07-CORE-VALUES.adoc#,Core Values>>

|*Quality-committed*
|You're dedicated to excellent work

|*Relationship-oriented*
|You value partnership over pure transaction

|*Independence-minded*
|You want support while maintaining ownership and autonomy
|===

CWS partnership may NOT be the best fit if you:

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Characteristic |Why It May Not Fit

|*Just want distribution*
|Pure platforms may serve you better

|*Expect CWS to own your work*
|We don't take IP; if you want to sell ownership, look elsewhere

|*Values conflict*
|Content that contradicts Kingdom values won't be published

|*Want guaranteed success*
|Partnership is genuine—success isn't guaranteed

|*Prefer complete independence*
|If you don't want any partnership relationship, self-publishing may be better
|===

'''

=== Phase 1: Initial Contact

==== How to Reach CWS

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Method |Notes

|*Referral*
|Introduction from existing partner or network member

|*Direct Contact*
|Reach out through CWS contact channels

|*Event Connection*
|Meet at gatherings, conferences, or community events

|*Network Discovery*
|Find CWS through faith-based business networks
|===

==== What Happens in Initial Contact

. *Introduction* — You share who you are and what you're working on
. *CWS Response* — We share about CWS and the partnership model
. *Questions* — Both parties ask clarifying questions
. *Interest Assessment* — Determine if further exploration makes sense

==== What We Look For Initially

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Factor |What We're Assessing

|*Mission Fit*
|Does your work align with Kingdom purposes?

|*Values Alignment*
|Do you share CWS core values?

|*Work Quality*
|Is there evidence of quality in your work?

|*Relationship Potential*
|Is genuine partnership possible?

|*Mutual Benefit*
|Can CWS genuinely help you, and vice versa?
|===

=== Phase 2: Mutual Assessment

==== What Happens During Assessment

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Activity |Purpose

|*Work Review*
|CWS reviews samples of your work

|*Values Conversation*
|Deeper discussion of values and alignment

|*Needs Assessment*
|Understanding what support you need

|*Capability Assessment*
|Understanding what CWS can provide

|*Fit Evaluation*
|Both parties assess partnership fit
|===

==== Questions CWS May Ask

* What are you creating and why?
* Who is your intended audience?
* How does your work serve Kingdom purposes?
* What support do you need?
* What's your timeline and vision?
* How do you see partnership working?

==== Questions You Should Ask

* How does CWS support creators like me?
* What would the revenue split look like for my support needs?
* How involved would CWS be in my work?
* What happens if I want to change terms later?
* What happens if I want to leave the partnership?
* Can I see examples of other partnerships?

==== Assessment Outcomes

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
|Outcome |What It Means |Next Step

|*Proceed*
|Good fit identified; move to agreement phase
|Begin terms discussion

|*Not Now*
|Timing or readiness isn't right
|Stay connected; revisit later

|*Not Fit*
|Values, quality, or purpose don't align
|Part graciously; may connect to others

|*Redirect*
|Better served elsewhere
|Refer to more appropriate partners
|===

=== Phase 3: Agreement Phase

==== Terms Discussion

If assessment indicates good fit, we discuss terms:

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Topic |What's Discussed

|*Support Level*
|What support you need (Full/Moderate/Light/Infrastructure)

|*Revenue Split*
|Corresponding split based on support level

|*Scope*
|What works are covered by agreement

|*Duration*
|Timeline for partnership

|*Specific Services*
|Detailed services CWS will provide

|*Expectations*
|What both parties commit to
|===

==== Partnership Agreement

Formal partnership includes:

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Element |Content

|*Parties*
|Who is entering the agreement

|*Scope*
|What works/projects are covered

|*Services*
|What CWS will provide

|*Revenue Terms*
|How revenue is split and paid

|*Duration*
|How long agreement lasts, renewal terms

|*IP Clarity*
|Explicit statement that creator retains IP

|*Exit Provisions*
|How either party can end agreement

|*Dispute Resolution*
|How disagreements are handled
|===

==== Agreement Principles

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Principle |Application

|*Clarity*
|Terms clear and understandable

|*Fairness*
|Balanced terms, not one-sided

|*Flexibility*
|Ability to adapt as needs change

|*Exit Freedom*
|Creator can leave with IP

|*Good Faith*
|Both parties commit to good-faith execution
|===

==== What Active Partnership Looks Like

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===

|*Publication Work*
|Preparing and publishing your work

|*Ongoing Support*
|CWS providing agreed services

|*Communication*
|Regular check-ins and updates

|*Growth*
|Developing relationship and expanding collaboration

==== Onboarding Process

. *Planning* — Develop initial work plan together

==== Ongoing Relationship

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Aspect |How It Works

|Scheduled check-ins based on your preferences

|*Support Access*
|Reach out when you need help

|*Performance Tracking*

|Ongoing dialogue about what's working

== Changing the Relationship

=== Adjusting Support Level

You can request support level changes:

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Change Type |Process

|*Increase Support*
|Request additional services; adjust revenue split accordingly

|*Decrease Support*

== Frequently Asked Questions

=== About IP

*Q: Does CWS take ownership of my work?*

A: No. Never. You retain full ownership of your intellectual property. This is non-negotiable.

*Q: Can I work with other publishers while partnered with CWS?*

A: Generally yes, unless you've agreed to exclusivity for specific works. Default is non-exclusive.

*Q: What happens to my IP if CWS closes?*

A: Your IP is yours. CWS closing would affect services, not ownership.

=== About Terms

*Q: Are revenue splits negotiable?*

A: Splits correspond to support levels. You choose your support level, which determines the split range. Specific terms are negotiated within that framework.

*Q: Can I change my support level later?*

A: Yes. As your needs change, we can adjust the support level and corresponding terms.

*Q: What if I'm unhappy with the partnership?*

A: Talk to us. Many issues can be resolved through communication. If not, you can exit per agreement terms.

=== About Process

*Q: How long does the process take?*

A: Varies based on complexity. Simple partnerships can be established quickly; complex ones take longer. Quality matters more than speed.

*Q: Do I need to have finished work to partner?*

A: Not necessarily. We can partner at various stages, though having work samples helps assessment.

*Q: What if CWS says no?*

A: Not every partnership is a good fit. If we decline, we'll be honest about why and may suggest alternatives.
