== icon:question-circle[] Frequently Asked Questions

=== icon:copyright[] About IP

*Q: Does {company-short} take ownership of my work?*

[.lead]
A: *No. Never.* You retain full ownership of your intellectual property. This is non-negotiable.

*Q: Can I work with other publishers while partnered with {company-short}?*

A: Generally yes, unless you've agreed to exclusivity for specific works. Default is non-exclusive.

*Q: What happens to my IP if {company-short} closes?*

A: Your IP is yours. {company-short} closing would affect services, not ownership.

=== icon:file-signature[] About Terms

*Q: Are revenue splits negotiable?*

A: Splits correspond to support levels. You choose your support level, which determines the split range. Specific terms are negotiated within that framework.

*Q: Can I change my support level later?*

A: Yes. As your needs change, we can adjust the support level and corresponding terms.

*Q: What if I'm unhappy with the partnership?*

A: Talk to us. Many issues can be resolved through communication. If not, you can exit per agreement terms.

=== icon:cogs[] About Process

*Q: How long does the process take?*

A: Varies based on complexity. Simple partnerships can be established quickly; complex ones take longer. Quality matters more than speed.

*Q: Do I need to have finished work to partner?*

A: Not necessarily. We can partner at various stages, though having work samples helps assessment.

*Q: What if {company-short} says no?*

A: Not every partnership is a good fit. If we decline, we'll be honest about why and may suggest alternatives.
