== Document Purpose

This document establishes the *economic model* underlying the publishing house approach. It explains how revenue works, what determines revenue splits, and how CWS achieves financial sustainability while maintaining Kingdom alignment.

[NOTE]
====
Economics serve mission, not reverse. Every financial structure exists to enable CWS to serve creators and the Kingdom sustainably, not to maximize extraction.
====

== Economic Philosophy

=== Mission-Driven Economics

CWS economic model is driven by mission, not profit maximization:

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
|Aspect |Profit-Maximization |Mission-Driven

|*Primary Goal*
|Maximum shareholder return
|Sustainable Kingdom service

|*Creator Relationship*
|Source of extraction
|Partner in success

|*Pricing Strategy*
|What market will bear
|Fair exchange for value provided

|*Growth Driver*
|Revenue growth above all
|Mission expansion enables growth

|*Long-term View*
|Quarterly optimization
|Generational sustainability
|===

=== Fair Exchange Principle

[IMPORTANT]
====
*The Principle:* CWS receives fair compensation for services provided—no more, no less. We don't extract maximum value; we exchange value fairly.
====

What this means:

* Revenue splits reflect actual value CWS provides
* Creators choosing less support keep more revenue
* CWS earns more by serving better, not by extracting more
* Transparency in what drives economics

== Revenue Split Model

=== The Core Concept

Revenue splits with external creatives vary based on *support level*:

[IMPORTANT]
====
*Creator Choice:* The creator chooses their support level and corresponding revenue split. More CWS support means higher CWS share; less support means creator keeps more. *The creator decides.*
====

=== Support Levels

==== Full Support

[sidebar]
****
*What CWS Provides:*

* Complete marketing strategy and execution
* Full publication production and preparation
* Consultation and strategic guidance
* Distribution across all available channels
* Access to all infrastructure and tools
* Ongoing support and relationship management

*Revenue Split:* Higher CWS share (creator receives lower %)

*Best For:* Creators who want comprehensive support and are willing to share more revenue for it
****

==== Moderate Support

[sidebar]
****
*What CWS Provides:*

* Marketing support (strategy or execution, not both)
* Publication assistance with creator involvement
* Periodic consultation
* Distribution through primary channels
* Access to standard infrastructure
* Regular check-ins and support

*Revenue Split:* Medium CWS share (balanced split)

*Best For:* Creators who want significant help but can handle some aspects themselves
****

==== Light Support

[sidebar]
****
*What CWS Provides:*

* Marketing guidance (creator executes)
* Publication review and quality assurance
* Available consultation when needed
* Distribution access
* Infrastructure access
* Support on request

*Revenue Split:* Lower CWS share (creator keeps more %)

*Best For:* Experienced creators who need less hands-on help
****

==== Infrastructure Only

[sidebar]
****
*What CWS Provides:*

* Access to CWS platforms and tools
* Distribution channel access
* Basic support for technical issues
* Network access

*Revenue Split:* Minimal CWS share (creator keeps majority %)

*Best For:* Established creators who primarily need infrastructure access
****

=== Split Guidelines

[cols="1,1,1,2",options="header"]
|===
|Support Level |CWS Share |Creator Share |Notes

|Full Support
|Higher %
|Lower %
|Specific % negotiated per agreement

|Moderate Support
|Medium %
|Medium %
|Balanced based on actual support

|Light Support
|Lower %
|Higher %
|Creator retains most

|Infrastructure Only
|Minimal %
|Majority %
|Near-platform level fees
|===

[NOTE]
====
*Specific percentages* are determined in individual partnership agreements based on actual services provided, market conditions, and mutual agreement. The framework above provides structure; specific terms are negotiated.
====

=== Autonomy in Choice

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Principle |Application

|*Creator Chooses*
|Creator selects support level based on their needs

|*Can Change*
|Support level can be adjusted over time

|*No Pressure*
|CWS doesn't push for higher-share arrangements

|*Transparent Reasoning*
|Splits clearly tied to support provided

|*Mutual Agreement*
|Both parties agree to terms before proceeding
|===
