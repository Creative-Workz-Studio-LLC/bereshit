== Document Purpose

This document defines the *faith-based businesses and organizations* CWS exists to serve — churches, ministries, Christian nonprofits, and Kingdom-aligned startups. It establishes their unique needs, how CWS serves them, and what alignment requires.

[quote, Acts 2:44-45 (KJV)]
____
And all that believed were together, and had all things common; And sold their possessions and goods, and parted them to all men, as every man had need.
____

[NOTE]
====
*Organizations, Not Just Individuals:* While <<05-01-CREATIVES-WE-SERVE.adoc#,creatives>> are often individuals, this audience includes organizations — entities with missions, employees, and operational complexity that require different service approaches.
====

== Who Are Faith-Based Businesses?

=== Defining the Audience

Faith-based businesses are organizations whose operations are grounded in faith and aligned with Kingdom purposes. This includes:

[cols="1,2,3",options="header"]
|===
|Type |Description |Examples

|*Churches*
|Local congregations of believers
|Community churches, house churches, church plants

|*Ministries*
|Organizations with specific ministry focus
|Teaching ministries, missions organizations, counseling ministries

|*Christian Nonprofits*
|501(c)(3) organizations serving Kingdom purposes
|Food banks, pregnancy centers, homeless services

|*Kingdom-Aligned Startups*
|Businesses built on faith principles
|Faith-tech startups, Christian publishers, values-aligned ventures

|*Para-Church Organizations*
|Organizations supporting the church without being churches
|Conferences, networks, educational institutions

|*Faith-Based Service Providers*
|Professional services with faith foundation
|Christian counseling, faith-based consulting, ministry accounting

|===

=== The Common Thread

What unites these organizations:

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Characteristic |What It Means

|*Mission-Driven*
|Purpose beyond profit — serving Kingdom purposes

|*Values-Grounded*
|Operations shaped by biblical values

|*Community-Oriented*
|Serve people, not just stakeholders

|*Stewardship Mindset*
|Resources managed as stewards, not owners

|*Eternal Perspective*
|Outcomes measured beyond quarterly results

|===

'''

== Types of Faith-Based Businesses

=== Churches

[sidebar]
****
*Who They Are:*

Local congregations — large or small, established or planting.

*Unique Needs:*

* Congregation communication (members, visitors)
* Service management (scheduling, media, giving)
* Privacy-respecting member data
* Technology that doesn't push worldly values
* Budget constraints (often volunteer-heavy)

*CWS Approach:*

Simple, affordable infrastructure. Training for volunteer tech teams. Privacy-first design. Long-term partnership mindset.
****

=== Ministries

[sidebar]
****
*Who They Are:*

Organizations with specific ministry focus — teaching, missions, counseling, discipleship.

*Unique Needs:*

* Content distribution (teaching, resources)
* Global reach (often international scope)
* Donor management and communication
* Mission-critical reliability
* Often remote and distributed teams

*CWS Approach:*

Scalable content infrastructure. Global-ready systems. Donor privacy and security. Reliable, distributed architecture.
****

=== Christian Nonprofits

[sidebar]
****
*Who They Are:*

501(c)(3) organizations serving Kingdom purposes — food banks, shelters, crisis centers, community services.

*Unique Needs:*

* Client/beneficiary management
* Volunteer coordination
* Compliance and reporting
* Integration with government systems (sometimes)
* Maximum resources to mission (minimal overhead)

*CWS Approach:*

Efficient systems that minimize overhead. Volunteer-friendly tools. Compliance-ready where needed. Cost-effective solutions.
****

=== Kingdom-Aligned Startups

[sidebar]
****
*Who They Are:*

New businesses built on faith principles — tech startups, service companies, product businesses.

*Unique Needs:*

* Scalable infrastructure for growth
* Modern technology stack
* Speed and agility
* Maintaining values under growth pressure
* Sustainable business models

*CWS Approach:*

Modern, scalable infrastructure. Consultation on values-aligned growth. Trade network for customer and partner connections.
****

== Alignment Requirements

=== What Alignment Means for Organizations

[IMPORTANT]
====
*Not Just the Label:* CWS serves organizations building for Kingdom purposes, not just those with Christian labels. Having "Christian" in the name doesn't guarantee alignment; consistent values in operation does.
====

=== Alignment Markers

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Marker |What It Looks Like

|*Mission Clarity*
|Clear about purpose; knows why it exists

|*Values Consistency*
|Operations match stated beliefs

|*Integrity in Dealing*
|Honest with donors, clients, partners, staff

|*Stewardship Posture*
|Resources managed faithfully, not hoarded

|*Community Benefit*
|Serves beyond self-interest

|*Eternal Perspective*
|Outcomes measured beyond quarterly metrics

|===

=== Red Flags

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Red Flag |Why It Concerns

|*Mission drift*
|Organization says one thing, does another

|*Extraction posture*
|Takes from community more than gives

|*Leadership integrity issues*
|Leaders don't embody stated values

|*Secrecy about operations*
|Unwilling to be transparent

|*Adversarial relationships*
|Pattern of conflict with partners, staff

|===
