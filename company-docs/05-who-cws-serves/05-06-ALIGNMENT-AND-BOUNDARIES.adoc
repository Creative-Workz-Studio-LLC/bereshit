== Document Purpose

This document establishes the *alignment requirements and boundaries* for who CWS serves. It clarifies who we don't serve, articulates the common thread uniting all who we do serve, and explains why alignment matters more than volume.

[quote, 2 Corinthians 6:14 (KJV)]
____
Be ye not unequally yoked together with unbelievers: for what fellowship hath righteousness with unrighteousness? and what communion hath light with darkness?
____

[NOTE]
====
*Boundaries Enable Service:* Knowing who we don't serve isn't exclusion for exclusion's sake. Clear boundaries protect mission focus and enable deeper service to those we are called to serve.
====

'''

== Why Boundaries Matter

=== The Temptation of Volume

[source]
----
THE VOLUME TRAP:

    "Serve everyone" ───► Diluted mission
                    ───► Shallow engagement
                    ───► Values compromise
                    ───► Mission drift
                    ───► No longer serving anyone well
----

=== What Boundaries Protect

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Protected Value |How Boundaries Help

|*Mission Focus*
|Energy concentrated on actual calling

|*Resource Stewardship*
|Limited resources applied where most effective

|*Values Integrity*
|No compromise to accommodate misalignment

|*Relationship Depth*
|Fewer, deeper relationships instead of many shallow

|*Long-term Sustainability*
|Not overextended or compromised

|===

=== Boundaries as Service

[IMPORTANT]
====
*Saying "no" enables "yes."* Every "no" to misalignment protects capacity for deeper "yes" to those we're called to serve. Boundaries aren't barriers to service; they're enablers of service.
====

'''

== Who We Don't Serve

=== Categories of Non-Service

CWS declines to serve in certain situations — not from hostility, but from clarity:

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Category |Description

|*Not Aligned*
|Values fundamentally incompatible with Kingdom purposes

|*Not Ready*
|Not in position to receive what CWS offers

|*Not Honest*
|Deceptive about intentions, capabilities, or situation

|===

=== Not Aligned: Values Incompatibility

[sidebar]
****
*Who They Are:*

Individuals or organizations whose values fundamentally conflict with Kingdom purposes.

*Examples:*

* Work that promotes explicit sin as good
* Organizations built on deception or exploitation
* Content that mocks or attacks faith
* Ventures designed to harm rather than help

*Why We Don't Serve:*

Partnership requires shared foundation. Without values alignment, "partnership" becomes mere transaction at best, corruption at worst.

*How We Respond:*

Honest, gracious decline. We don't pretend alignment exists when it doesn't. We wish well and part ways.
****

=== Not Ready: Timing and Preparation

[sidebar]
****
*Who They Are:*

Individuals or organizations not yet in position to benefit from CWS partnership.

*Examples:*

* Creative work not yet at publishable quality
* Organizations without basic operational stability
* Individuals not clear on their own vision
* Situations where CWS help isn't what's actually needed

*Why We Don't Serve (Yet):*

Premature partnership serves no one. If CWS engagement won't actually help, proceeding harms both parties.

*How We Respond:*

Honest assessment with kindness. Specific feedback where helpful. Door open for future when readiness develops.
****

=== Not Honest: Integrity Failures

[sidebar]
****
*Who They Are:*

Those who misrepresent themselves, their work, or their intentions.

*Examples:*

* Misrepresenting work quality or stage
* Hidden agendas that contradict stated purposes
* Exploitative intent disguised as partnership
* Patterns of deception in dealings

*Why We Don't Serve:*

Partnership requires trust. Deception destroys the foundation that makes partnership possible.

*How We Respond:*

Direct, truthful identification of the issue. Clear boundary. Not punitive, but protective — of CWS, other partners, and ultimately of the dishonest party who needs correction, not enablement.
****

'''

== Content Boundaries

=== What CWS Won't Publish

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Category |Examples

|*Explicit Promotion of Sin*
|Content celebrating or promoting what Scripture clearly identifies as sin

|*Anti-Faith Content*
|Material designed to attack, mock, or undermine genuine faith

|*Exploitative Content*
|Material designed to exploit vulnerable audiences

|*Deceptive Content*
|Material presenting falsehood as truth

|*Harmful Content*
|Material likely to cause genuine harm to consumers

|===

=== What CWS Will Consider

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Category |Why It's Acceptable

|*Dark Themes Handled Honestly*
|Scripture itself addresses dark themes; honest treatment ≠ promotion

|*Non-Explicit Faith Content*
|Work can reflect values without explicit gospel presentation

|*Controversial Topics*
|Prophetic work often challenges conventions; controversy isn't disqualifying

|*Imperfect Work*
|Excellence is goal; perfection isn't required

|*Challenging Content*
|Difficult truth-telling serves Kingdom

|===

=== The Discernment Required

[NOTE]
====
*Not Formula, But Wisdom:* Content evaluation isn't mechanical. It requires wisdom, discernment, and often conversation. CWS leadership makes judgment calls on edge cases, grounded in values and prayer.
====

== Alignment Over Volume

=== The Fundamental Priority

[IMPORTANT]
====
*CWS prioritizes alignment over volume.* We'd rather serve 10 deeply aligned partners than 1,000 poorly aligned customers. Alignment protects mission; volume without alignment destroys it.
====

=== Why Volume Isn't Primary

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Volume Priority |Consequence

|*Maximum customers*
|Diluted service quality

|*Revenue maximization*
|Values compromise inevitable

|*Rapid growth*
|Culture degradation

|*Market share*
|Mission drift

|*Broad appeal*
|Distinctive value lost

|===

=== Why Alignment Is Primary

[cols="1,3",options="header"]

|Genuine partnership possible

|*Values consistency*
|Integrity maintained

== Assessing Alignment

=== How CWS Evaluates

Alignment assessment isn't a checklist; it's a discernment process:

[source]
----
ALIGNMENT ASSESSMENT PROCESS:

    ┌──────────────────┐
    │ INITIAL CONTACT  │  First impressions, basic fit
    └────────┬─────────┘
             │
    ┌────────▼─────────┐
    │ CONVERSATION     │  Deeper exploration of values,
    │                  │  purpose, alignment
    └────────┬─────────┘
             │
    ┌────────▼─────────┐
    │ WORK REVIEW      │  Does actual work reflect
    │                  │  stated values?
    └────────┬─────────┘
             │
    ┌────────▼─────────┐
    │ DISCERNMENT      │  Prayer, wisdom, counsel
    │                  │  for unclear cases
    └────────┬─────────┘
             │
    ┌────────▼─────────┐
    │ DECISION         │  Clear communication of
    │                  │  outcome
    └──────────────────┘
----

=== Questions We Consider

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Question |What It Reveals

|"What is the purpose of this work?"
|Underlying motivation and alignment

|"Who benefits and how?"
|Service orientation vs. extraction

|"What values drive the approach?"
|True alignment, not just stated alignment

|"How are difficult situations handled?"
|Character revealed under pressure

|"What is the long-term vision?"
|Sustainable alignment vs. temporary convenience

|===

=== Red Flags in Assessment

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Red Flag |What It May Indicate

|*Inconsistency between words and work*
|Stated values aren't actual values

|*Pressure to skip assessment*
|Something to hide or ignore

|*Hostility to values conversation*
|Values misalignment probable

|*Pattern of broken relationships*
|Likely to repeat pattern

|*Exploitation history*
|Character issue, not situation

|===

== Gracious Boundaries

=== How We Say No

[IMPORTANT]
====
*Boundaries with dignity.* When CWS declines, we do so with honesty and respect. People are not rejected; partnerships that wouldn't serve either party are declined.
====

=== Boundary Principles

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Principle |Application

|*Direct*
|Clear communication; no ambiguity

|*Honest*
|True reasons given, not pretexts

|*Kind*
|Decline delivered with respect

|*Final*
|Decision is decision; not negotiable to pressure

|*Open Future*
|Door not permanently closed where appropriate

|===

=== What We Don't Do

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Avoid |Why

|*Stringing along*
|Wastes everyone's time; unkind

|*Vague excuses*
|Dishonest; doesn't help them

|*Public shame*
|Unnecessary cruelty

|*Negotiable boundaries*
|Pressure rewards undermine integrity

|*Permanent rejection always*
|People change; situations change

|===

'''

== When Alignment Changes

=== Alignment Can Shift

Alignment isn't static. Partners may:
* Grow more aligned over time
* Drift from alignment
* Experience sudden change due to circumstances

=== How CWS Responds to Drift

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Stage |Response

|*Early Signs*
|Conversation; clarification; concern expressed

|*Clear Pattern*
|Direct discussion; expectations restated

|*Continued Drift*
|Partnership modification or conclusion discussed

|*Fundamental Break*
|Partnership ended with grace

|===

=== Partnership Conclusion Process

When alignment breaks:

. *Direct Conversation* — Issues clearly identified
. *Opportunity to Address* — Where appropriate and possible
. *Clear Decision* — When alignment cannot be restored
. *Gracious Exit* — Partnership ends with dignity
. *Settlement* — Appropriate financial and practical conclusion
. *Door Position* — Clarity on whether future partnership possible
