== Document Purpose

This document establishes CWS's commitment to serving *local communities* — neighborhoods, local nonprofits, and settlements. This isn't about remote digital partnerships; it's about physical presence and personal service in the places where CWS people live.

[quote, Jeremiah 29:7 (KJV)]
____
And seek the peace of the city whither I have caused you to be carried away captives, and pray unto the LORD for it: for in the peace thereof shall ye have peace.
____

[NOTE]
====
*Presence Over Digital-Only:* CWS people don't just serve through screens. We serve where we live — in person, with our hands, in our communities. This is who we are, not just what we do.
====

'''

=== Neighborhoods

[sidebar]
****
*Who They Are:*

The actual physical areas where CWS people live — streets, apartment complexes, developments, rural areas.

*How We Serve:*

* Know neighbors by name
* Available in times of need
* Present at community gatherings
* Practical help (not just "thoughts and prayers")
* Witness through life, not just words

*Why It Matters:*

This is the most immediate circle of influence. If we can't love those physically nearest, our broader mission rings hollow.
****

=== Local Nonprofits

[sidebar]
****
*Who They Are:*

Organizations serving the local community — homeless shelters, food banks, crisis pregnancy centers, after-school programs, elder services.

*How We Serve:*

* Volunteer time and skills
* Technology assistance
* Board service where appropriate
* Financial support
* Professional services at reduced rates

*Why It Matters:*

These organizations are often under-resourced. CWS people have skills that can multiply their impact. We serve through them, not just alongside them.
****

=== Community Settlements

[sidebar]
****
*Who They Are:*

Intentional communities and settlement houses that provide ongoing presence in specific areas. For CWS, this particularly includes Guardian Angel Settlement.

*Guardian Angel Settlement Relationship:*

Guardian Angel Settlement represents a specific CWS commitment to long-term, embedded community presence. This isn't a client relationship — it's a familial commitment to a place and its people.

*How We Serve:*

* Ongoing presence and participation
* Technology infrastructure support
* Skills training and education
* Economic development support
* Long-term commitment, not projects

*Why It Matters:*

Settlement work represents the deepest form of community service — choosing to be permanently present with and for a community.
****

== Community Service Values

=== Guiding Principles

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Principle |Application

|*Humility*
|We serve under, not over; we learn, not just teach

|*Consistency*
|Long-term presence, not drive-by charity

|*Relationship*
|People, not projects; names, not numbers

|*Empowerment*
|Build capacity, not dependency

|*Respect*
|Honor community wisdom and leadership

|*Integration*
|Service woven into life, not compartmentalized

|===

=== What We Avoid

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Avoid |Why

|*Savior Complex*
|Communities have assets; we're partners, not saviors

|*Project Mentality*
|Relationships don't have end dates

|*Tokenism*
|Genuine service, not photo opportunities

|*Imposition*
|Serve what's wanted, not what we assume is needed

|*Condescension*
|Equal dignity; different resources

|===

'''
