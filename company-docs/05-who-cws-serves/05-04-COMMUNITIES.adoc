[.lead]
This document establishes {company-short}'s commitment to serving *local communities* — neighborhoods, local nonprofits, and settlements. This isn't about remote digital partnerships; it's about physical presence and personal service in the places where {company-short} people live.

[sidebar]
.icon:star[] Why This Document Matters
****
Technology alone doesn't transform communities — *presence* does. {company-short} is committed to being rooted in real places with real neighbors. This document articulates our commitment to incarnational service where we live, not just digital reach from a distance. It grounds our global vision in local reality.
****

== icon:bullseye[] Document Purpose

[quote, Jeremiah 29:7 (KJV)]
____
And seek the peace of the city whither I have caused you to be carried away captives, and pray unto the LORD for it: for in the peace thereof shall ye have peace.
____

[NOTE]
.icon:lightbulb[] Presence Over Digital-Only
====
{company-short} people don't just serve through screens. We serve where we live — in person, with our hands, in our communities. This is who we are, not just what we do.
====

=== icon:map-marker-alt[] Neighborhoods

[sidebar]
.icon:home[] Neighborhood Profile
****
*Who They Are:*

The actual physical areas where {company-short} people live — streets, apartment complexes, developments, rural areas.

*How We Serve:*

* Know neighbors by name
* Available in times of need
* Present at community gatherings
* Practical help (not just "thoughts and prayers")
* Witness through life, not just words

*Why It Matters:*

This is the most immediate circle of influence. If we can't love those physically nearest, our broader mission rings hollow.
****

=== icon:hands-helping[] Local Nonprofits

[sidebar]
.icon:hands-helping[] Nonprofit Partnership Profile
****
*Who They Are:*

Organizations serving the local community — homeless shelters, food banks, crisis pregnancy centers, after-school programs, elder services.

*How We Serve:*

* Volunteer time and skills
* Technology assistance
* Board service where appropriate
* Financial support
* Professional services at reduced rates

*Why It Matters:*

These organizations are often under-resourced. {company-short} people have skills that can multiply their impact. We serve through them, not just alongside them.
****

=== icon:building[] Community Settlements

[sidebar]
.icon:building[] Settlement Profile
****
*Who They Are:*

Intentional communities and settlement houses that provide ongoing presence in specific areas. For {company-short}, this particularly includes Guardian Angel Settlement.

*Guardian Angel Settlement Relationship:*

Guardian Angel Settlement represents a specific {company-short} commitment to long-term, embedded community presence. This isn't a client relationship — it's a familial commitment to a place and its people.

*How We Serve:*

* Ongoing presence and participation
* Technology infrastructure support
* Skills training and education
* Economic development support
* Long-term commitment, not projects

*Why It Matters:*

Settlement work represents the deepest form of community service — choosing to be permanently present with and for a community.
****
