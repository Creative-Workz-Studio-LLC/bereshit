[.lead]
This document defines how {company-short} serves the *broader Body of Christ* — believers across denominations who recognize the times and are preparing for what's coming. This isn't about individual partnerships or business engagements; it's about providing infrastructure and resources to the universal church.

[sidebar]
.icon:star[] Why This Document Matters
****
The Body of Christ faces a future that will require *practical infrastructure*, not just spiritual preparation. {company-short} exists to ensure believers have access to technology, communication networks, and commerce alternatives that function *without compromise*. This document articulates how we serve millions who may never become direct partners but who need what we're building.
****

== icon:bullseye[] Document Purpose

[quote, 1 Corinthians 12:27 (KJV)]
____
Now ye are the body of Christ, and members in particular.
____

[NOTE]
.icon:lightbulb[] Practical Service, Not Theoretical
====
{company-short} serves the Body through tangible resources and infrastructure — accessible technology, mark-free alternatives, and preparation resources. Not just ideas; actual tools people can use.
====
