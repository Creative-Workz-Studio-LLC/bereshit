== Why This Manual Exists

This is not a typical company document.

{company-short} exists to build technology for the Kingdom — technology that honors God, serves others, and has eternal value. That mission requires clarity. It requires that everyone who joins this work understands what they're joining, why it matters, and how we operate.

The prophet Habakkuk was told to write the vision *plainly* — not because God enjoys paperwork, but because a vision that isn't written becomes a vision that drifts. People who read it should be able to *run* with it. That's the standard for this manual.

This document is also a covenant. When the Board approves this manual, they are affirming not just information but *commitment*. When team members read it, they are encountering not just policies but *promises*. When partners reference it, they are holding us to our *word*.

We write it down because our word must be our bond.

=== What This Manual Does

[cols="1,4"]
|===

|✓
|*Defines identity* — Who {company-short} is at its core, rooted in Scripture and expressed in practice

|✓
|*Articulates mission* — Why we exist, what we're pursuing, and what success looks like

|✓
|*Establishes values* — The non-negotiable principles that guide every decision we make

|✓
|*Documents operations* — How we actually work, not how we wish we worked

|✓
|*Enables alignment* — Ensures everyone understands the same picture and can hold us accountable

|✓
|*Creates accountability* — Gives the Board, team, and partners a standard to measure us against

|===

=== What This Manual Does Not Do

[cols="1,4"]
|===

|✗
|*Replace Scripture* — The Bible is final authority. This manual derives from Scripture; it does not supersede it.

|✗
|*Substitute for prayer* — No document replaces the leading of the Holy Spirit. Discernment is always required.

|✗
|*Provide rigid rules* — Principles are constant; application requires wisdom. This is a guide, not a cage.

|✗
|*Remain static* — We learn, we grow, we correct. This manual evolves as God reveals more.

|===

== How to Use This Manual

Different audiences should approach this manual differently. The following guides provide recommended reading paths based on role.

=== For Board Members

. *Start with* Chapter 11 (The Whole Thing) for the complete overview
. *Focus on* Chapter 07 (Core Values) — this is your decision-making framework
. *Review* Chapter 08 (Mission and Vision) for alignment criteria
. *Reference* Chapter 09 (The Team) for governance specifics
. *Use this manual* when evaluating strategic decisions and holding leadership accountable

=== For Team Members

. *Start with* Chapter 11 (The Whole Thing) for the complete overview
. *Study* Chapter 07 (Core Values) — this is our culture, non-negotiable
. *Read* Chapter 08 (Mission and Vision) to understand our purpose
. *Then dive into* role-specific chapters based on your work
. *Use this manual* to understand context and ensure your work aligns

=== For Creative Partners

. *Start with* Chapter 11, Section 11-09 (For Partners) — written specifically for you
. *Focus on* Chapter 02 (Publishing House Model) — how partnership works
. *Review* Chapter 04 (What CWS Provides) to understand available services
. *Check* Chapter 05 (Who CWS Serves) to see where you fit
. *Use this manual* to understand partnership terms and expectations

=== For Faith-Based Business Partners

. *Start with* Chapter 11, Section 11-09 (For Partners) — written specifically for you
. *Review* Chapter 01 (Entity Overview) to understand who CWS is
. *Study* Chapter 06 (Business Model) for economic framework
. *Check* Chapter 04 (What CWS Provides) for available services
. *Use this manual* to evaluate fit and understand engagement terms

== A Word Before Reading

This manual documents human effort in service of divine vision.

It is fallible. It is incomplete. It is subject to revision. We do not claim perfection — we claim faithfulness. We have done our best to articulate what God has shown us, knowing that our understanding will deepen over time.

*The Lord leads; we follow.* Scripture is the final authority. When this manual and Scripture conflict, Scripture wins. When this manual and the Holy Spirit's leading conflict, follow the Spirit. We write to clarify, not to constrain.

*Read this with discernment.* Ask the Lord what He wants you to see. Some sections will convict. Some will challenge. Some will encourage. All of it is offered in good faith, seeking to honor God in how we work.

*Hold it loosely. Hold Him firmly.* This document is a tool, not an idol. It serves the mission; the mission doesn't serve the document. If something here needs to change, we will change it. The goal is faithfulness, not consistency for its own sake.
