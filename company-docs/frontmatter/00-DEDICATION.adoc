[.text-center]
=== To the Triune God

[.text-center]
*To the Father* — the Creator who created creation and called us to create. +
You spoke, and there was light. You formed, and there was order. +
You breathed, and there was life. +
Everything we build begins because You built first.

{empty} +

[.text-center]
*To the Son* — the Word through whom all things were made. +
In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God. +
Without You, nothing was made that has been made. +
Every line of code, every structure, every system — all things hold together in You.

{empty} +

[.text-center]
*To the Holy Spirit* — who moves over the face of the deep and brings form from void. +
You hovered over the waters at creation. You hover over our work now. +
Where there is chaos, You bring order. Where there is nothing, You bring something. +
We depend on Your guidance in every decision.

'''

[.text-center]
=== An Offering of Worship

[.text-center]
This work is offered as worship — +
not because it is worthy, but because *You* are worthy.

[.text-center]
These foundations are laid as service — +
not to build our own kingdom, but to serve *Yours*.

[.text-center]
This manual is written so that others may run — +
that the vision would be plain, and those who read it would know the way.

{empty} +

[.text-center]
[.lead]
*"All things were made by him; and without him was not any thing made that was made."*

[.text-center]
— _John 1:3 (KJV)_

'''

[.text-center]
=== A Declaration of Purpose

[.text-center]
_We build because He built first._ +
The Creator made us in His image — creative beings meant to create.

[.text-center]
_We serve because He served first._ +
The King of Kings came not to be served, but to serve.

[.text-center]
_We prepare because He told us to be ready._ +
The Bridegroom comes, and we must have oil in our lamps.

{empty} +

[.text-center]
We do not know when the age will turn. +
We do not know what trials lie ahead. +
But we know *who* holds the future — +
and we build for *His* Kingdom, not our own.

'''

[.text-center]
=== To Those Who Walk This Path

[.text-center]
To the Board who holds us accountable — +
iron sharpening iron, friends who became family, +
who said "yes" to a vision that makes no sense apart from God.

{empty} +

[.text-center]
To the team we haven't met yet — +
you are already prayed for, already anticipated, +
already part of what God is building.

{empty} +

[.text-center]
To the partners who will join this work — +
may you find here what the world cannot offer: +
a place where your gifts are honored, your ownership protected, +
and your work serves something eternal.

{empty} +

[.text-center]
To every creative who will read these pages — +
know that you were made to make things. +
The Creator put that in you. It is not accident. It is design. +
We exist to help you steward that gift.

'''

[.text-center]
=== The Founding Commitment

[.text-center]
On this day, we set our hand to the plow. +
We will not look back.

[.text-center]
We commit to build with excellence — +
because mediocrity does not honor the Master Builder.

[.text-center]
We commit to serve with integrity — +
because our word must be our bond.

[.text-center]
We commit to prepare with urgency — +
because the night comes when no one can work.

[.text-center]
We commit to trust with patience — +
because God's timing is not our timing, +
and His ways are higher than our ways.

{empty} +

[.text-center]
[.lead]
*"Write the vision, and make it plain upon tables, +
that he may run that readeth it."*

[.text-center]
— _Habakkuk 2:2 (KJV)_

'''
