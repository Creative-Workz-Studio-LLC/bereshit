[.lead]
This chapter describes *who builds {company-short}* — the current team, the board, the network structure, and the larger team the Lord has planned that we haven't met yet.

[sidebar]
.icon:star[] Why This Chapter Matters
****
Organizations are built by people. This chapter documents who is here, who governs, and how we're preparing for who's coming. We're being *faithful with who's here* while building foundations for *who's on the way*.
****

== icon:file-alt[] Document Purpose

[quote, Ecclesiastes 4:9-10 (KJV)]
____
Two are better than one; because they have a good reward for their labour. For if they fall, the one will lift up his fellow: but woe to him that is alone when he falleth; for he hath not another to help him up.
____

[NOTE]
.icon:lightbulb[] Core Understanding
====
*A Small Team with a Big Vision:* {company-short} operates with a small current team, a growing board, and the understanding that the Lord has a larger team planned. We work faithfully with who's here while remaining open to who's coming.
====
