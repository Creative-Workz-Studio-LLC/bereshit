[.lead]
This document describes *how {company-short} works together* — the principles, practices, and patterns that define team culture. Culture isn't what we say; it's what we do.

[sidebar]
.icon:star[] Why This Document Matters
****
Culture shapes everything. How people work together determines what they can build. This document establishes *the patterns* that enable Kingdom work — practices that reflect our values in daily operation.
****

== icon:file-alt[] Document Purpose

[quote, Amos 3:3 (KJV)]
____
Can two walk together, except they be agreed?
____

[NOTE]
.icon:lightbulb[] Core Understanding
====
*Culture Is Lived:* Culture isn't posters on walls or statements in documents. Culture is how people actually behave — especially when no one's watching. These principles must be lived, not just stated.
====

'''
