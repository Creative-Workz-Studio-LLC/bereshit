[.lead]
This document describes how {company-short} *develops team members* — onboarding new members and growing existing ones. We invest in people because people build the mission.

[sidebar]
.icon:star[] Why This Document Matters
****
People don't arrive fully formed. Team members need *onboarding* to understand {company-short}, and *development* to grow in capability. This investment in people is investment in the mission.
****

== icon:file-alt[] Document Purpose

[quote, Proverbs 27:17 (KJV)]
____
Iron sharpeneth iron; so a man sharpeneth the countenance of his friend.
____

[NOTE]
.icon:lightbulb[] Core Understanding
====
*Growth Is Expected:* We don't expect team members to stay static. Everyone should be learning and growing. {company-short} invests in development because capable people build better things.
====

'''
