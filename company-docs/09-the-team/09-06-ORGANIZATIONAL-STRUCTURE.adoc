[.lead]
This document describes {company-short}'s *organizational structure* — how the team is organized currently and how it may evolve as {company-short} grows. Structure serves mission; it adapts as needed.

[sidebar]
.icon:star[] Why This Document Matters
****
Structure determines how decisions flow and how work gets done. Understanding the structure helps everyone know *where they fit* and *how things work* — enabling effective collaboration.
****

== icon:file-alt[] Document Purpose

[quote, 1 Corinthians 14:40 (KJV)]
____
Let all things be done decently and in order.
____

[NOTE]
.icon:lightbulb[] Core Understanding
====
*Structure Serves Mission:* Organizational structure isn't an end in itself. Structure exists to serve the mission. The right structure is the one that enables {company-short} to accomplish its purpose effectively.
====

'''
