{empty} +

Everything we build begins because You built first.

Every line of code, every structure, every system — all things hold together in You.

{empty} +

{empty} +

*To the Holy Spirit* — who hovered over the waters at creation +
and hovers over our work now. +
Where there is chaos, You bring order. Where there is nothing, You bring something. +
We depend on Your guidance in every decision.
