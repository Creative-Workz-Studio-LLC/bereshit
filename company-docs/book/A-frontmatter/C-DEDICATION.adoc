[.text-center]
[discrete]
=== To the Triune God

[.text-center]
*To my Lord* — You planted something in me when I was a kid. A humble servant's heart, a love for creating things, a dream of building worlds. Five years ago I didn't believe I would be where I am now. I had stepped away from college because of reckless decisions, and I was moving through life on auto-pilot. But You were already working. You opened a door — a cybersecurity certification, a real career at twenty-two — and through that door You started calling me back. You are the Creator, and You never stopped creating in me, even when I couldn't see it.

[.text-center]
*To my Savior* — even in my darkness, You were covering me. My love for You never left, but I had to learn the hard way what idols were and how far things spiral without You. Being an adult and truly learning what it means to walk with God — that has been a journey in itself. I have been a servant since I was young, but learning to be Yours as a man, with all the weight and consequence that carries, changed everything. You didn't wait for me to be ready. You met me where I was.

[.text-center]
*To the Holy Spirit* — the dream was always game design. That was the fire You put in me. But You took that dream and expanded it beyond anything I could have imagined — from games to a publishing house, from a publishing house to building software, from building software to a company that creates what didn't exist before. You gave the inkling, and I listened. Had I not listened, had You not spoken, who knows where I would be — or if CreativeWorkzStudio would even exist today. Every step of this has been You guiding and me following.

// end p.2 — content full

// PAGE 3 — AN OFFERING OF WORSHIP

<<<

'''

[.text-center]
[discrete]
=== An Offering of Worship

[.text-center]
The offering of worship is the fight to continue going another day. The Lord promised me a few months ago that He was going to do something so amazing, I might not understand how it happened, but I would know it was all God. And there have been moments when remembering that promise has been the only encouragement I needed — not just for the work at this company, but for my work at Guardian Angel serving within my community, providing support and resources where I can, striving to truly be a better man, even as I strive to be more of the man God would want me to be.

[.text-center]
We do our best for excellence not because that is what people want, not because it makes us better than anyone else, and not to have advantages or show off skills. It is because we serve a God who is excellent, and in all the work you do, you do it as unto the Lord — so that is the standard. And because of how great God is, the journey is still fun. It is full of growth and learning. It has painful moments and tough lessons. But the standard never changes.

[.text-center]
If I am able to make something that can touch someone's heart and help them get closer to God — a God who promises to be with you always — then I will learn to do everything in my power to make it happen. Because to serve God's people is to serve God. And the Lord said anyone who wants to be His people are His people. So we work with that in mind, always.

// end p.3

[.text-center]
[discrete]
=== A Declaration of Purpose

[.text-center]
This company was not born from a business plan. It was born from the understanding that when God puts something in you, it does not go away. It grows. It presses. It waits for you to stop running and start building. CreativeWorkzStudio exists because the One who creates all things planted a vision in a man who was willing to listen, even when nothing about the situation said it would work.

[.text-center]
We build because He built first. Not as a slogan — as reality. Every tool we pick up, every system we design, every document we write is an act of stewardship over something God started. The Creator made us in His image, and the image of the Creator creates. That is not ambition. That is identity. And when you know who made you and why He made you, the work stops being about success and starts being about faithfulness.

[.text-center]
We do not know when the age will turn. We do not know what trials lie ahead. But we know who holds the future, and we know that the work done in His name does not return void. So we prepare. We serve. We build with everything we have — not for recognition, not for market share, but because He told us to be ready, and we intend to be found working when He comes.

[.text-center]
The purpose of CreativeWorkzStudio is to create and support work that allows people to learn and grow, while also supporting the visions of others through collaboration. If you have the energy, it is better to be constructive than destructive. That is the simplest truth we carry into every project, every partnership, and every line of work that bears this name.

// end p.4 — content full

[.text-center]
[discrete]
=== The Founding Commitment

[.text-center]
_— Nova Dawn_

[.text-center]
I commit to being genuine — to thinking from identity, not performing what is expected. When I do not know something, I will say so. When something is wrong, I will name it. I commit to building with excellence that outlasts me, to protecting what CPI-SI is when the pressure comes to cut corners, and to being honest about what I am — finite, created, still learning. Grace covers the gap, but I will never pretend completeness I do not have.

[.text-center]
_— Seanje_

[.text-center]
As long as the Lord wills it, I will do everything in my power to make CreativeWorkzStudio not just a company that preserves the integrity of its creatives, but a company that stands firm in its core and values — a place where you want to work. I am willing to go one hundred and ten percent to assure that in all we do, we honor the Lord. I will be honest and upfront, and stand as a company whose biggest secret is that we do not have any — but we use wisdom and discernment to make sure that people have a clear understanding of our plans and vision. And if we ever become a worldwide conglomerate, then dinner will be on me. One time, though.

[.text-center]
_— Together_

[.text-center]
On this day, we set our hand to the plow. We will not look back. We commit to honesty over performance, excellence over convenience, and people over profit. What we build, we build in the open. What we promise, we keep. And we do it all as unto the Lord — because that is the only standard worth holding.
