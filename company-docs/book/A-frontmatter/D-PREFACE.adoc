[.text-center]
_Purpose, scope, and authority of this document._

[[P-1]]
==== § P.1 — Purpose

. This <<def-manual,Manual>> sets forth the foundational governance of {company-name}. It defines:
.. the identity of the <<def-company,Company>> — who we are and why we exist;
.. the mission of the <<def-company,Company>> — what we build and whom we serve;
.. the values of the <<def-company,Company>> — the standards that govern every decision; and
.. the operations of the <<def-company,Company>> — how we work and how we are structured.

. This <<def-manual,Manual>> is not a formality. It is a <<def-covenant,covenant>> record — a written declaration of the <<def-company,Company>>'s identity, purpose, and commitments, grounded in Scripture and offered in good faith.

. {company-name} builds <<def-kingdom-technology,Kingdom Technology>> — technology built to honor God, serve others, and carry eternal value. That mission requires that every person who participates in this work understands what they are joining, what is expected, and why. This <<def-manual,Manual>> provides that understanding.

[[P-2]]
==== § P.2 — Scope and Authority

. This <<def-manual,Manual>> serves as the authoritative reference for {company-name}. All <<def-board,Board>> members, <<def-leadership,Leadership>>, <<def-team-members,Team Members>>, and <<def-partners,Partners>> shall be familiar with its contents as they relate to their respective roles and responsibilities.

. Upon approval by <<def-board,the Board>> of Directors, this <<def-manual,Manual>> shall constitute the governing standard by which the <<def-company,Company>>:
.. measures its decisions against the values and mission set forth herein;
.. evaluates its partnerships for alignment with the principles herein established;
.. holds its <<def-leadership,Leadership>> accountable to the commitments herein documented; and
// end p.2 — content full

.. aligns its operations with the identity, purpose, and standards of the <<def-company,Company>>.

[[P-3]]
==== § P.3 — Definitions

When used with initial capital letters in this Manual, the following terms shall carry the specific meanings set forth in this Section and shall be construed accordingly.

. [[def-board]]*"The Board"* means the Board of Directors of {company-name}, as constituted and empowered under the Company's operating agreement.

. [[def-company]]*"The Company"* or *"{company-short}"* means {company-name}, a limited liability company organized under the laws of the {jurisdiction}, Filing Number {filing-number}.

. [[def-covenant]]*"Covenant"* means a mutual commitment grounded in shared values, accountability, and relationship — distinct from a contract, which is grounded in obligation and enforcement. See <<P-4>>.

. [[def-kingdom-technology]]*"Kingdom Technology"* means technology built to honor God, serve others, and produce eternal value — the animating purpose of the Company as defined in <<P-1>>.

. [[def-leadership]]*"Leadership"* means the Chief Officers, division leads, and other persons bearing direct operational responsibility within the Company.

. [[def-team-members]]*"Team Members"* means all persons engaged in the work of the Company, whether as employees, contractors, or volunteers.

[[P-4]]
==== § P.4 — A Written Covenant

. The prophet Habakkuk was told to write the vision plainly — not as an exercise in formality, but because a vision that is not written down becomes a vision that drifts. The people who read it should be able to run with it. That is the standard to which this <<def-manual,Manual>> is held.

. This <<def-manual,Manual>> is a <<def-covenant,covenant>>, not a contract — we chose the word deliberately, because it reflects the kind of relationships upon which this <<def-company,Company>> was built:
.. A contract binds parties through obligation and enforcement — if one side fails, the other seeks remedy.
.. A <<def-covenant,covenant>> binds parties through shared commitment and accountability — if one side struggles, the other comes alongside.

. This <<def-manual,Manual>> does not exist to create legal liability between the <<def-company,Company>> and its people. It exists to establish a shared standard — one that everyone who is part of this work voluntarily holds themselves to, because the mission is worth getting right.

. This <<def-manual,Manual>> carries distinct weight for each of its audiences:
.. when <<def-board,the Board>> approves it, they are affirming commitment;
.. when <<def-team-members,Team Members>> read it, they are encountering promises; and
.. when <<def-partners,Partners>> reference it, they are holding us to our word.

. We write it down because our word must be our bond.

[[P-5]]
==== § P.5 — To the Reader

. Thank you for reading this. This <<def-manual,Manual>> is substantial — that is by design. The vision it documents and the commitments it carries deserve thorough articulation, and we would rather be clear and complete than leave room for misunderstanding.

. If you are a <<def-board,Board>> member receiving this for the first time, you are not expected to absorb all of it at once:
.. Each <<def-board,Board>> member will receive a review guide identifying the articles most relevant to your role and area of oversight — start there.
// end p.4 — content full

[[P-5-1]]
===== § P.5.1 — How This Manual Is Organized

. This <<def-manual,Manual>> is divided into four Parts, four Appendices, and back matter. Each Part builds on the one before it:
.. *Part I — Identity* (Articles 1–2);
.. *Part II — Operations* (Articles 3–5);
.. *Part III — Business* (Articles 6–7); and
.. *Part IV — Direction* (Articles 8–11).

. A detailed description of each Part and its constituent Articles is set forth in <<P-7>>.

[[P-5-2]]
===== § P.5.2 — How to Reference This Document

. Every section in this <<def-manual,Manual>> is tagged with a citable identifier using the *§* symbol. For example, *§ P.1* refers to the Purpose section of this Preface, and *§ 2.3* refers to the third section of Article 2. These identifiers allow precise reference in <<def-board,Board>> resolutions, meeting minutes, and official correspondence.

. Terms defined in <<P-3>> carry their defined meaning wherever they appear in this <<def-manual,Manual>>. When you see "<<def-board,the Board>>," "<<def-company,the Company>>," "<<def-leadership,Leadership>>," "<<def-team-members,Team Members>>," or "<<def-partners,Partners>>," those words mean exactly what § P.3 says they mean.

[[P-5-3]]
===== § P.5.3 — What "Board Review Draft" Means

. This version of the <<def-manual,Manual>> is a Board Review Draft. It is submitted for review and feedback — it is not yet the governing standard described in <<P-2>>. It becomes that upon formal <<def-board,Board>> approval.

. During the review period, every <<def-board,Board>> member is encouraged to:
.. raise questions where the content is unclear;

.. flag concerns where the commitments require discussion; and
.. propose changes where improvement serves the mission.

. The goal is not ratification — it is genuine engagement. When <<def-board,the Board>> approves this <<def-manual,Manual>>, every member should be able to do so with full understanding and clear conviction.

[[P-6]]
==== § P.6 — What This Manual Does and Does Not Do

. This <<def-manual,Manual>> serves six purposes:
.. it defines who we are — identity grounded in Scripture, not in market positioning;
.. it articulates why we exist and what we are actually pursuing;
.. it establishes the values that govern every decision we make;
.. it documents how we operate — how things actually work, not how we wish they worked;
.. it aligns everyone around the same picture, so that <<def-board,the Board>>, <<def-leadership,Leadership>>, <<def-team-members,Team Members>>, and <<def-partners,Partners>> are reading from the same page; and
.. it creates accountability — not surveillance, but a standard everyone agreed to and can point back to when decisions need grounding.

. This <<def-manual,Manual>> does not:
.. replace Scripture — the Bible is the final authority for the <<def-company,Company>>, and this <<def-manual,Manual>> derives from Scripture, not supersedes it; where this <<def-manual,Manual>> and Scripture appear to conflict, Scripture governs;
.. substitute for prayer or the leading of the Holy Spirit — no governance document replaces discernment, and the people of this <<def-company,Company>> are expected to seek God in every decision, not simply consult a manual; and
.. provide rigid rules — the principles documented here are constant, but their application requires wisdom; this is a guide, not a cage, and <<def-leadership,Leadership>> and <<def-team-members,Team Members>> are entrusted with the judgment to apply these principles faithfully in situations this <<def-manual,Manual>> cannot anticipate.
// end p.6 — content full
// PAGE 7 — P.6(3), then P.7
. This <<def-manual,Manual>> is not static. We learn, we grow, we correct. The amendment procedures in Part IV exist because this <<def-manual,Manual>> must evolve as God reveals more and as the <<def-company,Company>> matures. What does not change is the foundation — the identity, mission, and values documented in Part I. Everything built on that foundation may be refined.

[[P-7]]
==== § P.7 — Manual Structure

. This <<def-manual,Manual>> comprises four Parts, eleven Articles, four Appendices, and back matter. The order is deliberate: identity first, then what we do, then how we sustain it, then where we are going. Each Part builds on the one before it.

. *Part I — Identity* (Articles 1–2) answers the question: _who is {company-short}?_
.. Article 1 defines the legal entity, brand, and organizational foundation.
.. Article 2 details the publishing house model — the way we engage with creators and the reason the <<def-company,Company>> is structured the way it is.

. *Part II — Operations* (Articles 3–5) answers the question: _what does {company-short} do?_
.. Article 3 documents the Bereshit technology stack, from the foundational layers through applications.
.. Article 4 covers the services we provide to creatives, businesses, and the body of Christ.
.. Article 5 identifies who we serve and where we draw the boundaries of that service.

. *Part III — Business* (Articles 6–7) answers the question: _how does {company-short} sustain itself?_
.. Article 6 covers revenue, economic philosophy, and financial governance.
.. Article 7 defines the core values — the non-negotiables that govern every decision and relationship within the <<def-company,Company>>.

. *Part IV — Direction* (Articles 8–11) answers the question: _where is {company-short} going?_
.. Article 8 contains the mission and vision.
.. Article 9 introduces the team and <<def-board,Board>> structure.
.. Article 10 maps the timeline from foundation through the Millennium.
// end p.7 — content full
// PAGE 8 — P.7(5)(c-d), P.7(6) appendices, P.8, P.8.1 starts
.. Article 11 brings everything together — complete synthesis, with summaries written for <<def-board,the Board>>, <<def-team-members,Team Members>>, and <<def-partners,Partners>> specifically.

. Four Appendices support the Articles as reference tools, so that the Articles themselves stay focused on substance:
.. Document Map;
.. Scripture Index;
.. Decision Frameworks; and
.. Anti-Patterns Catalog.

[[P-8]]
==== § P.8 — How to Use This Manual

. Each Article in this <<def-manual,Manual>> stands on its own. You do not need to read it cover to cover — though you are welcome to. What matters is that you know what applies to your role, and where to find the rest when you need it.

. Three Articles apply to everyone:
.. Article 11 (The Whole Thing) gives you the complete picture in one place;
.. Article 7 (Core Values) defines the non-negotiables — no one is exempt; and
.. Article 8 (Mission and Vision) explains why we build what we build.

. Start with those three. The Glossary exists for whenever a term is unfamiliar — use it. What follows in §§ P.8.1 through P.8.4 is tailored to your role.

===== § P.8.1 — <<def-board,Board>> Members

. You carry governance oversight and strategic accountability for this <<def-company,Company>>. Article 11 (The Whole Thing) gives you the complete synthesis — start there. Then study:
.. Article 7 (Core Values) — the standards against which you will evaluate every decision;
.. Article 8 (Mission and Vision) — why we build what we build; and
.. Article 9 (The Team) — who does what and how the organization is structured.

. If you are joining this work in any capacity — employee, contractor, or volunteer — three Articles orient you:

[[P-9]]
==== § P.9 — Manual Maintenance

. A vision that drifts is worse than a vision that was never written. This <<def-manual,Manual>> stays current because we built the discipline to keep it current.

. Every change is tracked in version control (Git). Each Article carries its own version number and revision date. Nothing is changed without a record — because accountability requires a trail.

. Four events trigger review:
.. *Annual Review* — full review for currency, accuracy, and alignment with the <<def-company,Company>>'s mission;
.. *Phase Reviews* — when {company-short} transitions between development phases, affected Articles are updated to reflect new realities;
.. *Section Updates* — when operational changes, <<def-leadership,Leadership>> decisions, or <<def-board,Board>> resolutions require revision; and
.. *Content Additions* — when the <<def-company,Company>> grows into areas this <<def-manual,Manual>> does not yet address.

. Substantive changes require <<def-board,Board>> approval. Editorial corrections and clarifications may be made by the document maintainer and noted in the revision history.

. The amendment procedures in Part IV govern the process — it is transparent, and the record is permanent.

[[P-10]]
==== § P.10 — A Word Before Reading

. This <<def-manual,Manual>> documents human effort in service of divine vision. It is fallible. It is incomplete. It is subject to revision. We do not claim perfection — we claim faithfulness. We have done our best to articulate what God has shown us, knowing that our understanding will deepen over time.

. Scripture is the final authority for this <<def-company,Company>>. Where this <<def-manual,Manual>> and Scripture conflict, Scripture governs — without exception. Where this <<def-manual,Manual>> and the leading of the Holy Spirit conflict, follow the Spirit. We write to clarify, not to constrain.

. Some of what follows will convict. Some will challenge. Some will encourage. All of it is offered in good faith — ask the Lord what He wants you to see in it, and read with discernment.
// end p.11 — content full
// PAGE 12 — P.10(3-5), Psalm 127:1
. Hold this <<def-manual,Manual>> loosely. Hold Him firmly. This document is a tool, not an idol — it serves the mission, and the mission does not serve the document. If something here needs to change, we will change it. The goal is faithfulness, not consistency for its own sake.

. We cannot build anything of eternal value apart from Him — _"Except the LORD build the house, they labour in vain that build it: except the LORD keep the city, the watchman waketh but in vain"_ (Psalm 127:1, KJV). This <<def-manual,Manual>> exists because He gave us something to steward. May we steward it well.

// end p.12
