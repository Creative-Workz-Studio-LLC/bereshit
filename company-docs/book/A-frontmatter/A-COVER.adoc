=== Eight Core Values

The following eight values constitute the binding operational principles of {company-name}. These values are not aspirational — they are operational commitments that govern every decision, partnership, and product of the Company.

. *Service to Community.* Community benefit is the primary measure of every product, service, and decision — not revenue, not growth, not market position.
. *Faithful Stewardship.* The Company builds within its means and on God's timeline — not on borrowed capital or market pressure.
. *Connective Tissue, Not Center.* The Company builds infrastructure that enables others to operate independently — not platforms that create dependency.
. *Teaching Through Making.* Every product is designed to transfer understanding, not create reliance. Documentation and education are core deliverables.
. *Accessible by Design.* Accessibility is a design constraint, not an accommodation. Non-technical stakeholders can understand every product the Company ships.
. *Trade and Mutual Support.* The Company operates on direct value exchange — no hidden monetization, no data harvesting, no lock-in.
. *Reverence for the Creator.* Design patterns originate from Scripture — they are architectural, not decorative. Genesis 1:1 is the foundation of the technology, not a label applied to it.
. *Creator Autonomy.{asterisk}* Creators retain full intellectual property rights over their work. The Company provides services — it does not acquire ownership stakes in what creators produce.

No decision, product, or partnership of {company-name} may proceed in conflict with the values enumerated above. Amendments to core values require unanimous consent of the Board of Directors, as further specified in Article 7 of this Manual.
