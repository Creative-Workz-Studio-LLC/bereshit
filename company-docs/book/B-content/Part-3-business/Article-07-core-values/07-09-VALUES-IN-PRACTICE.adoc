== icon:cogs[] Practical Scenarios

=== icon:handshake[] Scenario 1: Profitable But Misaligned Partnership

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-circle[] Values in Action
****
*Situation:*

A potential partner offers significant revenue but their values don't align with {company-short} values.

*Values Test:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Service to Community: Would this serve Kingdom community?
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Reverence for Creator: Does this honor God?
* icon:check-circle[role=green] Others: May pass

*Decision:*

*DECLINE.* Failing even one value test means the opportunity is not for {company-short}, regardless of revenue potential.

*Reasoning:*

Values aren't for sale. Short-term revenue from misalignment damages long-term mission. Trust the process.
****

=== icon:award[] Scenario 2: Creator Wants Quality-Compromising Decision

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-circle[] Values in Action
****
*Situation:*

A creative partner wants to publish work that doesn't meet {company-short} quality standards.

*Values Test:*

* icon:check-circle[role=green] Creator Autonomy: Their work, their decision
* icon:question-circle[role=orange] Service to Community: Does low-quality work serve?
* icon:question-circle[role=orange] Teaching: Does this set good example?

*Decision:*

*ADVISE, RESPECT, POSSIBLY SEPARATE.* Advise honestly about quality concerns. Ultimately respect creator decision. If quality is too low for {company-short} association, end partnership graciously — but work stays with creator.

*Reasoning:*

Autonomy is protected; {company-short} can advise and can separate, but can't control.
****

=== icon:chart-line[] Scenario 3: Growth Opportunity Requiring Complexity

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-circle[] Values in Action
****
*Situation:*

A growth opportunity requires building something more complex than regular people can understand.

*Values Test:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Accessible by Design: Complex, expert-only
* icon:question-circle[role=orange] Teaching Through Making: Can complexity still teach?
* icon:question-circle[role=orange] Service to Community: Does this serve regular people?

*Decision:*

*FIND ACCESSIBLE PATH OR DECLINE.* Look for ways to make it accessible. If accessibility is impossible, this may not be for {company-short}. Some legitimate work is expert-only; {company-short} focus is accessibility.

*Reasoning:*

Accessibility is core to {company-short} identity. Growth at expense of accessibility isn't {company-short} growth.
****

=== icon:hand-holding-heart[] Scenario 4: Partner Struggling Financially

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-circle[] Values in Action
****
*Situation:*

A valued, aligned partner is struggling financially and asking for accommodation.

*Values Test:*

* icon:check-circle[role=green] Service to Community: Serving partner in difficulty
* icon:check-circle[role=green] Trade and Mutual Support: This is what mutual support means
* icon:question-circle[role=orange] Faithful Stewardship: Must balance with {company-short} sustainability

*Decision:*

*SUPPORT WITHIN SUSTAINABILITY.* Find ways to accommodate that serve the partner without compromising {company-short} sustainability. Use trade, adjusted terms, patience.

*Reasoning:*

Mutual support means helping through difficulty. But unsustainable help serves no one long-term.
****

'''
