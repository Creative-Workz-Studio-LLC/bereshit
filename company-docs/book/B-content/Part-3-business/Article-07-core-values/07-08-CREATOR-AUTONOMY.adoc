== icon:ban[] Anti-Patterns

=== icon:hand-holding-usd[] Ownership Taking

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Ownership Taking
****
*What It Is:*

Acquiring ownership of creator's work — whether through contract terms, work-for-hire structures, or gradual accumulation of rights.

*Why It Violates Autonomy:*

Taking ownership takes control. It transfers the fruit of labor from creator to publisher. It inverts the proper relationship.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Contract clauses transferring ownership
* icon:times-circle[role=red] "We need to own this for protection"
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Gradual accumulation of rights
* icon:times-circle[role=red] IP as payment for services
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Work-for-hire as default
****

=== icon:lock[] Control Without Ownership

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Control Without Ownership
****
*What It Is:*

Maintaining effective control over work without legal ownership — through exclusive agreements, platform lock-in, or information asymmetry.

*Why It Violates Autonomy:*

Control without ownership is ownership in effect. Creator may technically own, but can't actually exercise ownership.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Exclusive agreements that prevent alternative action
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Platform dependencies that lock in
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Information hoarded that creator needs
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Processes that require {company-short} involvement
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Contracts with onerous terms for exercise of rights
****

=== icon:link[] Dependency Creation

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Dependency Creation
****
*What It Is:*

Making creators dependent on {company-short} — whether through knowledge withholding, relationship gatekeeping, or infrastructure lock-in.

*Why It Violates Autonomy:*

True autonomy includes ability to function independently. Dependency undermines autonomy even when ownership is technically retained.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Knowledge not transferred
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Relationships mediated through {company-short}
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Infrastructure that only works with {company-short}
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Skills not developed in partner
* icon:times-circle[role=red] "They can't do this without us"
****

'''
