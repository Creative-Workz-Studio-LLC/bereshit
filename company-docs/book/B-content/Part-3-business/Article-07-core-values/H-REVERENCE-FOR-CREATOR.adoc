== icon:ban[] Anti-Patterns

=== icon:globe[] Secular Defaults

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Secular Defaults
****
*What It Is:*

Adopting worldly patterns and conventions without examination — doing things the way the world does because that's "how it's done."

*Why It Violates Reverence:*

Secular defaults may contradict biblical patterns. Adopting them uncritically fails to honor God as source of wisdom. It conforms to the world rather than transforms.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] "That's industry standard"
* icon:times-circle[role=red] No biblical foundation sought
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Worldly success metrics adopted
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Patterns chosen for convention, not conviction
* icon:times-circle[role=red] "That's how everyone does it"
****

=== icon:random[] Arbitrary Choices

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Arbitrary Choices
****
*What It Is:*

Making foundational choices without purpose or grounding — random selections rather than intentional decisions rooted in principle.

*Why It Violates Reverence:*

Arbitrary choices waste the opportunity to honor God through intentional design. They miss the chance to embed biblical patterns. They treat creation carelessly.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] "It doesn't matter"
* icon:times-circle[role=red] No reasoning behind choices
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Inconsistent foundations
* icon:times-circle[role=red] No pattern or purpose visible
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Random rather than intentional
****

=== icon:divide[] Compartmentalization

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Compartmentalization
****
*What It Is:*

Separating "spiritual" work from "secular" work — treating faith as relevant only in certain contexts while ignoring it in technical or business decisions.

*Why It Violates Reverence:*

"Whatsoever ye do" includes all work. There is no secular-sacred divide. Faith that doesn't affect Tuesday afternoon work isn't affecting life.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] "Faith doesn't apply here"
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Technical decisions made without values consideration
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Business decisions divorced from biblical principles
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Different standards in different contexts
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Faith reserved for "religious" activities
****

'''
