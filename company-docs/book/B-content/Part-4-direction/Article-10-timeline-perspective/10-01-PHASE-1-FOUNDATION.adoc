== icon:times-circle[] What We're NOT Doing

=== icon:chart-line[] Chasing Growth

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Growth Obsession
****
*What It Is:*

Pursuing growth metrics (users, revenue, team size) as primary goals rather than building solid foundations that enable sustainable growth.

*Why It's Wrong for Phase 1:*

Growth built on weak foundations collapses. Phase 1 exists to build foundations strong enough to support future growth. Chasing growth now undermines everything later.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Decisions justified by "it will help us grow"
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Metrics focus on size rather than solidity
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Impatience with the pace of foundation-building
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Cutting corners to show progress
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Comparing ourselves to growth-focused companies
****

=== icon:hand-holding-usd[] Taking Compromising Investment

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Compromising Money
****
*What It Is:*

Accepting funding, partnerships, or revenue that comes with strings attached — requirements that conflict with values or mission.

*Why It's Wrong for Phase 1:*

Mission clarity is foundational. If we compromise on mission for funding in Phase 1, we've corrupted the very thing we're building. Better to be slow and pure than fast and compromised.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] "We'll just take this for now and stay true later"
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Rationalizing small compromises
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Financial pressure driving mission-adjacent decisions
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Investors/partners with incompatible values
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Revenue that requires values violations
****

=== icon:user-plus[] Hiring Before Revenue

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Premature Team Growth
****
*What It Is:*

Bringing people onto the team before there's sustainable capacity to compensate them fairly and provide meaningful work.

*Why It's Wrong for Phase 1:*

People deserve to be paid. Promising what we can't deliver harms people. Team growth must follow revenue growth, not precede it.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] "We'll figure out compensation later"
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Relying on passion to replace fair payment
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Taking on commitments we can't sustain
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Growing team to look legitimate
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Burnout from overcommitment
****

=== icon:bullhorn[] Marketing Before Product

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Premature Promotion
****
*What It Is:*

Building audience or generating demand before having products or services to deliver.

*Why It's Wrong for Phase 1:*

Marketing creates expectations. Unmet expectations destroy trust. Better to build quietly and launch when ready than promote loudly and disappoint.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Social media presence exceeds substance
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Announcements without delivery capability
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Audience building before product building
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Hype over substance
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Reputation built on promises, not delivery
****

=== icon:bolt[] Rushing Foundations

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Speed Over Substance
****
*What It Is:*

Moving fast at the expense of thoroughness — taking shortcuts that will create technical, relational, or organizational debt.

*Why It's Wrong for Phase 1:*

Foundations determine what can be built on them. Rushed foundations limit future potential and often require costly reconstruction. Phase 1 is precisely when going slow is fastest.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] "We'll fix it later"
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Skipping documentation to save time
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Making decisions without proper consideration
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Technical debt accumulating without plan
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Quality sacrificed for speed
****

4. icon:check-circle[role=orange] *Decide* — Choose what serves foundations

* **"Should we bring on this partner?"** — Can we sustain the commitment?
* **"Should we pursue this opportunity?"** — Is now the right time, or is it a Phase 2/3 opportunity?
****
