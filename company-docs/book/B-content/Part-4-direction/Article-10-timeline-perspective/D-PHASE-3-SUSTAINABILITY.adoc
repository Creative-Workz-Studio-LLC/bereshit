== icon:exclamation-triangle[] Sustainability Risks

=== icon:trophy[] Success Complacency

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Mission Drift
****
*What It Is:*

Allowing success to shift focus from mission to maintenance — preserving what exists rather than pursuing what's needed.

*Why It's Dangerous:*

{company-short} exists to serve a purpose beyond itself. Sustainability that becomes self-perpetuation loses the point. The mission is preparing Kingdom infrastructure, not maintaining an organization.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Risk-aversion dominating decisions
* icon:times-circle[role=red] "Don't rock the boat" thinking
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Phase 4 preparation delayed for current comfort
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Mission language without mission urgency
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Growth for growth's sake
****

=== icon:expand-alt[] Unsustainable Scaling

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Growing Beyond Capacity
****
*What It Is:*

Expanding faster than capacity can support — taking on more than can be sustained well.

*Why It's Dangerous:*

Overextension collapses. Growing beyond capacity creates fragility that undermines everything built. Sustainable means actually sustainable, not stretched to breaking.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Quality declining with growth
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Team burnout increasing
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Commitments exceeding capacity
* icon:times-circle[role=red] "We'll figure it out" replacing "We can sustain this"
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Revenue requiring unsustainable effort
****

=== icon:user-tie[] Institutional Capture

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Becoming What We Resisted
****
*What It Is:*

Evolving into the kind of institution {company-short} was designed to avoid — bureaucratic, self-serving, extractive.

*Why It's Dangerous:*

Many organizations start with mission clarity and drift into institutional preservation. Phase 3 is when this danger is greatest — success provides resources to insulate from accountability.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Processes serving the organization, not mission
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Leadership becoming entrenched
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Values documented but not practiced
* icon:times-circle[role=red] "That's how we've always done it" blocking improvement
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Mission statements that no one lives
****

'''
