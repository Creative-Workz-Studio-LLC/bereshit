[cols="^1,2,4",options="header",frame=all,grid=all]
|===
|icon:tag[] |Aspect |Application

|icon:crown[role=blue]
|*Reality*
|God controls timing; we don't

|icon:hands[role=green]
|*Response*
|Trust His timing, even when it confuses us

|icon:clock[role=purple]
|*Implication*
|Phase transitions happen when He says, not when we push

|icon:praying-hands[role=orange]

* Trust that delays have purpose even when unclear

== icon:exclamation-triangle[] Timeline Anti-Patterns

=== icon:bolt[] Forcing Timing

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Pushing Ahead of God
****
*What It Is:*

Trying to make things happen before God's timing — pushing transitions, forcing growth, running ahead of provision.

*Why It's Wrong:*

Running ahead of God is disobedience disguised as initiative. Forced timing produces fragile results that collapse under pressure.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Anxiety driving decisions
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Ignoring counsel that says "wait"
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Rationalizing lack of confirmation
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Cutting corners to speed progress
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Feeling like you must make things happen
****

=== icon:hourglass-end[] Missing Timing

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Lagging Behind God
****
*What It Is:*

Failing to move when God says move — hesitating, delaying, letting fear or comfort prevent obedience.

*Why It's Wrong:*

God opens doors that don't stay open forever. Comfort can become a trap. Waiting too long is as much disobedience as moving too soon.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Moving goalposts on readiness
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Fear disguised as wisdom
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Comfort exceeding calling
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Always finding "one more thing" before moving
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Ignoring confirmation because it's uncomfortable
****

=== icon:exchange-alt[] Wrong Phase Work

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Misaligned Priorities
****
*What It Is:*

Doing Phase 2 work in Phase 1, or Phase 1 work in Phase 3 — priorities that don't match the current phase.

*Why It's Wrong:*

Each phase has appropriate work. Doing wrong-phase work either rushes unprepared or regresses from progress. It wastes resources and creates confusion.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Growth initiatives during foundation phase
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Foundation rework during scaling phase
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Sustainability focus during transition
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Priorities that don't match stated phase
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Confusion about what phase you're in
****

'''
