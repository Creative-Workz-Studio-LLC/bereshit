== icon:ban[] Anti-Patterns

=== icon:landmark[] Empire Building

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Empire Building
****
*What It Is:*

Building personal fiefdoms within {company-short} — accumulating influence, control, or territory for self rather than serving the mission.

*Why It Violates Culture:*

{company-short} is connective tissue, not center. Empire building reverses the proper relationship — people serve the empire instead of serving the mission.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Hoarding information or relationships
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Resisting others' involvement in "my area"
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Measuring success by personal influence
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Building dependencies on self rather than systems
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Taking credit; deflecting blame
****

=== icon:money-bill-alt[] Extraction Mindset

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Extraction
****
*What It Is:*

Approaching {company-short} primarily to extract value — taking more than giving, using relationships for personal benefit.

*Why It Violates Culture:*

Service gives; extraction takes. {company-short} culture is built on service, not extraction.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Calculating personal benefit in every decision
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Minimal contribution, maximum expectation
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Relationships valued by what they provide
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Reluctance to help when benefit unclear
* icon:times-circle[role=red] "What's in it for me?" as primary question
****

=== icon:bolt[] Short-Term Thinking

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Short-Term Focus
****
*What It Is:*

Optimizing for immediate results at the expense of long-term mission — quick wins over sustainable building.

*Why It Violates Culture:*

{company-short} builds for the Millennium. Short-term thinking sacrifices eternal purpose for temporary gains.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Rushing foundations to show progress
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Compromising quality for speed
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Decisions that help now but hurt later
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Impatience with proper process
* icon:times-circle[role=red] "Just this once" compromises
****

=== icon:handshake-alt-slash[] Values Compromise

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Values Compromise
****
*What It Is:*

Bending or breaking non-negotiable values for convenience, opportunity, or pressure.

*Why It Violates Culture:*

Values define who we are. Compromising them destroys {company-short}'s foundation and witness.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] "Just this once won't hurt"
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Rationalizing violations as necessary
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Pressure to compromise for revenue
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Looking for loopholes in principles
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Different standards for different situations
****

== icon:question-circle[] The Culture Test

=== icon:clipboard-check[] Decision Framework

When evaluating behaviors, decisions, or practices, ask:

[cols="^1,4,2",options="header",frame=all,grid=all]
|===
|icon:tag[] |Question |Good Answer

|icon:users[role=blue]
|*Does this build trust or erode it?*
|Builds trust among team

|icon:hands-helping[role=green]
|*Does this serve others or serve self?*
|Serves team and mission

|icon:compass[role=purple]
|*Does this align with our values?*
|Clearly aligned with values

|icon:clock[role=orange]
|*Does this think long-term or short-term?*
|Considers long-term impact

|icon:book[role=gold]
|*Would I document this openly?*
|Yes, without hesitation

|===

=== icon:balance-scale[] How to Apply This Test

[sidebar]
.icon:lightbulb[] Practical Application
****
*Before making decisions:*

1. icon:pause-circle[role=blue] *Pause* — Don't rush; consider implications
2. icon:question-circle[role=green] *Question* — Run through the culture test
3. icon:users[role=purple] *Consider* — How would this affect others?
4. icon:check-circle[role=orange] *Decide* — Choose what builds culture

*When observing behaviors:*

1. icon:eye[role=blue] *Notice* — Observe what's happening
2. icon:balance-scale[role=green] *Evaluate* — Against cultural standards
3. icon:comments[role=purple] *Address* — If needed, with grace and truth
4. icon:sync[role=orange] *Follow Up* — Ensure resolution
****
