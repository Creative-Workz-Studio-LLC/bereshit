[.lead]
This document describes the *network structure* around {company-short} — the creative partners, business partners, trade network members, and community connections that form the broader ecosystem.

[sidebar]
.icon:star[] Why This Document Matters
****
{company-short} isn't an island. Beyond the core team, a network of partners and connections enables the mission. Understanding this network structure helps everyone see *how they fit* in the larger picture.
****

== icon:file-alt[] Document Purpose

[quote, 1 Corinthians 12:14 (KJV)]
____
For the body is not one member, but many.
____

[NOTE]
.icon:lightbulb[] Core Understanding
====
*Connective Tissue, Not Center:* {company-short} operates as connective tissue in a larger network — enabling, connecting, and serving rather than controlling. The network is the Body; we help it function.
====

'''
