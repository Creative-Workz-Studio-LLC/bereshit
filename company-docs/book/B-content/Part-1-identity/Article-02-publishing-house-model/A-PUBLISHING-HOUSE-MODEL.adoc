== icon:compass[] Chapter Purpose

[.lead]
This chapter establishes and explains the *Publishing House Model* that defines how {company-short} operates as a business. While <<../01-entity-overview/01-00-ENTITY-OVERVIEW.adoc#,Chapter 01>> established *who* {company-short} is, this chapter explains *how* {company-short} operates in the marketplace.

[sidebar]
.icon:question-circle[] Why This Chapter Matters
****
Understanding the Publishing House Model is essential for:

* icon:copyright[] *Creator Protection* — Our IP retention policy is non-negotiable
* icon:balance-scale[] *Fair Partnerships* — Services for revenue share, not ownership extraction
* icon:crown[] *Kingdom Alignment* — Operating as servants, not extractors
* icon:shield-alt[] *Preparation* — Building resilient infrastructure for uncertain times
****

[IMPORTANT]
.icon:building[] Identity in Operation
====
{company-short} is a *publishing house*—not a traditional tech company, not a studio that only makes its own products, but a publisher that serves creatives while also creating original works. This distinction shapes everything about how we operate.
====

'''

== icon:scroll[] Key Principles Summary

[.lead]
Before diving into details, understand these non-negotiable principles that govern all {company-short} operations.

=== icon:copyright[] Principle 1: Creators Keep Their IP

[IMPORTANT]
.icon:shield-alt[] Non-Negotiable
====
{company-short} *never* takes intellectual property rights from creators. This is the foundational principle of our publishing model. When you partner with {company-short}, you retain full ownership of what you create.
====

=== icon:sliders-h[] Principle 2: Autonomy Through Choice

[.lead]
Creators choose their level of support and corresponding revenue split:

* icon:arrow-up[] More {company-short} support = higher {company-short} share
* icon:arrow-down[] Less {company-short} support = creator keeps more
* icon:user-cog[] Creator decides based on their needs

=== icon:hands-helping[] Principle 3: Service, Not Extraction

[.lead]
{company-short} exists to serve creators and the Kingdom, not to extract maximum value:

* icon:server[] Infrastructure serves creators
* icon:handshake[] Relationships are partnerships, not transactions
* icon:crown[] Success is measured by Kingdom impact, not just profit

=== icon:shield-alt[] Principle 4: Preparation, Not Fear

[.lead]
The model prepares for economic disruption without being driven by fear:

* icon:stream[] Multiple revenue streams create resilience
* icon:exchange-alt[] Trade networks reduce monetary dependency
* icon:ban[] Mark-free options available when needed

'''
