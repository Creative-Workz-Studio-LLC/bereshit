== icon:compass[] Document Purpose

[.lead]
This document establishes how {company-short} works with *external creatives*—individuals and teams who partner with us to publish their work. This is *Pillar One* of the publishing house model and often the most visible aspect of {company-short} operations.

[sidebar]
.icon:question-circle[] Why This Document Matters
****
Understanding how we serve external creatives is essential for:

* icon:shield-alt[] *Protecting Creators* — Our commitment to IP retention defines every relationship
* icon:handshake[] *Building Trust* — Clear expectations create lasting partnerships
* icon:balance-scale[] *Fair Exchange* — Services for revenue share, not ownership extraction
* icon:heart[] *Kingdom Alignment* — Honoring how God gifts His people
****

[IMPORTANT]
.icon:crown[] The Core Principle
====
*The Non-Negotiable:* Creators keep their intellectual property. This is non-negotiable and fundamental to everything in this document.
====

'''

== icon:shield-alt[] The Non-Negotiable: IP Retention

=== icon:gavel[] The Principle

[.lead]
*{company-short} does not take intellectual property rights from creators.*

When a creative partners with {company-short} to publish their work:

[cols="^1,4",options="header",frame=all,grid=all]
|===
|icon:check[] |What Creators Receive

|icon:file-signature[role=blue]
|The creator *retains full ownership* of their intellectual property

|icon:coins[role=gold]
|{company-short} provides services in exchange for a *revenue share*

|icon:user-shield[role=green]
|The creator maintains *autonomy* over their creation

|icon:door-open[role=purple]
|The creator can *leave the partnership* with their IP intact
|===

=== icon:question[] Why This Is Non-Negotiable

[cols="^1,2,4",options="header",frame=all,grid=all]
|===
|icon:tag[] |Reason |Explanation

|icon:crown[role=gold]
|*Kingdom Alignment*
|Creators received gifts from God; extracting ownership violates stewardship principles

|icon:fist-raised[role=blue]
|*Creator Empowerment*
|Creators can build their own legacy, not just contribute to {company-short}'s portfolio

|icon:handshake[role=green]
|*Trust Foundation*
|IP retention establishes trust that enables genuine partnership

|icon:star[role=purple]
|*Differentiation*
|This distinguishes {company-short} from extractive publishers

|icon:heart[role=red]
|*Long-term Relationships*
|Creators stay because they want to, not because they're locked in
|===

=== icon:clipboard-check[] What IP Retention Means

[cols="^1,2,3,3",options="header",frame=all,grid=all]
|===
|icon:tag[] |Aspect |What It Means |What It Does NOT Mean

|icon:copyright[role=blue]
|*Ownership*
|Creator owns all rights to their work
|{company-short} gains no ownership stake

|icon:sliders-h[role=green]
|*Control*
|Creator decides how work is used
|{company-short} cannot use work without permission

|icon:edit[role=purple]
|*Modifications*
|Creator controls changes to work
|{company-short} cannot alter work unilaterally

|icon:door-open[role=gold]
|*Exit*
|Creator can end partnership with IP intact
|{company-short} cannot hold IP hostage

|icon:forward[role=teal]
|*Future Works*
|No automatic claims on future creations
|Each work is separately considered
|===

|icon:hand-point-right[role=red]

|icon:user-slash[role=red]

|We maintain quality and values standards

|icon:gavel[role=red]

|===
