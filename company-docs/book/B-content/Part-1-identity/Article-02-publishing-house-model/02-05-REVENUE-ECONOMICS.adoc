== icon:percentage[] Revenue Split Model

=== icon:lightbulb[] The Core Concept

[.lead]
Revenue splits with external creatives vary based on *support level*—creators choose their level of {company-short} involvement and corresponding revenue share.

[IMPORTANT]
.icon:user-cog[] Creator Choice
====
*The creator chooses their support level and corresponding revenue split.* More {company-short} support means higher {company-short} share; less support means creator keeps more. *The creator decides.*
====

=== icon:layer-group[] Support Levels

==== icon:hands-helping[] Full Support

[sidebar]
.icon:star[] Comprehensive Partnership
****
*What {company-short} Provides:*

* icon:bullhorn[] Complete marketing strategy and execution
* icon:book-open[] Full publication production and preparation
* icon:comments[] Consultation and strategic guidance
* icon:truck[] Distribution across all available channels
* icon:server[] Access to all infrastructure and tools
* icon:headset[] Ongoing support and relationship management

*Revenue Split:* icon:arrow-up[] Higher {company-short} share (creator receives lower %)

*Best For:* Creators who want comprehensive support and are willing to share more revenue for it
****

==== icon:hand-holding-heart[] Moderate Support

[sidebar]
.icon:check-circle[] Balanced Partnership
****
*What {company-short} Provides:*

* icon:bullhorn[] Marketing support (strategy or execution, not both)
* icon:book-open[] Publication assistance with creator involvement
* icon:comments[] Periodic consultation
* icon:truck[] Distribution through primary channels
* icon:server[] Access to standard infrastructure
* icon:headset[] Regular check-ins and support

*Revenue Split:* icon:arrows-alt-h[] Medium {company-short} share (balanced split)

*Best For:* Creators who want significant help but can handle some aspects themselves
****

==== icon:feather[] Light Support

[sidebar]
.icon:leaf[] Lean Partnership
****
*What {company-short} Provides:*

* icon:bullhorn[] Marketing guidance (creator executes)
* icon:book-open[] Publication review and quality assurance
* icon:comments[] Available consultation when needed
* icon:truck[] Distribution access
* icon:server[] Infrastructure access
* icon:headset[] Support on request

*Revenue Split:* icon:arrow-down[] Lower {company-short} share (creator keeps more %)

*Best For:* Experienced creators who need less hands-on help
****

==== icon:plug[] Infrastructure Only

[sidebar]
.icon:tools[] Platform Access
****
*What {company-short} Provides:*

* icon:server[] Access to {company-short} platforms and tools
* icon:truck[] Distribution channel access
* icon:headset[] Basic support for technical issues
* icon:network-wired[] Network access

*Revenue Split:* icon:percentage[] Minimal {company-short} share (creator keeps majority %)

*Best For:* Established creators who primarily need infrastructure access
****
