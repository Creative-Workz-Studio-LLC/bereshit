== icon:bullseye[] Chapter Purpose

This chapter establishes the *foundational identity* of Creative Workz Studio LLC. Before understanding what we do, how we operate, or where we're going, stakeholders must understand *who we are* at the most fundamental level.

[NOTE]
====
This chapter answers the question: **"What IS Creative Workz Studio LLC?"**

Subsequent chapters build upon this foundation to address what we do (Operations), how we sustain ourselves (Business), and where we're headed (Direction).
====

=== Chapter Scope

This chapter covers:

* *Legal existence* — How CWS exists as a legal entity
* *Brand identity* — What our name means and how it's used
* *Organizational identity* — Our primary and secondary roles
* *Relational identity* — How we connect to other entities
* *Technical identity* — How our work is organized
* *Spiritual identity* — The theological foundation underlying everything
