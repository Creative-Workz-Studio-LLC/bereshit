=== icon:times-circle[] Not Aligned: Values Incompatibility

[sidebar]
.icon:heart-broken[] Values Incompatibility Profile
****
*Who They Are:*

Individuals or organizations whose values fundamentally conflict with Kingdom purposes.

*Examples:*

* Work that promotes explicit sin as good
* Organizations built on deception or exploitation
* Content that mocks or attacks faith
* Ventures designed to harm rather than help

*Why We Don't Serve:*

Partnership requires shared foundation. Without values alignment, "partnership" becomes mere transaction at best, corruption at worst.

*How We Respond:*

Honest, gracious decline. We don't pretend alignment exists when it doesn't. We wish well and part ways.
****

=== icon:clock[] Not Ready: Timing and Preparation

[sidebar]
.icon:hourglass-half[] Timing and Readiness Profile
****
*Who They Are:*

Individuals or organizations not yet in position to benefit from {company-short} partnership.

*Examples:*

* Creative work not yet at publishable quality
* Organizations without basic operational stability
* Individuals not clear on their own vision
* Situations where {company-short} help isn't what's actually needed

*Why We Don't Serve (Yet):*

Premature partnership serves no one. If {company-short} engagement won't actually help, proceeding harms both parties.

*How We Respond:*

Honest assessment with kindness. Specific feedback where helpful. Door open for future when readiness develops.
****

=== icon:user-slash[] Not Honest: Integrity Failures

[sidebar]
.icon:mask[] Integrity Failure Profile
****
*Who They Are:*

Those who misrepresent themselves, their work, or their intentions.

*Examples:*

* Misrepresenting work quality or stage
* Hidden agendas that contradict stated purposes
* Exploitative intent disguised as partnership
* Patterns of deception in dealings

*Why We Don't Serve:*

Partnership requires trust. Deception destroys the foundation that makes partnership possible.

*How We Respond:*

Direct, truthful identification of the issue. Clear boundary. Not punitive, but protective — of {company-short}, other partners, and ultimately of the dishonest party who needs correction, not enablement.
****
