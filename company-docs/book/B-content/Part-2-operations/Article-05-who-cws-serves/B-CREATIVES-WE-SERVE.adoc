This document defines the *creative individuals* {company-short} exists to serve — artists, developers, writers, musicians, and makers of all kinds. It establishes who they are, what they need, what alignment looks like, and how {company-short} serves them through the publishing house model.

.icon:star[] Why This Document Matters

Understanding *who* they are, *what* they need, and *how* we serve them shapes everything from service design to partnership terms. == icon:signal[] Creative Types by Career Stage

=== icon:seedling[] Emerging Creatives

[sidebar]
.icon:user-plus[] Emerging Creative Profile
****
*Who They Are:*

* Early in creative journey
* Developing craft and finding voice
* Building initial audience
* Often balancing creative work with other employment

*What They Need:*

* Guidance and mentorship
* Infrastructure they couldn't build alone
* Patience and development time
* Affordable entry points
* Community and encouragement

*{company-short} Approach:*

Full support available with corresponding revenue split. We invest in potential, not just proven success. Emerging creatives may need more support but can grow into significant partners.
****

=== icon:user-check[] Established Creatives

[sidebar]
.icon:user-cog[] Established Creative Profile
****
*Who They Are:*

* Track record of completed work
* Established audience, even if modest
* Clear creative identity and voice
* May have existing distribution but seeking expansion

*What They Need:*

* Amplification of existing reach
* Professional services to level up
* Strategic guidance for growth
* Infrastructure access
* Partnership, not micromanagement

*{company-short} Approach:*

Flexible support levels — some want full service, others want light touch. Established creatives know what they need; we provide it.
****

=== icon:award[] Veteran Creatives

[sidebar]
.icon:star[] Veteran Creative Profile
****
*Who They Are:*

* Significant body of work
* Established reputation
* May be seeking new direction
* Possibly burned by previous industry experiences

*What They Need:*

* Respect for experience
* Infrastructure without interference
* Fair terms after industry exploitation
* Freedom with support available
* Legacy considerations

*{company-short} Approach:*

Infrastructure-only to moderate support often preferred. These creatives know their craft; they need access and fairness, not hand-holding.
****
