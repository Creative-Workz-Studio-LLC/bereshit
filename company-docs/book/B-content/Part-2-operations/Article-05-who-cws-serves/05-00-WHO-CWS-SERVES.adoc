[.lead]
This chapter establishes *who {company-short} serves* — the specific audiences we exist to support, why we serve them, and what alignment looks like for each. While <<../04-what-cws-provides/04-00-WHAT-CWS-PROVIDES.adoc#,Chapter 04>> explained _what_ we provide, this chapter answers _to whom_.

[sidebar]
.icon:star[] Why This Chapter Matters
****
Knowing *who* we serve shapes everything — from the products we build to the services we offer to the relationships we prioritize. {company-short} doesn't serve everyone. We serve specific audiences aligned with Kingdom purposes. This intentionality enables *depth over breadth* — true partnership over mere transaction. Clear audience definition protects against mission drift and ensures resources flow to those we're called to serve.
****
