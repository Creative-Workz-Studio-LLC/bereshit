[.lead]
This chapter documents *what services {company-short} provides*—the ways we serve our three primary audiences beyond the products we build. While <<../03-what-cws-builds/03-00-WHAT-CWS-BUILDS.adoc#,Chapter 03>> established what we build, this chapter explains how we serve through services, support, and resources.

[sidebar]
.icon:star[] Why This Chapter Matters
****
Building great technology isn't enough. The body of Christ needs more than tools—they need *service, teaching, and community*. This chapter defines how {company-short} delivers value beyond products: equipping creatives, strengthening businesses, and preparing the church for what's coming. Our approach to service delivery matters as much as what we deliver.
****

== icon:bullseye[] Chapter Purpose

[NOTE]
.icon:lightbulb[] Products vs. Services
====
*Complementary Roles:* Chapter 03 covers what we *build* (products, infrastructure). This chapter covers what we *provide* (services, support, resources). Both serve the Kingdom; they're complementary.
====

'''

== icon:heart[] Service Philosophy Summary

Before diving into specific services, understand our foundational approach:

=== icon:hands-helping[] We Serve, Not Extract

[IMPORTANT]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Core Principle
====
{company-short} exists to serve the Kingdom, not to build an empire. Every service aims to strengthen recipients, not create dependency on {company-short}.
====

=== icon:list-ol[] Five Core Principles

[cols="^1,2,4",options="header",frame=all,grid=all]
|===
|icon:tag[] |Principle |What It Means

|icon:chalkboard-teacher[role=blue]
|*Teach, Don't Just Deliver*
|Every engagement transfers knowledge; clients understand more afterward

|icon:user-graduate[role=green]
|*Build Autonomy, Not Dependency*
|Success is clients who can function without us

|icon:balance-scale[role=purple]
|*Values Before Revenue*
|We decline work that doesn't align, even if profitable

|icon:users[role=orange]
|*Community Over Transaction*
|Engagements build relationships, not just deliverables

|icon:shield-alt[role=red]
|*Preparation Orientation*
|Everything makes recipients more prepared for what's coming
|===
