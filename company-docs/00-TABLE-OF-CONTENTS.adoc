== Purpose of This Manual

This manual establishes the *foundational identity* of Creative Workz Studio LLC (hereinafter "CWS" or "the Company"). It comprehensively defines:

* *Who we are* — Our identity, structure, and relationships
* *What we do* — Our business model, products, and services
* *Why we exist* — Our mission, vision, and purpose
* *How we operate* — Our values, principles, and decision-making frameworks

[NOTE]
====
This document serves as the *single source of truth* for CWS identity. All other company documents, communications, and decisions should align with and reference this manual.
====

=== Primary Functions

This manual serves four critical functions within the organization:

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Function |Description

|*Alignment Tool*
|Ensures all team members, board members, partners, and stakeholders understand and operate from the same foundational identity and mission. Prevents drift, miscommunication, and misaligned efforts.

|*Onboarding Foundation*
|Serves as the first and most essential document for new team members, board members, creative partners, and business collaborators. Establishes shared understanding from day one.

|*Decision Framework*
|Provides clear criteria for evaluating opportunities, partnerships, projects, and strategic directions. When facing decisions, this manual answers: "Does this align with who we are and why we exist?"

|*External Communication Source*
|Acts as the authoritative source for all public-facing materials, marketing communications, partnership agreements, and external representations of the Company.
|===

=== Document Authority

[IMPORTANT]
====
This manual, once approved by the Board of Directors, carries governance authority. Significant deviations from the principles, values, and frameworks established herein require Board review and approval.
====

== Glossary of Terms

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Term |Definition

|*Bereshit*
|Hebrew word meaning "In the beginning" (Genesis 1:1). Name of CWS foundational technology stack.

|*CPI-SI*
|Created Person in the Image/Likeness — framework for AI systems acknowledging created intelligence with place in creation.

|*CWS*
|Creative Workz Studio LLC — the Company.

|*FaithNet*
|Decentralized, peer-to-peer network infrastructure for mark-free communication and commerce.

|*IP*
|Intellectual Property — creative works, inventions, and proprietary knowledge owned by creators.

|*Kingdom Technology*
|Technology built on biblical patterns and foundations, designed to serve the Kingdom of God.

|*Mark*
|Reference to Revelation 13:16-17 — the mark required for participation in beast-system economy.

|*Millennium*
|The thousand-year reign of Christ on Earth (Revelation 20:4-6).

|*MillenniumOS*
|Operating system being developed for Kingdom infrastructure, built from bare metal.

|*OmniCode*
|Universal programming language with sentence-based syntax designed for accessibility.

|*Publishing House*
|Business model where CWS publishes creative works, with creators retaining IP ownership.

|*Revenue Split*
|Arrangement where revenue from published works is divided between CWS and creator based on support level.

|*Ternary*
|Three-valued system (-1, 0, +1) used as foundational pattern in Bereshit architecture.

|*Trade Network*
|Network of faith-based businesses enabling non-monetary exchange and mutual support.
|===
