This document establishes how {company-short} achieves *financial sustainability* — the ability to continue serving the Kingdom over the long term without compromising mission.

.icon:star[] Why This Document Matters
****
Sustainability is the bridge between *good intentions and lasting impact*. Without sustainable economics, even the best mission dies. This document ensures {company-short} structures finances for generational service — through tribulation and into the Millennium.
****

== icon:bullseye[] Document Purpose

[quote, Proverbs 21:5 (KJV)]
____
The thoughts of the diligent tend only to plenteousness; but of every one that is hasty only to want.
____

[NOTE]
.icon:lightbulb[] Core Principle

== icon:coins[] Cost Structure Philosophy

=== icon:feather[] Lean Operations

[IMPORTANT]
.icon:exclamation-circle[] Critical Distinction
====
*Lean doesn't mean cheap — it means efficient.* {company-short} maintains lean operations not by underpaying or under-resourcing, but by avoiding waste and focusing resources on mission-critical activities.
====

[sidebar]
.icon:balance-scale[] The Lean Balance
****
Lean isn't about deprivation. It's about *intentionality*. Every dollar should serve the mission. Waste dishonors stewardship.
****
