[.lead]
This chapter establishes the *complete business model* of {company-short} — how we generate revenue, sustain operations, grow responsibly, and prepare economically for Kingdom realities.

[sidebar]
.icon:star[] Why This Chapter Matters
****
The business model is where *mission meets mathematics*. Without sustainable economics, {company-short} cannot serve the Kingdom long-term. This chapter ensures every financial decision flows from Kingdom purposes — generating resources to sustain mission, not pursuing profit as an end in itself.
****

== icon:bullseye[] Chapter Purpose

[quote, Proverbs 27:23 (KJV)]
____
Be thou diligent to know the state of thy flocks, and look well to thy herds.
____

While <<../02-publishing-house-model/02-05-REVENUE-ECONOMICS.adoc#,Chapter 02-05>> introduced revenue splits with creative partners, this chapter provides the comprehensive economic framework.

[NOTE]
.icon:lightbulb[] Core Principle
====
*Economics Serve Mission:* {company-short}'s business model exists to sustain the mission, not the reverse. Every financial decision is evaluated against Kingdom purposes, not just profit maximization.
====
