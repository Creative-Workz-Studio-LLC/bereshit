== Chapter Purpose

This chapter establishes the *complete business model* of Creative Workz Studio — how we generate revenue, sustain operations, grow responsibly, and prepare economically for Kingdom realities. While <<../02-publishing-house-model/02-05-REVENUE-ECONOMICS.adoc#,Chapter 02-05>> introduced revenue splits with creative partners, this chapter provides the comprehensive economic framework.

[quote, Proverbs 27:23 (KJV)]
____
Be thou diligent to know the state of thy flocks, and look well to thy herds.
____

[NOTE]
====
*Economics Serve Mission:* CWS's business model exists to sustain the mission, not the reverse. Every financial decision is evaluated against Kingdom purposes, not just profit maximization.
====

'''

== Why Business Model Matters

=== The Sustainability Imperative

[IMPORTANT]
====
*No mission without means.* CWS cannot serve the Kingdom without sustainable economics. Good intentions don't pay for infrastructure, team members, or services. A clear, Kingdom-aligned business model is essential.

== Business Model Philosophy Summary

=== Economics Serve Mission

[IMPORTANT]
====
*First Principle:* CWS does not exist to make money. CWS makes money to exist — to serve the Kingdom. This distinction shapes every financial decision.
====

=== Key Philosophical Positions

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Position |What It Means

|*Mission Over Profit*
|We decline profitable opportunities that conflict with mission

|*Fair Exchange*
|We receive fair compensation — no more, no less

|*Sustainable Growth*
|We grow at the pace mission requires and resources allow

|*Transparency*
|We're clear about financial matters with appropriate parties

|*Preparation*
|We ready for economic scenarios most ignore

|===

'''

== Document Governance

=== Review Schedule

This chapter should be reviewed:

* *Annually* — Full business model review
* *Quarterly* — Financial performance against model
* *When Strategy Shifts* — Significant changes to economic approach
* *Market Changes* — Adaptation to significant external shifts

=== Financial Document Security

Financial documents require appropriate handling:

* Board-level documents restricted to Board and authorized leadership
* Operational financial data on need-to-know basis
* Partner-facing terms clearly documented but specific terms confidential
* Public positioning can reference philosophy without specific figures
