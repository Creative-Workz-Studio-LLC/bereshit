[.lead]
This document establishes *how {company-short} grows* — the philosophy, phases, and principles governing organizational growth. It defines sustainable growth that serves mission versus growth-at-all-costs that can destroy it.

[sidebar]
.icon:star[] Why This Document Matters
****
Growth isn't inherently good or bad — it depends on *what kind of growth* and *what it serves*. This document ensures every growth decision advances the mission, protects culture, and prepares {company-short} for long-term Kingdom service.
****

== icon:bullseye[] Document Purpose

[quote, Psalm 1:3 (KJV)]
____
And he shall be like a tree planted by the rivers of water, that bringeth forth his fruit in his season; his leaf also shall not wither; and whatsoever he doeth shall prosper.
____

[NOTE]
.icon:lightbulb[] Core Principle
====
*Growth in Season:* Like a tree that grows according to its nature and season, {company-short} grows at the pace mission requires and resources allow — not forced, not rushed, but fruitful.
====

'''

== icon:seedling[] Growth Philosophy

=== icon:bullseye[] What Growth Means at {company-short}

[IMPORTANT]
.icon:exclamation-circle[] Foundational Principle
====
*Growth serves mission; mission doesn't serve growth.* {company-short} grows to increase Kingdom impact, not to satisfy external pressure or ego. Every growth decision is evaluated against mission advancement.
====

=== icon:check-circle[] Growth Defined

[sidebar]
.icon:tree[] Healthy Growth
****
Healthy growth is like a tree — rooted, sustainable, bearing fruit in season. Not forced, not rushed, but *fruitful*.
****

[cols="^1,2,4",options="header",frame=all,grid=all]
|===
|icon:tag[] |Type |What It Means

|icon:bullseye[role=blue]
|*Mission Capacity Growth*
|Increased ability to serve the Kingdom — more impact, not just more activity

|icon:users[role=green]
|*Partner Network Growth*
|More aligned creatives and businesses served — relationships, not transactions

|icon:server[role=purple]
|*Technology Capability Growth*
|Expanded infrastructure for Kingdom use — better tools, not just more features

|icon:globe[role=orange]
|*Geographic Reach Growth*
|Service extended to new areas — broader impact, maintained quality

|icon:user-plus[role=gold]
|*Team Capability Growth*
|Increased skills and capacity — deeper capability, not just more people

|===

=== icon:ban[] What Growth Is Not

[cols="^1,2,4",options="header",frame=all,grid=all]
|===
|icon:tag[] |Not Growth |Why

|icon:ban[role=red]
|*Revenue for revenue's sake*
|Revenue without mission purpose is meaningless — money isn't the point

|icon:ban[role=red]
|*Headcount inflation*
|More people without more capacity is waste — bodies ≠ capability

|icon:ban[role=red]
|*Market share obsession*
|Dominance isn't the goal; service is — we're not empire builders

|icon:ban[role=red]
|*Vanity metrics*
|Numbers that look good but don't serve mission — impressiveness ≠ impact
