[.lead]
This document establishes *how {company-short} grows* — the philosophy, phases, and principles governing organizational growth. It defines sustainable growth that serves mission versus growth-at-all-costs that can destroy it.

[sidebar]
.icon:star[] Why This Document Matters
****
Growth isn't inherently good or bad — it depends on *what kind of growth* and *what it serves*. This document ensures every growth decision advances the mission, protects culture, and prepares {company-short} for long-term Kingdom service.
****

== icon:bullseye[] Document Purpose

[quote, Psalm 1:3 (KJV)]
____
And he shall be like a tree planted by the rivers of water, that bringeth forth his fruit in his season; his leaf also shall not wither; and whatsoever he doeth shall prosper.
____

[NOTE]
.icon:lightbulb[] Core Principle
====
*Growth in Season:* Like a tree that grows according to its nature and season, {company-short} grows at the pace mission requires and resources allow — not forced, not rushed, but fruitful.
====

== Growth Philosophy

=== What Growth Means at CWS

[IMPORTANT]
====
*Growth serves mission; mission doesn't serve growth.* CWS grows to increase Kingdom impact, not to satisfy external pressure or ego. Every growth decision is evaluated against mission advancement.
====

=== Growth Defined

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Type |What It Means

|*Mission Capacity Growth*
|Increased ability to serve the Kingdom

|*Partner Network Growth*
|More aligned creatives and businesses served

|*Technology Capability Growth*
|Expanded infrastructure for Kingdom use

|*Geographic Reach Growth*
|Service extended to new areas

|*Team Capability Growth*
|Increased skills and capacity

|===

=== What Growth Is Not

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Not Growth |Why

|*Revenue for revenue's sake*
|Revenue without mission purpose is meaningless

|*Headcount inflation*
|More people without more capacity is waste

|*Market share obsession*
|Dominance isn't the goal; service is

|*Vanity metrics*
|Numbers that look good but don't serve mission

|*Forced scaling*
|Growth faster than quality and culture allow

|===

'''

== Development Phases

=== Phase Overview

[source]
----
CWS DEVELOPMENT PHASES:

    PHASE 1          PHASE 2           PHASE 3          PHASE 4
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────►

    FOUNDATION       SUSTAINABILITY    SCALE            MATURITY

    │                │                 │                │

    │   partners     │ • Team growth   │ • Network      │   building
    │ • Prove model  │ • Systems       │   effects      │ • Knowledge
    │                │   mature        │                │   transfer
    │                │                 │                │
    └────────────────┴─────────────────┴────────────────┘
----

=== Phase 1: Foundation

[sidebar]
****
*Focus:* Build core capabilities, initial partnerships, prove model

*Key Activities:*

* Develop Bereshit technology stack
* Establish initial publishing partnerships
* Demonstrate publishing house model works
* Build team core
* Document thoroughly

*Success Indicators:*

* Technology functioning
* Partners successful
* Model validated
* Team stable
* Documentation comprehensive

*Duration:* Until model proven and core technology functional
****

=== Phase 2: Sustainability

[sidebar]
****
*Focus:* Achieve economic sustainability, mature operations

*Key Activities:*

* Reach break-even operations
* Stabilize revenue streams
* Build reserves
* Mature systems and processes
* Expand team carefully

*Success Indicators:*

* Operating sustainably
* Reserves building
* Systems reliable
* Partner base stable
* Team growing appropriately

*Duration:* Until economic sustainability achieved
****

=== Phase 3: Scale

[sidebar]
****
*Focus:* Expand reach and multiply impact

*Key Activities:*

* Grow partner network significantly
* Extend geographic reach
* Launch additional technology products
* Build trade network
* Strengthen preparation infrastructure

*Success Indicators:*

* Partner network multiplied
* Geographic expansion
* Product portfolio expanded
* Trade network functional
* Mark-free infrastructure ready

*Duration:* Ongoing expansion as mission requires
****

=== Phase 4: Maturity

[sidebar]
****
*Focus:* Stable operations, legacy building, knowledge transfer

*Key Activities:*

* Maintain stable operations
* Transfer knowledge to future builders
* Strengthen resilience
* Deepen preparation
* Build for generations

*Success Indicators:*

* Operations self-sustaining
* Knowledge preserved
* Succession ready
* Preparation complete
* Legacy established

*Duration:* Ongoing through tribulation and Millennium
****

== Growth Principles

=== Principle 1: Mission Alignment

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Application |Description

|*Growth must serve mission*
|Every growth initiative evaluated against mission

|*Mission-misaligned growth declined*
|Even attractive opportunities refused if misaligned

|*Mission defines pace*
|Grow at speed mission requires, not faster

|===

=== Principle 2: Quality Preservation

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Application |Description

|*Quality over quantity*
|Better to serve fewer well than many poorly

|*Standards maintained*
|Growth doesn't compromise quality standards

|*Partner success matters*
|Growth measured by partner outcomes, not just numbers

|===

=== Principle 3: Culture Protection

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Application |Description

|*Values transmitted*
|New members absorb values before rapid expansion

|*Hiring carefully*
|Team growth slow enough to maintain culture

|*Partnership selectivity*
|Partners aligned, not just interested

|===

=== Principle 4: Sustainable Pace

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Application |Description

|*Internal funding*
|Growth funded by operations, not external pressure

|*Capacity-matched*
|Grow at pace current capacity allows

|*Buffer maintained*
|Don't grow to maximum capacity; maintain margin

|===

'''

== What CWS Doesn't Do

=== Growth Approaches Rejected

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
|Approach |What It Means |Why Rejected

|*VC-style growth*
|External investment for rapid scaling
|Creates exit pressure, misaligns incentives

|*Debt-fueled expansion*
|Borrow to grow faster
|Interest pressure affects decisions

|*Growth-at-all-costs*
|Prioritize growth over everything
|Sacrifices mission, values, quality

|*Hockey stick expectations*
|Exponential growth required
|Unsustainable, pressure-creating

|*Market dominance play*
|Crush competition, dominate market
|Not Kingdom mindset; service focus instead

|===

=== Why These Are Rejected

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Reason |Explanation

|*Mission Protection*
|External pressure distorts mission priorities

|*Values Preservation*
|Growth pressure leads to values compromise

|*Quality Maintenance*
|Rapid growth typically degrades quality

|*Sustainability*
|Artificial acceleration creates fragility

|*Kingdom Alignment*
|These approaches reflect worldly patterns, not Kingdom

|===

'''

== Growth Decision Framework

=== Decision Questions

For growth opportunities, ask:

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Question |What It Tests

|"Does this advance the mission?"
|Mission alignment

|"Can we maintain quality?"
|Capacity to deliver well

|"Does this preserve culture?"
|Values and culture protection

|"Is this sustainable?"
|Long-term viability

|"What's the Kingdom impact?"
|Ultimate purpose

|===
