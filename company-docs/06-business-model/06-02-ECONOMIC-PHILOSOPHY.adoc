== Document Purpose

This document establishes the *economic philosophy* underlying all CWS financial decisions — mission-driven economics, fair exchange principles, Kingdom economics, and values-based pricing. This philosophy shapes how CWS approaches money, value, and financial relationships.

[quote, Matthew 6:24 (KJV)]
____
No man can serve two masters: for either he will hate the one, and love the other; or else he will hold to the one, and despise the other. Ye cannot serve God and mammon.
____

[NOTE]
====
*Philosophy Precedes Practice:* Financial decisions don't happen in a vacuum. The philosophy established here guides every economic choice CWS makes — from pricing to partnerships to growth strategies.
====

'''

== Mission-Driven Economics

=== The Core Distinction

[IMPORTANT]
====
*CWS does not exist to make money. CWS makes money to exist.* This is the fundamental distinction that shapes all economic philosophy. Money is a tool for mission, not the purpose of our existence.
====

=== Comparison Framework

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
|Dimension |Profit-Driven |Mission-Driven (CWS)

|*Primary Goal*
|Maximize shareholder return
|Sustain Kingdom service

|*Success Metric*
|Revenue and profit growth
|Mission impact + sustainability

|*Decision Driver*
|"Will this increase profit?"
|"Does this serve the mission?"

|*Partner View*
|Source of extraction
|Fellow traveler in Kingdom work

|*Growth Orientation*
|Growth for growth's sake
|Growth that serves mission

|*Time Horizon*
|Quarterly optimization
|Generational sustainability

|*Ethical Boundaries*
|Legal compliance
|Kingdom values (beyond legal)

|===

=== What Mission-Driven Looks Like

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Situation |Mission-Driven Response

|*Profitable but misaligned opportunity*
|Decline — mission alignment trumps profit

|*Less profitable but mission-aligned*
|Pursue — mission is primary measure

|*Short-term loss for long-term mission*
|Accept — time horizon extends beyond quarters

|*Partner struggles but aligned*
|Support — relationships matter more than transactions

|*Growth opportunity requiring values compromise*
|Refuse — values aren't negotiable for growth

|===

'''

== Fair Exchange Principle

=== The Principle Defined

[source]
----
FAIR EXCHANGE:

    ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │                                             │
    │   CWS provides value  ◄────►  CWS receives  │
    │   (services, products,       fair           │
    │    support, infrastructure)   compensation  │
    │                                             │
    │   NO MORE than fair    │    NO LESS than    │
    │   (not extraction)     │    fair (not       │
    │                        │    exploitation    │
    │                        │    of CWS)         │
    └─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    Fair = commensurate with actual value provided
----

=== What Fair Exchange Means

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Aspect |Application

|*No Extraction*
|We don't take maximum possible — just fair value for services

|*No Subsidization*
|We don't systematically undercharge to unsustainable levels

|*Transparent Basis*
|Pricing has clear reasoning that can be explained

|*Value Correlation*
|Payment corresponds to actual value delivered

|*Mutual Benefit*
|Both parties should benefit from exchange

|===

=== Fair Exchange vs. Alternatives

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
|Model |Characteristic |CWS Position

|*Market Maximum*
|Charge what market bears
|Rejected — extraction mentality

|*Race to Bottom*
|Undercut all competitors
|Rejected — unsustainable

|*Cost Plus*
|Cost plus standard margin
|Partial — costs matter but value matters more

|*Fair Exchange*
|Value delivered = fair compensation
|Adopted — sustainable and just

|===

'''

== Kingdom Economics

=== Biblical Economic Principles

CWS economics are grounded in biblical principles:

==== Stewardship

[quote, 1 Corinthians 4:2 (KJV)]
____
Moreover it is required in stewards, that a man be found faithful.
____

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Principle |Application

|*Not Owners*
|Resources belong to God; we manage them

|*Accountability*
|Will give account for how resources used

|*Faithfulness*
|Consistent, reliable management

|*Multiplication*
|Grow what's entrusted, don't bury it

|===

==== Generosity

[quote, 2 Corinthians 9:7 (KJV)]
____
Every man according as he purposeth in his heart, so let him give; not grudgingly, or of necessity: for God loveth a cheerful giver.
____

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Principle |Application

|*Built-In Giving*
|Generosity is structural, not afterthought

|*Cheerful*
|Giving from abundance mentality

|*Strategic*
|Generosity directed for Kingdom impact

|*Non-Manipulative*
|Giving without strings attached

|===

==== Justice

[quote, Proverbs 11:1 (KJV)]
____
A false balance is abomination to the LORD: but a just weight is his delight.
____

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Principle |Application

|*Honest Dealing*
|No deception in financial matters

|*Fair Terms*
|Balanced agreements, not one-sided

|*Worker Dignity*
|Fair compensation for labor

|*Partner Respect*
|Terms that honor partner contribution

|===

==== Sufficiency

[quote, Proverbs 30:8-9 (KJV)]
____
Give me neither poverty nor riches; feed me with food convenient for me: Lest I be full, and deny thee, and say, Who is the LORD? or lest I be poor, and steal, and take the name of my God in vain.
____

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Principle |Application

|*Enough Is Enough*
|Not pursuing endless accumulation

|*Contentment*
|Sufficient for mission, not maximum possible

|*Anti-Greed*
|Rejecting greed-driven growth

|*Balance*
|Neither poverty (can't serve) nor excess (distraction)

|===

==== Trust

[quote, Matthew 6:31-33 (KJV)]
____
Therefore take no thought, saying, What shall we eat? or, What shall we drink? or, Wherewithal shall we be clothed?... But seek ye first the kingdom of God, and his righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you.
____

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Principle |Application

|*God Provides*
|Fundamental trust in provision

|*Kingdom First*
|Priority on mission, trusting economics follow

|*Not Anxious*
|Planning without anxiety

|*Faith-Based*
|Decisions rooted in faith, not fear

|===

== Values-Before-Revenue Decisions

=== The Principle

[IMPORTANT]
====
*When values and revenue conflict, values win.* CWS will decline revenue opportunities that require values compromise. This is non-negotiable — values aren't for sale.
====

=== Decision Examples

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
|Situation |Values Decision |Revenue Impact

|*Misaligned partner offers large deal*
|Decline partnership
|Significant revenue foregone

|*Profitable product conflicts with values*
|Don't build it
|Revenue opportunity passed

|*Client requests values-violating work*
|Refuse the work
|Fee declined

|*Growth requires values compromise*
|Grow slower
|Growth rate reduced

|*Competitor succeeds through compromised values*
|Don't follow
|Market position potentially affected

|===

=== Why This Matters

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Reason |Explanation

|*Integrity*
|Can't serve Kingdom while compromising Kingdom values

|*Trust*
|Partners trust CWS because values are reliable

|*Long-Term*
|Short-term revenue from compromise destroys long-term mission

|*Witness*
|How we operate witnesses to what we believe

|*Accountability*
|Will give account for how resources and opportunities were handled

|===

'''

== Economic Decision Framework

=== Decision Questions

For any significant financial decision, ask:

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Question |What It Tests

|"Does this serve the mission?"
|Mission alignment

|"Is this fair exchange?"
|Fair exchange principle

|"Does this honor Kingdom economics?"
|Biblical alignment

|"Does this maintain values?"
|Values integrity

|"Is this sustainable?"
|Long-term viability

|"Would we be comfortable if this were public?"
|Transparency and integrity

|===

=== Red Flags

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Red Flag |What It Indicates

|"We need this revenue"
|Desperation overriding judgment

|"Everyone else does it"
|Worldly conformity pressure

|"Just this once"
|Values slippage beginning

|"They won't know"
|Integrity failure

|"We can do good with the money"
|Ends-justify-means thinking

|===
