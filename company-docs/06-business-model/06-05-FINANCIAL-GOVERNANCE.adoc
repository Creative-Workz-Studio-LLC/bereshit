[.lead]
This document establishes the *financial governance framework* for {company-short} — who makes financial decisions, how financial information is reported, what controls ensure accountability, and how the Board maintains oversight.

[sidebar]
.icon:star[] Why This Document Matters
****
*Trust is built on transparency.* Without clear governance, financial decisions become opaque and accountability impossible. This framework ensures every financial action is authorized, documented, and aligned with mission — protecting {company-short}, its partners, and its purpose.
****

== icon:bullseye[] Document Purpose

[quote, Luke 16:10 (KJV)]
____
He that is faithful in that which is least is faithful also in much: and he that is unjust in the least is unjust also in much.
____

[NOTE]
.icon:lightbulb[] Core Principle
====
*Governance Enables Trust:* Clear financial governance isn't bureaucracy — it's the structure that enables trust. Partners, team members, and the Board can trust financial management because governance is clear.
====

'''

=== icon:columns[] Separation of Duties

[cols="^1,2,3,3",options="header",frame=all,grid=all]
|===
|icon:tag[] |Function |Separation Principle |Why

|icon:check[role=blue]
|*Authorization*
|Separate from execution
|Prevent unauthorized transactions

|icon:box[role=green]
|*Custody*
|Separate from record-keeping
|Prevent misappropriation hiding

|icon:sync[role=purple]
|*Reconciliation*
|Independent of transactions
|Prevent concealment

|icon:thumbs-up[role=orange]
|*Approval*
|Different from requestor
|Prevent self-dealing

|===

[IMPORTANT]
.icon:exclamation-circle[] Non-Negotiable
====
Separation of duties is *not optional*. Small organization size doesn't excuse bypass. Find creative solutions to maintain separation.
====
