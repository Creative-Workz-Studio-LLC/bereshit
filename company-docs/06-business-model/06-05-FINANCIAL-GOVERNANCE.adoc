== Document Purpose

This document establishes the *financial governance framework* for CWS — who makes financial decisions, how financial information is reported, what controls ensure accountability, and how the Board maintains oversight.

[quote, Luke 16:10 (KJV)]
____
He that is faithful in that which is least is faithful also in much: and he that is unjust in the least is unjust also in much.
____

[NOTE]
====
*Governance Enables Trust:* Clear financial governance isn't bureaucracy — it's the structure that enables trust. Partners, team members, and the Board can trust financial management because governance is clear.
====

'''

== Governance Framework

=== Governance Principles

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Principle |Application

|*Accountability*
|Every financial decision has accountable party

|*Transparency*
|Appropriate visibility into financial matters

|*Separation*
|Duties separated to prevent problems

|*Oversight*
|Board maintains strategic financial oversight

|*Stewardship*
|Resources managed as stewards, not owners

|===

== Decision Authority Matrix

=== Overview

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
|Decision Type |Authority |Process

|*Strategic Financial Decisions*
|Board
|Board meeting approval required

|*Annual Budget*
|Board approval
|Leadership proposes, Board approves

|*Major Investments*
|Board
|Proposal with analysis, Board decision

|*Operating Within Budget*
|Leadership
|Execute approved budget

|*Day-to-Day Transactions*
|Authorized personnel
|Within delegated authority

|===

=== Threshold Definitions

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
|Threshold |Amount Range |Approval Required

|*Routine*
|Up to $X
|Authorized operational personnel

|*Significant*
|$X to $Y
|Leadership approval

|*Major*
|$Y to $Z
|Leadership + Board notification

|*Strategic*
|Above $Z
|Board approval required

|===

[NOTE]
====
Specific dollar thresholds to be established based on operational scale. The principle is clear authority levels regardless of specific amounts.
====

=== Separation of Duties

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
|Function |Separation Principle |Why

|*Authorization*
|Separate from execution
|Prevent unauthorized transactions

|*Custody*
|Separate from record-keeping
|Prevent misappropriation hiding

|*Reconciliation*
|Independent of transactions
|Prevent concealment

|*Approval*
|Different from requestor
|Prevent self-dealing

|===

=== Key Controls

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Control |Description

|*Multi-signature*
|Large transactions require multiple approvals

|*Reconciliation*
|Regular reconciliation of accounts

|*Review*
|Management review of financial activity

|*Documentation*
|All transactions documented with support

|*Audit Trail*
|Complete history of financial decisions

|===

'''
