## Service Delivery Principles

### 1. Teach, Don't Just Deliver

Every service engagement should transfer knowledge. Clients should understand more after working with us, not less.

### 2. Build Autonomy, Not Dependency

Our goal is clients who can function without us, not clients locked into our services. Success is independence.

### 3. Values Before Revenue

We turn down work that doesn't align with Kingdom values, even if profitable. Alignment matters more than money.

### 4. Community Over Transaction

Engagements build relationships, not just deliverables. Every client joins a network, not just a vendor relationship.

### 5. Preparation Orientation

Everything we provide should make recipients more prepared for what's coming, not more dependent on current systems.

## Engagement Process

### For Creatives

1. **Initial Conversation** — Understanding your work and goals
2. **Values Alignment** — Ensuring Kingdom alignment
3. **Support Level Selection** — Choosing investment/revenue split
4. **Agreement** — Formalizing partnership terms
5. **Onboarding** — Getting set up with tools and access
6. **Ongoing Support** — Continuous relationship, not one-time transaction

### For Businesses

1. **Assessment** — Understanding current state and needs
2. **Values Alignment** — Ensuring Kingdom alignment
3. **Planning** — Designing approach and timeline
4. **Implementation** — Executing with knowledge transfer
5. **Network Integration** — Connecting to broader community
6. **Ongoing Relationship** — Continued support and collaboration

## Summary

CWS provides services that equip, connect, and prepare. For creatives: marketing, publication, consultation while preserving ownership. For businesses: infrastructure, integration, trade network access. For the body: accessible technology, mark-free infrastructure, preparation resources.

We serve to build up the Kingdom, not to build an empire.
