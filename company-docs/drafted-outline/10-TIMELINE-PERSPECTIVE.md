## Overview

CWS operates with an unusual timeline perspective. We're not building for quarterly earnings or five-year exits. We're building for the Millennium—Christ's thousand-year reign—while being faithful in the present.

This document outlines our phased perspective from now through the age to come.

## Timeline Principles

### We Don't Know Timing

We don't know when Christ returns. We don't know how long each phase lasts. We build faithfully regardless.

> *"For the vision is yet for an appointed time, but at the end it shall speak, and not lie: though it tarry, wait for it; because it will surely come, it will not tarry."* — Habakkuk 2:3

### Faithfulness Over Forecasting

We don't optimize for predicted timing. We build what should be built, when we can build it, trusting the Lord with results.

### Present and Future Together

We serve the present (creatives need publishing, businesses need infrastructure) while building for the future (Millennium needs technology). These aren't in conflict.

### Noah Built Before Rain

Noah built the ark before the flood. He looked foolish until he didn't. We build mark-free infrastructure before it's obviously needed.

CWS operates across five phases: Foundation (current), Transition, Sustainability, Tribulation Preparation, and Millennium. We build for all phases simultaneously—serving present needs while preparing for future realities. We don't know timing, but we know direction.

Noah built before rain. We build before need.
