## Purpose of This Document

This manual establishes the foundational identity of Creative Workz Studio LLC. It defines who we are, what we do, why we exist, and how we operate. This document serves as:

1. **Alignment Tool** — Ensures all team members understand our identity and mission
2. **Onboarding Foundation** — First document for new team members and partners
3. **Decision Framework** — Reference for evaluating opportunities and partnerships
4. **External Communication** — Source of truth for all public-facing materials
