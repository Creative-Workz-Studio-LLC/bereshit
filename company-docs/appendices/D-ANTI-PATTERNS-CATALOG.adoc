== icon:landmark[] Empire Building

*Source: Multiple chapters*

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning
****
*What It Is:* Building {company-short} into a large, powerful organization with us at the center — accumulating influence, control, or territory for self rather than serving the mission.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Decisions driven by growth for growth's sake
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Hoarding information or relationships
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Resisting others' involvement in "my area"
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Measuring success by organizational size
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Taking credit; deflecting blame
****

'''

== icon:money-bill-alt[] Extraction Mindset

*Source: 07-01, 09-05*

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning
****
*What It Is:* Approaching {company-short} primarily to extract value — taking more than giving, using relationships for personal benefit.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Calculating personal benefit in every decision
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Minimal contribution, maximum expectation
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Relationships valued by what they provide
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Reluctance to help when benefit unclear
* icon:times-circle[role=red] "What's in it for me?" as primary question
****

'''

== icon:bolt[] Short-Term Thinking

*Source: 09-05, 10-01*

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning
****
*What It Is:* Optimizing for immediate results at the expense of long-term mission — quick wins over sustainable building.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Rushing foundations to show progress
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Compromising quality for speed
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Decisions that help now but hurt later
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Impatience with proper process
* icon:times-circle[role=red] "Just this once" compromises
****

'''

== icon:handshake-alt-slash[] Values Compromise

*Source: 07-09, 09-05*

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning
****
*What It Is:* Bending or breaking non-negotiable values for convenience, opportunity, or pressure.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] "Just this once won't hurt"
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Rationalizing violations as necessary
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Pressure to compromise for revenue
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Looking for loopholes in principles
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Different standards for different situations
****

'''

*Source: 07-03*

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning
****
*What It Is:* Gradually shifting from connective tissue to center — making {company-short} the hub everything depends on rather than enabling the network.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Decisions that increase dependency on {company-short}
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Reluctance to transfer knowledge
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Taking roles others could do better
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Network health secondary to {company-short} prominence
* icon:times-circle[role=red] "We're the experts" mentality
****

'''

== icon:chart-line[] Growth Obsession

*Source: 10-01*

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning
****
*What It Is:* Pursuing growth metrics (users, revenue, team size) as primary goals rather than building solid foundations.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Decisions justified by "it will help us grow"
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Metrics focus on size rather than solidity
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Impatience with foundation-building pace
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Cutting corners to show progress
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Comparing ourselves to growth-focused companies
****

'''

== icon:hand-holding-usd[] Compromising Money

*Source: 10-01*

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning
****
*What It Is:* Accepting funding, partnerships, or revenue that comes with strings attached — requirements that conflict with values or mission.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] "We'll just take this for now and stay true later"
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Rationalizing small compromises
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Financial pressure driving mission-adjacent decisions
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Investors/partners with incompatible values
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Revenue that requires values violations
****

'''

*Source: 10-04*

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning
****
*What It Is:* Refusing to acknowledge the reality of what Scripture clearly describes — pretending tribulation won't happen or won't affect us.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] "It won't happen in our lifetime"
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Spiritualizing away literal prophecy
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Assuming someone else will prepare
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Dismissing preparation as paranoia
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Normalcy bias overriding Scripture
****

'''

== icon:comments-dollar[] Culture Killers

*Source: 09-05*

=== icon:comments[] Gossip

[cols="^1,4",frame=all,grid=all]
|===
