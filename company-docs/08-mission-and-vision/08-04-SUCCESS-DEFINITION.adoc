[.lead]
This document defines *what success looks like for {company-short}* — using Kingdom metrics rather than merely worldly measures.

[sidebar]
.icon:star[] Why This Document Matters
****
How you define success determines what you pursue. Worldly metrics can mislead; Kingdom metrics reveal truth. This document ensures {company-short} measures what *actually matters* in light of eternity.
****

== icon:file-alt[] Document Purpose

[quote, 1 Samuel 16:7 (KJV)]
____
...for the LORD seeth not as man seeth; for man looketh on the outward appearance, but the LORD looketh on the heart.
____

[NOTE]
.icon:lightbulb[] Core Principle
====
*Kingdom Metrics:* Worldly success metrics (revenue, market share, growth rate) may indicate progress but don't define success. {company-short} success is measured by mission fulfillment, not worldly metrics alone.
====

'''

== icon:check-circle[] What Success IS

=== icon:crown[] Kingdom Success Definition

[IMPORTANT]
====
*CWS Success:*

[.lead]
CWS succeeds when creators are served while keeping their work, when the Body has trusted infrastructure, when mark-free alternatives exist and function, and when what we build teaches and endures — regardless of worldly metrics.
====
