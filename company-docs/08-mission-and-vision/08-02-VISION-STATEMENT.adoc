== The Vision Statement

[IMPORTANT]
====
*CWS Vision Statement:*

[.lead]
A publishing house spanning every industry where creators keep what they create, faith-based businesses have foundations they can trust, and the faithful have infrastructure that doesn't require the mark — all built on patterns that honor the Creator, teaching through everything we make, for the Millennium and beyond.
====

'''

== Vision vs. Mission

=== The Difference

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
|Aspect |Mission |Vision

|*Focus*
|What we do
|What we're building toward

|*Timeframe*
|Ongoing — daily work
|Future — destination

|Action-oriented
|Outcome-oriented

|*Question*
|"What do we do?"
|"What does success look like?"

Mission is the path; vision is the destination. We pursue mission daily, and that pursuit moves us toward vision.
