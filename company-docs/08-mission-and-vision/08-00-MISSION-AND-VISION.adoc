== Document Purpose

This chapter establishes CWS's *mission and vision* — the formal statements of why CWS exists, what CWS is working toward, and how these guide all decisions. Mission and vision provide direction; values provide guardrails.

[quote, Proverbs 29:18 (KJV)]
____
Where there is no vision, the people perish: but he that keepeth the law, happy is he.
____

[NOTE]
====
*Direction and Guardrails:* Mission and vision tell us where we're going. Values (<<../07-core-values/07-00-CORE-VALUES.adoc#,Chapter 07>>) tell us how we must travel. Both are essential — direction without guardrails leads astray; guardrails without direction lead nowhere.
====

'''

== Mission Statement

=== The Statement

[IMPORTANT]
====
*CWS Mission Statement:*

To publish and equip creatives across every sector — preserving their ownership and autonomy — while building Kingdom-aligned infrastructure that serves the body of Christ and provides mark-free alternatives for the world to come.
====

=== Quick Breakdown

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Element |Meaning

|*"publish and equip creatives"*
|Publishing house identity — serving creators

|*"across every sector"*
|Not limited to one creative domain

|*"preserving their ownership and autonomy"*
|Creator autonomy — the non-negotiable

|*"building Kingdom-aligned infrastructure"*
|Infrastructure builder identity

|*"serves the body of Christ"*
|Primary audience — the Church

|*"mark-free alternatives"*
|Preparation for prophetic realities

|*"for the world to come"*
|Eternal perspective — Millennium and beyond

|===

For detailed breakdown, see <<08-01-MISSION-STATEMENT.adoc#,08-01: Mission Statement>>.

== Vision Statement

=== The Statement

[IMPORTANT]
====
*CWS Vision Statement:*

A publishing house spanning every industry where creators keep what they create, faith-based businesses have foundations they can trust, and the faithful have infrastructure that doesn't require the mark — all built on patterns that honor the Creator, teaching through everything we make, for the Millennium and beyond.
====

=== Quick Breakdown

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Element |Future State

|*"publishing house spanning every industry"*
|CWS serves creatives across all sectors

|*"creators keep what they create"*
|Creator autonomy is the norm, not exception

|*"faith-based businesses have foundations they can trust"*
|Reliable infrastructure for Kingdom organizations

|*"the faithful have infrastructure that doesn't require the mark"*
|Mark-free alternatives exist and function

|*"built on patterns that honor the Creator"*
|Genesis patterns throughout technology

|*"teaching through everything we make"*
|Education embedded in all products

|*"for the Millennium and beyond"*
|Technology serves through Christ's reign

|===

For detailed breakdown, see <<08-02-VISION-STATEMENT.adoc#,08-02: Vision Statement>>.
