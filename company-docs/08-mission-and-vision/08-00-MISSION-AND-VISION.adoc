[.lead]
This chapter establishes {company-short}'s *mission and vision* — the formal statements of why we exist, what we're working toward, and how these guide all decisions.

[sidebar]
.icon:star[] Why This Chapter Matters
****
Organizations without clear mission drift. Organizations without clear vision meander. This chapter provides the *North Star* that guides every {company-short} decision — ensuring we build for the Kingdom to come, not just for today's needs.
****

== icon:bullseye[] Document Purpose

[quote, Proverbs 29:18 (KJV)]
____
Where there is no vision, the people perish: but he that keepeth the law, happy is he.
____

[NOTE]
.icon:lightbulb[] Core Understanding
====
*Direction and Guardrails:* Mission and vision tell us where we're going. Values (<<../07-core-values/07-00-CORE-VALUES.adoc#,Chapter 07>>) tell us how we must travel. Both are essential — direction without guardrails leads astray; guardrails without direction lead nowhere.
====
