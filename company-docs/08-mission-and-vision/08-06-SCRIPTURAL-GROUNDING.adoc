== Document Purpose

This document presents the *scriptural foundation* for CWS mission and vision — the key scriptures that inform, shape, and ground our direction. The Bible isn't just inspiration; it's the foundation from which CWS operates.

[quote, 2 Timothy 3:16-17 (KJV)]
____
All scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness: That the man of God may be perfect, throughly furnished unto all good works.
____

'''

== Foundation Scriptures

=== Genesis 1:1 — The Beginning

[quote, Genesis 1:1 (KJV)]
____
In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth.
____

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Aspect |Application to CWS

|*Teaching*
|God is the original Creator; all creation flows from Him

|*Application*
|CWS follows creation patterns (Bereshit), honoring the Creator through how we build

|*Mission Connection*
|"Kingdom-aligned infrastructure" — built on patterns from the Beginning

|*Practical*
|Ternary math, void/word/tov structure, Genesis patterns throughout technology

|===

See: <<../03-what-cws-builds/03-01-BERESHIT-STACK-OVERVIEW.adoc#,03-01: Bereshit Stack Overview>>

=== Habakkuk 2:2-3 — Write the Vision

[quote, Habakkuk 2:2-3 (KJV)]
____
And the LORD answered me, and said, Write the vision, and make it plain upon tables, that he may run that readeth it. For the vision is yet for an appointed time, but at the end it shall speak, and not lie: though it tarry, wait for it; because it will surely come, it will not tarry.
____

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Aspect |Application to CWS

|*Teaching*
|Document clearly; make accessible; the vision has appointed timing

|*Application*
|Teaching Through Making, Accessible by Design — documentation imperative

|*Mission Connection*
|"Teaching through everything we make" — write plainly so others can run

|*Practical*
|All products teach; all documentation accessible; knowledge preserved

|===

See: <<../07-core-values/07-04-TEACHING-THROUGH-MAKING.adoc#,07-04: Teaching Through Making>>

=== Revelation 13:16-17 — The Mark

[quote, Revelation 13:16-17 (KJV)]
____
And he causeth all, both small and great, rich and poor, free and bond, to receive a mark in their right hand, or in their foreheads: And that no man might buy or sell, save he that had the mark, or the name of the beast, or the number of his name.
____

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Aspect |Application to CWS

|*Teaching*
|Economic restriction is coming; preparation is wisdom

|*Application*
|Mark-free alternatives — infrastructure that functions without beast system

|*Mission Connection*
|"Provides mark-free alternatives for the world to come"

|*Practical*
|FaithNet, trade networks, decentralized infrastructure, self-hosting

|===

See: <<../06-business-model/06-06-MARK-FREE-ECONOMICS.adoc#,06-06: Mark-Free Economics>>

=== Revelation 20:4-6 — The Millennium

[quote, Revelation 20:4-6 (KJV)]
____
...and they lived and reigned with Christ a thousand years... This is the first resurrection. Blessed and holy is he that hath part in the first resurrection...
____

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Aspect |Application to CWS

|*Teaching*
|Christ's thousand-year reign is literal; we build for it

|*Application*
|Technology designed to serve through the Millennium, not just present

|*Mission Connection*
|"For the world to come" — eternal perspective, not just immediate needs

|*Practical*
|Long-term architecture, knowledge preservation, teaching future builders

|===

See: <<../05-who-cws-serves/05-05-FUTURE-BUILDERS.adoc#,05-05: Future Builders>>

== Biblical Examples

=== Noah — Building for What's Coming

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Aspect |Parallel to CWS

|*Situation*
|Warning of coming judgment; world unaware

|*Response*
|Built ark over many years despite ridicule

|*Faithfulness*
|Did exactly what God commanded

|*Outcome*
|Preserved life through judgment

|*CWS Parallel*
|Building infrastructure for coming restriction while most ignore warnings

|===

=== Joseph — Preparation During Plenty

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Aspect |Parallel to CWS

|*Situation*
|Seven years of plenty, then seven years of famine coming

|*Response*
|Stored during plenty for famine years

|*Faithfulness*
|Systematic preparation, wise stewardship

|*Outcome*
|Egypt and many others survived famine

|*CWS Parallel*
|Building during relatively free times for restricted times ahead

|===

=== Nehemiah — Building Infrastructure for Community

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Aspect |Parallel to CWS

|*Situation*
|Jerusalem's walls broken; community vulnerable

|*Response*
|Organized community to rebuild walls

|*Faithfulness*
|Worked despite opposition; completed the work

|*Outcome*
|Protected community, renewed worship

|*CWS Parallel*
|Building infrastructure to protect and enable the Body

|===

=== Bezalel — Skilled Work for God's Purposes

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Aspect |Parallel to CWS

|*Situation*
|Tabernacle needed; skilled workers required (Exodus 31:1-11)

|*Response*
|God filled Bezalel with wisdom, understanding, knowledge for craftsmanship

|*Faithfulness*
|Used skills for sacred purpose

|*Outcome*
|Tabernacle built for God's presence

|*CWS Parallel*
|Using technical skills for Kingdom purposes, not just commercial

|===
