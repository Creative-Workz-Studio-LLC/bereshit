=== Nature of Relationship

The relationship between CWS and Guardian Angel Settlement Association is *indirect*:

* *Seanje Lenox-Wise* (CWS Founder) serves as a volunteer at Guardian Angel Settlement Association
* *CWS as an entity* has no formal relationship with Guardian Angel Settlement Association
* This is a *personal service commitment*, not a corporate partnership

|===
|Significance |Explanation

|*Values Demonstration*
|Founder's personal service demonstrates the "Service to Community" value is lived, not just stated

|*Separate Identities*
|CWS and Guardian Angel Settlement Association maintain distinct identities and operations

|*No Conflicts*
|Clear separation prevents conflicts of interest or confusion

|*Leadership Example*
|Founder models community service expected of CWS culture
|===

=== Relationship Boundaries

[cols="1,2",options="header"]
|===
|Boundary |Implication

|*Legal Separation*
|CWS has no legal obligations to or from Guardian Angel Settlement Association

|*Financial Separation*
|CWS funds are not committed to Guardian Angel Settlement Association; Seanje's personal service is volunteer

|*Operational Separation*
|CWS operations do not include Guardian Angel Settlement Association activities

|*Brand Separation*
|Guardian Angel Settlement Association does not represent CWS; CWS does not represent Guardian Angel Settlement Association

== Business Partner Relationships

=== Creative Partners

Creative partners are individuals or organizations who publish through CWS.

[cols="1,2",options="header"]
|===
|Aspect |Description

|*Relationship Type*
|Contractual partnership

|*Governing Document*
|Partnership Agreement (per partner)

|*IP Ownership*
|Creator retains full ownership

|*CWS Role*
|Publishing services provider

|*Financial Model*
|Revenue split per agreement
|===

==== Partner Independence

Creative partners:

* Remain independent entities (or individuals)
* Are not employees, subsidiaries, or affiliates
* Maintain their own identity and brand
* Can have other publishing relationships (unless specifically agreed otherwise)

==== Partner Selection Principles

CWS partners with creators who demonstrate:

* Values alignment with CWS mission
* Quality work worthy of publication
* Integrity in business dealings
* Willingness to engage in community (not just transaction)

=== Infrastructure Partners

Organizations that adopt CWS infrastructure for their operations.

[cols="1,2",options="header"]
|===
|Aspect |Description

|*Relationship Type*
|Service/licensing agreement

|*Governing Document*
|Service Agreement or License

|*CWS Role*
|Infrastructure provider, possibly ongoing support

|*Partner Role*
|Infrastructure user, possibly contributor

|*Financial Model*
|Service fees, licensing, or partnership arrangement
|===

==== Infrastructure Partner Independence

Infrastructure partners:

* Use CWS technology but remain independent
* Own their implementations and customizations
* Can switch to alternatives (no lock-in intent)
* Receive knowledge transfer to enable independence

== Relationship Governance

=== Documentation Requirements

All formal relationships should have:

[cols="1,2",options="header"]
|===
|Requirement |Purpose

|*Written Agreement*
|Clear terms, expectations, responsibilities

|*Defined Duration*
|When relationship begins/ends or renews

|*Exit Provisions*
|How either party can end relationship

|*Dispute Resolution*
|How disagreements are handled

|*Values Alignment*
|Explicit acknowledgment of shared values
|===

=== Relationship Review

Formal relationships should be reviewed:

* *Annually* — Assess ongoing value and alignment
* *Upon Issues* — When problems arise
* *Upon Changes* — When circumstances change significantly
* *Before Renewal* — Prior to automatic renewals

=== Conflict of Interest

==== Disclosure Requirements

Board members, team members, and key partners must disclose:

* Ownership interests in related entities
* Employment or service relationships with related entities
* Family relationships with related entities
* Financial interests that could create conflicts

==== Management Approach

When conflicts exist:

* *Disclosure* — Transparent acknowledgment
* *Recusal* — Conflicted parties don't participate in related decisions
* *Documentation* — Record how conflicts are managed
* *Monitoring* — Ongoing awareness of potential issues
