== icon:bullseye[] Document Purpose

[.lead]
This document establishes the relationships between {company-name} and other organizational entities. It clarifies what relationships exist, how they function, and the principles governing these connections.

[NOTE]
.icon:info-circle[] Governance Importance
====
Understanding entity relationships is essential for governance, compliance, and strategic clarity. This document distinguishes between formal organizational relationships and informal personal connections.
====

[sidebar]
.icon:question-circle[] Why This Document Matters
****
Understanding our relationships is essential for:

* icon:gavel[] *Legal Clarity* — Distinct entities with clear boundaries
* icon:coins[] *Financial Integrity* — No commingling of resources
* icon:handshake[] *Partnership Context* — What relationships mean
* icon:shield-alt[] *Conflict Prevention* — Clear expectations from the start
****

'''
