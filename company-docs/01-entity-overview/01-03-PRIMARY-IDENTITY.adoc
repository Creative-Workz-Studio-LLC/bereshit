== icon:bullseye[] Document Purpose

[.lead]
This document establishes and explains {company-name}'s *primary identity* as a publishing house. While {company-short} engages in multiple activities (including infrastructure building, technology development, and service provision), our foundational identity is that of a *publisher* — an entity that helps bring creative works into the world.

[IMPORTANT]
.icon:exclamation-circle[] Foundational Understanding
====
Understanding {company-short} as a publishing house *first* is essential. All other activities (technology development, infrastructure building) serve and support the publishing mission. This ordering is intentional and foundational.
====

[sidebar]
.icon:question-circle[] Why This Document Matters
****
Understanding our primary identity is essential for:

* icon:compass[] *Strategic Clarity* — All decisions flow from identity
* icon:users[] *Partner Alignment* — Creators understand who they're partnering with
* icon:building[] *Organizational Focus* — Infrastructure serves publishing, not the reverse
* icon:crosshairs[] *Mission Integrity* — Prevents drift into unrelated activities
****

'''

== icon:book[] What Is a Publishing House?

=== icon:history[] Traditional Definition

A publishing house is an organization that:

[cols="^1,4",frame=all,grid=all]
|===
|icon:search-plus[role=blue]
|*Acquires* creative works from creators

|icon:cogs[role=green]
|*Produces* those works in distributable form

|icon:paper-plane[role=purple]
|*Distributes* works to audiences through various channels

|icon:bullhorn[role=orange]
|*Markets* works to reach potential audiences

|icon:hands-helping[role=gold]
|*Supports* creators through the publication process
|===

=== icon:star[] {company-short} Definition

{company-name} is a publishing house that:

[cols="^1,2,4",options="header",frame=all,grid=all]
|===
|icon:tag[] |Action |Distinction

|icon:handshake[role=blue]
|*Partners with* creators
|Rather than acquiring from them

|icon:shield-alt[role=green]
|*Preserves* creator ownership
|Rather than taking IP

|icon:globe[role=purple]
|*Spans* all sectors and media types
|Not limited to books or single medium

|icon:hammer[role=orange]
|*Builds* infrastructure
|That enables publishing at scale

|icon:crown[role=gold]
|*Serves* Kingdom purposes
|Through publishing activity
|===

[NOTE]
.icon:info-circle[] Key Distinction
====
Traditional publishers often take ownership of intellectual property. {company-short} explicitly does *not*. This is not a minor policy difference — it is a *fundamental identity characteristic*.
====

== icon:user-shield[] The Creator-Centric Philosophy

=== icon:shield-alt[] Core Principle: Creators Keep Their IP

[.lead]
*This is non-negotiable.*

When a creator partners with {company-short}:

[cols="^1,4",frame=all,grid=all]
|===
|icon:check[role=green]
|Creator *retains full ownership* of their intellectual property

|icon:file-signature[role=blue]
|{company-short} receives *license to publish* (not ownership transfer)

|icon:paintbrush[role=purple]
|Creator maintains *creative control* over their work

|icon:door-open[role=orange]
|Creator can *exit* the partnership (per agreement terms)
|===

[IMPORTANT]
.icon:crown[] IP Ownership is Sacred
====
We never take intellectual property from creators. This is not just a policy — it is a *defining characteristic* of who {company-short} is.
====
