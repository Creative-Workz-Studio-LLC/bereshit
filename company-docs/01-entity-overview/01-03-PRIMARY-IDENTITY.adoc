== Document Purpose

This document establishes and explains Creative Workz Studio's *primary identity* as a publishing house. While CWS engages in multiple activities (including infrastructure building, technology development, and service provision), our foundational identity is that of a *publisher*—an entity that helps bring creative works into the world.

[IMPORTANT]
====
Understanding CWS as a publishing house first is essential. All other activities (technology development, infrastructure building) serve and support the publishing mission. This ordering is intentional and foundational.
====

'''

== What Is a Publishing House?

=== Traditional Definition

A publishing house is an organization that:

* *Acquires* creative works from creators
* *Produces* those works in distributable form
* *Distributes* works to audiences through various channels
* *Markets* works to reach potential audiences
* *Supports* creators through the publication process

=== CWS Definition

Creative Workz Studio is a publishing house that:

* *Partners with* creators (rather than acquiring from them)
* *Preserves* creator ownership (rather than taking IP)
* *Spans* all sectors and media types (not limited to books or single medium)
* *Builds* infrastructure that enables publishing at scale
* *Serves* Kingdom purposes through publishing activity

[NOTE]
====
The key distinction: Traditional publishers often take ownership of intellectual property. CWS explicitly does not. This is not a minor policy difference—it is a fundamental identity characteristic.
====

'''

== The Creator-Centric Philosophy

=== Core Principle: Creators Keep Their IP

[.lead]
*This is non-negotiable.*

When a creator partners with CWS:

* Creator *retains full ownership* of their intellectual property
* CWS receives *license to publish* (not ownership transfer)
* Creator maintains *creative control* over their work
* Creator can *exit* the partnership (per agreement terms)

=== Why This Matters

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Stakeholder |Benefit

|*For Creators*
|Security, long-term value retention, creative freedom, multiple partnership options

|*For CWS*
|Attracts best creators (who won't sign away rights), builds trust, aligns with values

|*For Audiences*
|Creators maintain vision integrity; works aren't diluted by publisher control

|*For Kingdom*
|Honors biblical principles of ownership and stewardship; doesn't exploit creators
|===

=== Theological Basis

[quote, Psalm 24:1]
____
The earth is the LORD's, and the fulness thereof; the world, and they that dwell therein.
____

* God is ultimate owner of all things
* Humans are stewards, not ultimate owners
* But stewardship implies responsibility and authority over what's entrusted
* Taking IP from creators violates their God-given stewardship
* CWS supports stewardship rather than usurping it

'''

== Quality Standards

=== What We Publish

CWS publishes works that are:

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Standard |Meaning

|*Excellent*
|High quality in craft, execution, and presentation

|*Valuable*
|Genuinely useful, entertaining, or meaningful to audiences

|*Aligned*
|Consistent with CWS values and mission

|*Honest*
|Truthful, not deceptive or manipulative

|*Complete*
|Finished, not half-done or abandoned

|*Legal*
|Properly rights-cleared, not infringing
|===

=== What We Don't Publish

CWS will not publish works that:

* Contradict core biblical truths
* Exploit or harm audiences
* Violate intellectual property rights
* Misrepresent themselves
* Would damage CWS reputation or mission
* Creator cannot stand behind

[NOTE]
====
Values alignment does not mean every work must be explicitly Christian content. Excellent work in any genre can honor the Creator. But works that actively contradict Kingdom values are outside our scope.
====
