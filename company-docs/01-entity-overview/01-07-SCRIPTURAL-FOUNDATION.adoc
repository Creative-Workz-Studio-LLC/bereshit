== Primary Foundation: Genesis 1:1

=== The Verse

[quote, Genesis 1:1 (KJV)]
____
In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth.
____

[quote, Genesis 1:1 (Hebrew transliteration)]
____
Bereshit bara Elohim et hashamayim ve'et ha'aretz.
____

=== Why This Verse

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Aspect |Significance

|*Name Origin*
|"Bereshit" — our technology stack is named from this verse

|*Creation Pattern*
|God is Creator; creation is intentional, ordered, good

|*Existence Foundation*
|Everything that exists derives from God's creative act

|*Work Model*
|We create because He created; our work mirrors His
|===

=== Theological Implications

==== God as Creator

* God exists before creation
* Creation is deliberate, not accidental
* Creation has purpose and meaning
* The Creator is distinct from creation

==== Creation as Pattern

* Creation follows order (days 1-6)
* Creation moves from formless to formed
* Creation is evaluated ("it was good")
* Creation has structure and hierarchy

==== Image-Bearers Create

[quote, Genesis 1:27 (KJV)]
____
So God created man in his own image, in the image of God created he him; male and female created he them.
____

As image-bearers:

* We reflect God's creative nature
* Our creativity is derived, not original
* Creating is part of our design
* Excellent creation honors the Creator

'''

== The Ternary Pattern: Genesis 1:6

=== The Verse

[quote, Genesis 1:6-7 (KJV)]
____
And God said, Let there be a firmament in the midst of the waters, and let it divide the waters from the waters. And God made the firmament, and divided the waters which were under the firmament from the waters which were above the firmament: and it was so.
____

=== The Pattern

The firmament creates a *ternary* (three-part) structure:

[source]
----
Waters Above    (+1)  — Heaven, spiritual realm
    │
Firmament       (0)   — The dividing point, the center
    │
Waters Below    (-1)  — Earth, physical realm
----

=== Application to CWS Architecture

==== Filesystem Structure

[cols="1,1,2",options="header"]
|===
|Directory |Value |Meaning

|*tov/*
|+1
|Complete, validated, "it was good"

|*word/*
|0
|Source of truth, the spoken word, definitions

|*void/*
|-1
|Formless, planning, awaiting form
|===

==== Balanced Ternary

The ternary pattern extends to our mathematical foundations:

* *-1* — Negative, break down, below
* *0* — Neutral, anchor, center
* *+1* — Positive, build up, above

This provides a true center (0) that binary systems lack.

==== Health Scoring

[cols="1,1,2",options="header"]
|===
|Range |Status |Meaning

|Above +50
|Granted (Good)
|Healthy, proceed

|±50
|Deferred (Neutral)
|Needs attention

|Below -50
|Denied (Bad)
|Broken, stop
|===

'''

== The Word: John 1:1-3

=== The Verses

[quote, John 1:1-3 (KJV)]
____
In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God. The same was in the beginning with God. All things were made by him; and without him was not any thing made that was made.
____

=== Theological Significance

==== Christ as Creator

* The Word (Logos) is Christ
* Christ was present at creation
* All things were made *through* Him
* Nothing exists apart from His creative power

==== Implication for CWS

* Our technology exists because Christ created the conditions for its existence
* We cannot claim autonomous creation
* Our "making" is derivative of His making
* Everything we build is under His sovereignty

==== The "word/" Directory

Our source of truth directory is named "word" because:

* God spoke creation into existence ("God said...")
* The Word is the agent of creation
* Definitions and specifications are our "spoken word" that creates structure
* word/ is where truth lives in our system

'''

== Writing the Vision: Habakkuk 2:2-3

=== The Verses

[quote, Habakkuk 2:2-3 (KJV)]
____
And the LORD answered me, and said, Write the vision, and make it plain upon tables, that he may run that readeth it. For the vision is yet for an appointed time, but at the end it shall speak, and not lie: though it tarry, wait for it; because it will surely come, it will not tarry.
____

=== Application to CWS

|===

|*"Write the vision"*
|Document everything — mission, vision, architecture, decisions

|*"Make it plain"*
|Clear, accessible documentation anyone can understand

|*"Upon tables"*
|Persistent, durable record (not just in heads)

|*"That he may run that readeth it"*
|Others can execute based on documentation alone
|===

==== Timing Trust

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Instruction |CWS Application

|*"For an appointed time"*
|The vision has God's timing, not ours

|*"Though it tarry, wait for it"*
|Patient building, not rushing ahead

|*"It will surely come"*
|Trust in ultimate fulfillment

|*"Will not tarry"*
|When the time comes, it comes fully
|===

==== Documentation as Obedience

Our extensive documentation isn't bureaucratic overhead — it's obedience to "write the vision and make it plain."
