=== icon:palette[] "Creative"

[sidebar]
.icon:book-open[] Etymology
****
[horizontal]
*Definition*:: Having the quality or power of creating; marked by the ability to create

*Root*:: From Latin _creāre_ ("to make, bring forth, produce")

*Hebrew*:: בָּרָא (_bara_) — to create, as used in Genesis 1:1
****

==== icon:cross[] Theological Significance

[quote, Genesis 1:1]

____

[quote, Genesis 1:27]

So God *created* man in his own image, in the image of God *created* he him; male and female *created* he them.
____

=== icon:hammer[] "Workz"

[sidebar]
.icon:book-open[] Etymology
****
[horizontal]
*Definition*:: Labor, effort, and output — with intentional stylistic distinction

*Note*:: The 'z' replaces 's' deliberately, marking distinction from corporate sterility

*Why 'z'?*:: Visual uniqueness, memorable spelling, trademark strength
****

==== icon:cross[] Theological Significance

[quote, James 2:17-18]
____
Even so faith, if it hath not *works*, is dead, being alone. Yea, a man may say, Thou hast faith, and I have *works*: shew me thy faith without thy *works*, and I will shew thee my faith by my *works*.
____

[quote, Ephesians 2:10]
____
For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus unto good *works*, which God hath before ordained that we should walk in them.
____

*Key Principles:*

* *Faith Produces Works* — Genuine faith manifests in action
* *Works Are Prepared* — God ordained the works we would do before we existed
* *Works Demonstrate Faith* — Our output reveals what we believe
* *We Are His Workmanship* — We ourselves are God's creative work

==== The 'Z' Distinction

The spelling "Workz" rather than "Works" is intentional:

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Aspect |Purpose

|*Visual Distinction*
|Immediately signals this is different from typical corporate entities

|*Phonetic Identity*
|Same pronunciation, memorable spelling

|*Cultural Signal*
|Approachable, not stuffy or pretentious

|*Trademark Strength*
|More distinctive for intellectual property protection

|*Kingdom Marker*
|We're in the world but not of it—even in spelling
|===

==== icon:pencil-ruler[] Logo Principles

The CWS logo should embody:

* *Simplicity* — Recognizable at small sizes, clean lines
* *Meaning* — Visual elements that reflect identity, not arbitrary design
* *Versatility* — Works in color, black/white, various backgrounds
* *Timelessness* — Avoids trends that will date quickly
* *Distinctiveness* — Memorable and unique
