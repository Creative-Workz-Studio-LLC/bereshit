== Chapter Purpose

This chapter documents *what Creative Workz Studio builds*—the internal intellectual properties, technology infrastructure, and products that CWS creates. While <<../02-publishing-house-model/02-00-PUBLISHING-HOUSE-MODEL.adoc#,Chapter 02>> established CWS as a publishing house that serves external creatives, this chapter focuses on CWS's own creative output.

[NOTE]
====
Building is CWS's *secondary identity* (infrastructure builder) in service of the *primary identity* (publishing house). What we build enables our publishing mission and serves the Kingdom.
====

'''
