== icon:ban[] Anti-Patterns

=== icon:landmark[] Empire Building

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Empire Building
****
*What It Is:*

Building {company-short} into a large, powerful, central entity — growth for growth's sake, accumulation of influence and control.

*Why It Violates Service:*

Empire building serves {company-short} ambition, not community need. It reverses the proper relationship — community serves empire instead of empire serves community.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Decisions driven by "what makes {company-short} bigger"
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Reluctance to empower others who might "compete"
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Hoarding resources instead of sharing
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Measuring success by {company-short} size rather than community health
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Taking credit for community achievements
****

=== icon:money-bill-alt[] Extraction

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Extraction
****
*What It Is:*

Taking more value from community than providing — using relationships primarily to benefit {company-short}.

*Why It Violates Service:*

Service gives; extraction takes. Extraction treats community as resource to mine rather than people to serve.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Pricing that maximizes revenue rather than serves access
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Partnership terms heavily favoring {company-short}
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Marketing that manipulates rather than informs
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Support that minimizes cost rather than maximizes help
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Relationships valued by what {company-short} gains
****

=== icon:calculator[] Transactional Relationships

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Transactional Mindset
****
*What It Is:*

Treating relationships as mere exchanges — "what do I get for what I give" calculation.

*Why It Violates Service:*

Service flows from love, not calculation. Transactional mindset reduces people to utilities.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Evaluating relationships by ROI
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Ending relationships when benefit decreases
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Selective service based on partner value
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Reluctance to help when benefit unclear
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Community seen as customer base, not family
****

'''

== icon:question-circle[] Decision Test

=== icon:check[] The Question

[IMPORTANT]
.icon:exclamation-circle[] The Service Test
====
*"Does this serve others?"*

This is the simple test for every decision, project, feature, relationship, and action.
====

=== icon:clipboard-check[] Applying the Test

[cols="^1,2,4",options="header",frame=all,grid=all]
|===
|icon:tag[] |Situation |How to Apply

|icon:code[role=blue]
|*New Feature*
|Who is served by this? How are they better off?

|icon:handshake[role=green]
|*Partnership*
|Does this help them succeed, or just benefit us?

|icon:tags[role=purple]
|*Pricing*
|Does this price serve accessibility, or just maximize revenue?

|icon:file-alt[role=orange]
|*Policy*
|Who does this serve? Does it serve community or just {company-short}?

|icon:comments[role=gold]
|*Communication*
|Is this helpful to them, or just promotional for us?

|===

=== icon:times-circle[] When the Answer Is "No"

If a decision doesn't clearly serve others:

. icon:pause-circle[role=blue] *Pause* — Don't proceed automatically
. icon:search[role=green] *Examine* — Is there a way to reframe to serve?
. icon:question[role=purple] *Reconsider* — Should this be done at all?
. icon:edit[role=orange] *Adjust* — Modify to genuinely serve
. icon:ban[role=red] *Decline* — If it can't serve, don't do it

'''
