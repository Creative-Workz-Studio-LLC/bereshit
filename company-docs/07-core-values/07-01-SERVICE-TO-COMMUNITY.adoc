== Document Purpose

This document establishes *Service to Community* as CWS's first core value — the foundational commitment that everything we build exists to serve others, not ourselves.

== Anti-Patterns

=== Empire Building

[sidebar]
****
*What It Is:*

Building CWS into a large, powerful, central entity — growth for growth's sake, accumulation of influence and control.

*Why It Violates Service:*

Empire building serves CWS ambition, not community need. It reverses the proper relationship — community serves empire instead of empire serves community.

*Warning Signs:*

* Decisions driven by "what makes CWS bigger"
* Reluctance to empower others who might "compete"
* Hoarding resources instead of sharing
* Measuring success by CWS size rather than community health
* Taking credit for community achievements
****

=== Extraction

[sidebar]
****
*What It Is:*

Taking more value from community than providing — using relationships primarily to benefit CWS.

*Why It Violates Service:*

Service gives; extraction takes. Extraction treats community as resource to mine rather than people to serve.

*Warning Signs:*

* Pricing that maximizes revenue rather than serves access
* Partnership terms heavily favoring CWS
* Marketing that manipulates rather than informs
* Support that minimizes cost rather than maximizes help
* Relationships valued by what CWS gains
****

=== Transactional Relationships

[sidebar]
****
*What It Is:*

Treating relationships as mere exchanges — "what do I get for what I give" calculation.

*Why It Violates Service:*

Service flows from love, not calculation. Transactional mindset reduces people to utilities.

*Warning Signs:*

* Evaluating relationships by ROI
* Ending relationships when benefit decreases
* Selective service based on partner value
* Reluctance to help when benefit unclear
* Community seen as customer base, not family
****

== Decision Test

=== The Question

[IMPORTANT]
====
*"Does this serve others?"*

This is the simple test for every decision, project, feature, relationship, and action.
====

=== Applying the Test

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Situation |How to Apply

|*New Feature*
|Who is served by this? How are they better off?

|*Partnership*
|Does this help them succeed, or just benefit us?

|*Pricing*
|Does this price serve accessibility, or just maximize revenue?

|*Policy*
|Who does this serve? Does it serve community or just CWS?

|*Communication*
|Is this helpful to them, or just promotional for us?

|===

=== When the Answer Is "No"

If a decision doesn't clearly serve others:

. *Pause* — Don't proceed automatically
. *Examine* — Is there a way to reframe to serve?
. *Reconsider* — Should this be done at all?
. *Adjust* — Modify to genuinely serve
. *Decline* — If it can't serve, don't do it

'''

== Tensions and Balance

=== Service vs. Sustainability

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Tension |Resolution

|*Give everything away?*
|No — unsustainable service serves no one long-term

|*Charge nothing?*
|No — fair exchange enables ongoing service

|*Ignore CWS needs?*
|No — healthy CWS serves better than depleted CWS

|*Never say no?*
|No — focused service beats scattered service

|===

=== The Balance

[IMPORTANT]
====
*Service is the orientation, not the annihilation of self.* CWS serves by being healthy enough to serve well, sustained enough to serve long-term, and focused enough to serve effectively.
====
