== icon:ban[] Anti-Patterns

=== icon:puzzle-piece[] Complexity Worship

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Complexity Worship
****
*What It Is:*

Valuing complexity for its own sake — creating unnecessarily complex systems, using jargon when plain language works, designing for experts when regular people could be served.

*Why It Violates Accessibility:*

Complexity worship excludes people. It creates barriers. It confuses difficulty with quality. It serves ego, not users.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Pride in complexity
* icon:times-circle[role=red] "It's supposed to be hard"
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Jargon used when simpler terms exist
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Resistance to simplification
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Dismissing accessibility as "dumbing down"
****

=== icon:lock[] Gatekeeping

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Gatekeeping
****
*What It Is:*

Maintaining barriers that keep people out — whether through required expertise, exclusive language, insider knowledge, or intentional obscurity.

*Why It Violates Accessibility:*

Gatekeeping contradicts Kingdom values. Jesus criticized those who "shut up the kingdom of heaven against men" (Matthew 23:13). Knowledge hoarded is knowledge wasted.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] "You have to earn access"
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Insider language maintained
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Documentation intentionally sparse
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Community exclusive rather than welcoming
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Help conditional on proving worthiness
****

=== icon:user-graduate[] Expert Assumptions

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Expert Assumptions
****
*What It Is:*

Assuming users have expertise they don't have — skipping foundational explanation, using unexplained jargon, building on assumed knowledge.

*Why It Violates Accessibility:*

Expert assumptions exclude the people who most need what we build. They create invisible barriers. They mistake exclusion for quality.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Documentation starting at advanced level
* icon:times-circle[role=red] No glossary or explanation of terms
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Prerequisites unstated
* icon:times-circle[role=red] "This is obvious"
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Impatience with basic questions
****

'''
