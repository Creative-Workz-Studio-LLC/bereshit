== Document Purpose

This document establishes *Creator Autonomy* as CWS's eighth core value — the non-negotiable commitment that creators keep ownership of their work, maintaining control over what they create.

[.lead]
*"Creators keep their work."*

[quote, 1 Corinthians 3:8 (KJV)]
____
Now he that planteth and he that watereth are one: and every man shall receive his own reward according to his own labour.
____

== Anti-Patterns

=== Ownership Taking

[sidebar]
****
*What It Is:*

Acquiring ownership of creator's work — whether through contract terms, work-for-hire structures, or gradual accumulation of rights.

*Why It Violates Autonomy:*

Taking ownership takes control. It transfers the fruit of labor from creator to publisher. It inverts the proper relationship.

*Warning Signs:*

* Contract clauses transferring ownership
* "We need to own this for protection"
* Gradual accumulation of rights
* IP as payment for services
* Work-for-hire as default
****

=== Control Without Ownership

[sidebar]
****
*What It Is:*

Maintaining effective control over work without legal ownership — through exclusive agreements, platform lock-in, or information asymmetry.

*Why It Violates Autonomy:*

Control without ownership is ownership in effect. Creator may technically own, but can't actually exercise ownership.

*Warning Signs:*

* Exclusive agreements that prevent alternative action
* Platform dependencies that lock in
* Information hoarded that creator needs
* Processes that require CWS involvement
* Contracts with onerous terms for exercise of rights
****

=== Dependency Creation

[sidebar]
****
*What It Is:*

Making creators dependent on CWS — whether through knowledge withholding, relationship gatekeeping, or infrastructure lock-in.

*Why It Violates Autonomy:*

True autonomy includes ability to function independently. Dependency undermines autonomy even when ownership is technically retained.

*Warning Signs:*

* Knowledge not transferred
* Relationships mediated through CWS
* Infrastructure that only works with CWS
* Skills not developed in partner
* "They can't do this without us"
****

== Tensions and Balance

=== Autonomy vs. Quality

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Tension |Resolution

|*Creator wants something that damages work?*
|Advise honestly; ultimately their decision

|*Quality standards not met?*
|CWS can decline partnership; can't force changes

|*Creative direction CWS disagrees with?*
|Their work, their choice — CWS can advise

|*What if it reflects badly on CWS?*
|Separate reputation; creator decisions are theirs

|===

=== The Balance

CWS maintains standards through partnership selection, not through control. Once partnered, creator autonomy is protected. CWS advises but doesn't dictate. If values misalign fundamentally, partnership ends graciously — but work stays with creator.
