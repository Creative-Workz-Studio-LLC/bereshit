This document establishes *Connective Tissue, Not Center* as {company-short}'s third core value — the commitment to enabling and connecting rather than controlling, serving as infrastructure for the Body rather than becoming its center.

{company-short} is *ligaments, not the brain*. We connect and enable the Body of Christ to function, but we don't control it. Christ is the head; we serve the joints.

== icon:ban[] Anti-Patterns

=== icon:landmark[] Empire Building

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Empire Building
****
*What It Is:*

Growing {company-short} into a large, central, controlling organization — seeking power, influence, and growth for {company-short} itself.

*Why It Violates Connective Tissue:*

Empires position themselves at the center. Everything flows through them. They accumulate power rather than distribute it. They make others dependent rather than independent.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Decisions driven by "what grows {company-short}"
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Reluctance to empower potential "competitors"
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Centralized architecture when distributed is possible
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Information hoarded for strategic advantage
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Relationships mediated through {company-short} when direct is possible
****

=== icon:lock[] Control

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Control
****
*What It Is:*

Grasping for control over community, partners, or outcomes — being a gatekeeper rather than an enabler.

*Why It Violates Connective Tissue:*

Control centralizes power in {company-short}. It makes others dependent. It positions {company-short} as necessary intermediary. It replaces Christ's headship with human control.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Approval required for community actions
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Information controlled through {company-short}
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Connections mediated rather than direct
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Policies that create dependency
* icon:times-circle[role=red] "We need to maintain control of..."
****

=== icon:link[] Indispensability

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Indispensability
****
*What It Is:*

Making {company-short} necessary for community to function — designing systems and relationships where {company-short} removal would cause collapse.

*Why It Violates Connective Tissue:*

Indispensability is a form of control. True connective tissue strengthens the body; removing it might reduce function but shouldn't cause collapse.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Single points of failure at {company-short}
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Partners unable to function without {company-short}
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Knowledge concentrated in {company-short} alone
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Infrastructure that only works through {company-short}
* icon:times-circle[role=red] "They can't do it without us"
****

'''
