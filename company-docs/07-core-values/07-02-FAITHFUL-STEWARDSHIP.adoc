== Anti-Patterns

=== Hoarding

[sidebar]
****
*What It Is:*

Accumulating resources beyond need, holding back from use or sharing out of fear or greed.

*Why It Violates Stewardship:*

The servant who buried the talent was condemned. Resources are given to use, not to hide. Hoarding trusts reserves more than God.

*Warning Signs:*

* Excessive reserves beyond prudent levels
* Reluctance to invest in mission opportunities
* Fear-driven financial decisions
* "We might need this someday" without specific purpose
* Knowledge kept instead of shared
****

=== Recklessness

[sidebar]
****
*What It Is:*

Careless use of resources, spending without wisdom, launching without readiness, taking unnecessary risks.

*Why It Violates Stewardship:*

Stewards are accountable. Recklessness wastes what's entrusted, damages capacity to serve, and breaks trust.

*Warning Signs:*

* Spending without clear purpose
* Launching before ready
* Ignoring financial constraints
* Overcommitting resources
* Presuming on future provision
****

=== Impatience

[sidebar]
****
*What It Is:*

Forcing action before right timing, borrowing to accelerate, manufacturing opportunities that don't exist naturally.

*Why It Violates Stewardship:*

Faithful stewardship includes timing wisdom. Impatience trusts human effort over God's timing, often resulting in debt, burnout, or misalignment.

*Warning Signs:*

* "We need to move faster" without clear reason
* Taking on debt to accelerate growth
* Forcing partnerships that aren't ready
* Launching before foundations are solid
* Anxiety driving decisions
****

=== Neglect

[sidebar]
****
*What It Is:*

Failing to maintain, develop, or use what's been entrusted — letting resources decay through inattention.

*Why It Violates Stewardship:*

What's entrusted should be cared for. Neglect wastes through decay what hoarding wastes through disuse.

*Warning Signs:*

* Code unmaintained and deteriorating
* Documentation outdated
* Relationships left unattended
* Skills undeveloped
* Opportunities ignored
****

== Decision Test

=== The Question

[IMPORTANT]
====
*"Are we being faithful stewards?"*

This question tests whether we're managing what's entrusted wisely, using it when given, neither hoarding nor wasting.
====

=== Applying the Test

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Situation |How to Apply

|*Major Purchase*
|Is this wise use? Is the timing right? Is this mission-aligned?

|*New Initiative*
|Do we have resources to do this well? Is now the time?

|*Opportunity*
|Is this for us? Do we have capacity? Is timing right?

|*Reserve Decision*
|Is this prudent or hoarding? What's the right level?

|*Maintenance*
|Are we caring for what we have before acquiring more?

|===

=== When Uncertain

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Uncertainty |Approach

|*Resources unclear*
|Clarify before committing

|*Timing uncertain*
|Wait and watch for clearer signals

|*Opportunity ambiguous*
|Test small before full commitment

|*Risk assessment*
|Count the cost before building

|===

'''
