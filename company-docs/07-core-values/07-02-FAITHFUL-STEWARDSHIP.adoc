[.lead]
This document establishes *Faithful Stewardship* as {company-short}'s second core value — the commitment to managing all resources as entrusted gifts, building with what's given when it's given.

[sidebar]
.icon:star[] Why This Value Matters
****
We are *managers, not owners*. Everything — time, talent, resources, relationships — belongs to God and is entrusted to us for Kingdom purposes. This value shapes how we handle every resource.
****

== icon:bullseye[] Document Purpose

[.lead]
*"Build with what's given, when it's given."*

[quote, 1 Corinthians 4:2 (KJV)]
____
Moreover it is required in stewards, that a man be found faithful.
____

== icon:ban[] Anti-Patterns

=== icon:archive[] Hoarding

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Hoarding
****
*What It Is:*

Accumulating resources beyond need, holding back from use or sharing out of fear or greed.

*Why It Violates Stewardship:*

The servant who buried the talent was condemned. Resources are given to use, not to hide. Hoarding trusts reserves more than God.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Excessive reserves beyond prudent levels
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Reluctance to invest in mission opportunities
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Fear-driven financial decisions
* icon:times-circle[role=red] "We might need this someday" without specific purpose
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Knowledge kept instead of shared
****

=== icon:bomb[] Recklessness

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Recklessness
****
*What It Is:*

Careless use of resources, spending without wisdom, launching without readiness, taking unnecessary risks.

*Why It Violates Stewardship:*

Stewards are accountable. Recklessness wastes what's entrusted, damages capacity to serve, and breaks trust.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Spending without clear purpose
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Launching before ready
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Ignoring financial constraints
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Overcommitting resources
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Presuming on future provision
****

=== icon:running[] Impatience

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Impatience
****
*What It Is:*

Forcing action before right timing, borrowing to accelerate, manufacturing opportunities that don't exist naturally.

*Why It Violates Stewardship:*

Faithful stewardship includes timing wisdom. Impatience trusts human effort over God's timing, often resulting in debt, burnout, or misalignment.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] "We need to move faster" without clear reason
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Taking on debt to accelerate growth
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Forcing partnerships that aren't ready
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Launching before foundations are solid
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Anxiety driving decisions
****

=== icon:trash-alt[] Neglect

[sidebar]
.icon:exclamation-triangle[] Warning: Neglect
****
*What It Is:*

Failing to maintain, develop, or use what's been entrusted — letting resources decay through inattention.

*Why It Violates Stewardship:*

What's entrusted should be cared for. Neglect wastes through decay what hoarding wastes through disuse.

*Warning Signs:*

* icon:times-circle[role=red] Code unmaintained and deteriorating
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Documentation outdated
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Relationships left unattended
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Skills undeveloped
* icon:times-circle[role=red] Opportunities ignored
****
