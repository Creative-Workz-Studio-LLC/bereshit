== Apparent Value Conflicts

=== When Values Seem to Conflict

Sometimes values appear to conflict. Most conflicts are apparent, not real:

[cols="1,2,3",options="header"]
|===
|Apparent Conflict |Seems Like |Resolution

|*Service vs. Stewardship*
|"Give everything away" vs. "Be wise with resources"
|Sustainable service serves more than depleted giving

|*Autonomy vs. Quality*
|"Let creators decide" vs. "Maintain standards"
|Standards through partnership selection, not control

|*Accessible vs. Excellence*
|"Simple" vs. "Excellent"
|True excellence includes accessibility; simplicity is hard

|*Service vs. Autonomy*
|"Help them" vs. "Let them decide"
|Serve by advising; respect autonomy in final decision

|*Teaching vs. Doing*
|"Time spent teaching" vs. "Time spent building"
|Teaching embedded in making serves both

|===

=== Resolution Process

When values seem to conflict:

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Step |Action

|*1. Clarify*
|Ensure both values are correctly understood

|*2. Examine*
|Is conflict real or misunderstanding?

|*3. Context*
|What does wisdom say in this specific situation?

|*4. Counsel*
|Seek input from others who hold these values

|*5. Decide*
|Make best decision with full consideration

|*6. Document*
|Record reasoning for future reference

|===

=== Value Priority

When genuine conflict exists (rare), consider:

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Priority |Reasoning

|*Reverence for Creator*
|Foundational — grounds all others

|*Service to Community*
|Core purpose — why CWS exists

|*Creator Autonomy*
|Non-negotiable — defines publishing house

|*Connective Tissue*
|Structural — shapes how we operate

|*Faithful Stewardship*
|Sustainability — enables continued service

|*Teaching/Accessible*
|Methodological — how we work

|*Trade/Mutual Support*
|Relational — community economics

|===

== Practical Scenarios

=== Scenario 1: Profitable But Misaligned Partnership

[sidebar]
****
*Situation:*

A potential partner offers significant revenue but their values don't align with CWS values.

*Values Test:*

* Service to Community: ✗ Would this serve Kingdom community?
* Reverence for Creator: ✗ Does this honor God?
* Others: May pass

*Decision:*

*DECLINE.* Failing even one value test means the opportunity is not for CWS, regardless of revenue potential.

*Reasoning:*

Values aren't for sale. Short-term revenue from misalignment damages long-term mission. Trust the process.
****

=== Scenario 2: Creator Wants Quality-Compromising Decision

[sidebar]
****
*Situation:*

A creative partner wants to publish work that doesn't meet CWS quality standards.

*Values Test:*

* Creator Autonomy: ✓ Their work, their decision
* Service to Community: ? Does low-quality work serve?
* Teaching: ? Does this set good example?

*Decision:*

*ADVISE, RESPECT, POSSIBLY SEPARATE.* Advise honestly about quality concerns. Ultimately respect creator decision. If quality is too low for CWS association, end partnership graciously — but work stays with creator.

*Reasoning:*

Autonomy is protected; CWS can advise and can separate, but can't control.
****

=== Scenario 3: Growth Opportunity Requiring Complexity

[sidebar]
****
*Situation:*

A growth opportunity requires building something more complex than regular people can understand.

*Values Test:*

* Accessible by Design: ✗ Complex, expert-only
* Teaching Through Making: ? Can complexity still teach?
* Service to Community: ? Does this serve regular people?

*Decision:*

*FIND ACCESSIBLE PATH OR DECLINE.* Look for ways to make it accessible. If accessibility is impossible, this may not be for CWS. Some legitimate work is expert-only; CWS focus is accessibility.

*Reasoning:*

Accessibility is core to CWS identity. Growth at expense of accessibility isn't CWS growth.
****

=== Scenario 4: Partner Struggling Financially

[sidebar]
****
*Situation:*

A valued, aligned partner is struggling financially and asking for accommodation.

*Values Test:*

* Service to Community: ✓ Serving partner in difficulty
* Trade and Mutual Support: ✓ This is what mutual support means
* Faithful Stewardship: ? Must balance with CWS sustainability

*Decision:*

*SUPPORT WITHIN SUSTAINABILITY.* Find ways to accommodate that serve the partner without compromising CWS sustainability. Use trade, adjusted terms, patience.

*Reasoning:*

Mutual support means helping through difficulty. But unsustainable help serves no one long-term.
****

== When Values Are Tested

=== Pressure Points

Values face testing under certain conditions:

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Pressure |Values Tested

|*Financial stress*
|Stewardship, Service, Trade

|*Growth opportunity*
|Autonomy, Control, Accessibility

|*Partner conflict*
|Service, Autonomy, Support

|*Time pressure*
|Teaching, Quality, Stewardship

|*External criticism*
|All values — do we stand firm?

|===

=== Holding the Line

[IMPORTANT]
====
*Values aren't values until tested.* Anyone can hold values when easy. CWS commits to holding values under pressure — this is where values become real.
====
