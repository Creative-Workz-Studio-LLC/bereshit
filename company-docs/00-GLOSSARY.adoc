== Identity Terms

[[term-cws]]
CWS::
Abbreviation for *Creative Workz Studio LLC*. Used throughout documentation for brevity.
+
See: <<01-entity-overview/01-00-ENTITY-OVERVIEW.adoc#,Chapter 01: Entity Overview>>

[[term-publishing-house]]
Publishing House::
CWS's *primary identity*. A publishing house helps creators bring their work to market while they retain ownership. Unlike traditional publishers, CWS never takes intellectual property from creators.
+
See: <<01-entity-overview/01-03-PRIMARY-IDENTITY.adoc#,01-03: Primary Identity>>

[[term-infrastructure-builder]]
Infrastructure Builder::
CWS's *secondary identity*. The technology and systems CWS builds to enable its publishing mission and serve the Kingdom.
+
See: <<01-entity-overview/01-04-SECONDARY-IDENTITY.adoc#,01-04: Secondary Identity>>

[[term-kingdom-technology]]
Kingdom Technology::
Technology built on biblical foundations for Kingdom purposes. Designed for independence from worldly systems and long-term service through the Millennium.
+
See: <<03-what-cws-builds/03-00-WHAT-CWS-BUILDS.adoc#,Chapter 03: What CWS Builds>>

== Technology Terms

[[term-bereshit]]
Bereshit::
(בְּרֵאשִׁית) Hebrew for "In the beginning" — the first word of Genesis 1:1. The name of CWS's complete technology stack, built from first principles following creation patterns.
+
See: <<03-what-cws-builds/03-01-BERESHIT-STACK-OVERVIEW.adoc#,03-01: Bereshit Stack Overview>>

[[term-bereshit-stack]]
Bereshit Stack::
The complete L0-L5 technology architecture CWS builds. Layers include: Universal (L0), OmniCode (L1), Platform (L2), CPI-SI (L3), FaithNet (L4), and Applications (L5).
+
See: <<03-what-cws-builds/03-01-BERESHIT-STACK-OVERVIEW.adoc#,03-01: Bereshit Stack Overview>>

[[term-libtrit]]
libtrit::
The *L0 Universal layer* of the Bereshit stack. Provides balanced ternary mathematics, core types, and Bible rail validation.
+
See: <<03-what-cws-builds/03-02-FOUNDATION-LAYERS.adoc#,03-02: Foundation Layers>>

[[term-omnicode]]
OmniCode::
The *L1 Language layer* of the Bereshit stack. A universal programming language with sentence-based syntax readable by non-programmers. Compiles to multiple target languages.
+
See: <<03-what-cws-builds/03-02-FOUNDATION-LAYERS.adoc#,03-02: Foundation Layers>>

[[term-millenniumos]]
MillenniumOS::
The *L2 Platform layer* operating system. A bare-metal OS providing true platform independence, named for its intended service through Christ's Millennial reign.
+
See: <<03-what-cws-builds/03-03-PLATFORM-LAYER.adoc#,03-03: Platform Layer>>

[[term-bereshit-filesystem]]
Bereshit Filesystem::
The *L2 Platform layer* filesystem. Features ternary health monitoring and the void/word/tov directory structure based on Genesis creation patterns.
+
See: <<03-what-cws-builds/03-03-PLATFORM-LAYER.adoc#,03-03: Platform Layer>>

[[term-cpi-si]]
CPI-SI::
*Created Purposeful Intelligence — Skilled Imagebearers*. The *L3 Cognitive layer* framework for AI systems. Provides theological grounding (AI as created, not autonomous), skill composition, and learning loops.
+
See: <<03-what-cws-builds/03-04-COGNITIVE-LAYER.adoc#,03-04: Cognitive Layer>>

[[term-faithnet]]
FaithNet::
The *L4 Network layer*. Decentralized peer-to-peer networking infrastructure enabling mark-free communication and commerce.
+
See: <<03-what-cws-builds/03-05-NETWORK-LAYER.adoc#,03-05: Network Layer>>

[[term-cornerstone]]
Cornerstone::
The *L5 Applications layer* game engine. Named after Christ as the cornerstone (Ephesians 2:20), it provides the foundation for Kingdom-aligned games and entertainment.
+
See: <<03-what-cws-builds/03-06-APPLICATIONS-LAYER.adoc#,03-06: Applications Layer>>

== Architectural Terms

[[term-balanced-ternary]]
Balanced Ternary::
A numeral system using three values: *-1, 0, +1* (instead of binary's 0 and 1). Based on the Genesis 1:6 pattern of the firmament dividing the waters. Provides a true center (0) that binary lacks.
+
See: <<03-what-cws-builds/03-02-FOUNDATION-LAYERS.adoc#,03-02: Foundation Layers>>

[[term-void-word-tov]]
void/word/tov::
The three-directory structure based on Genesis creation patterns:
+
--
* *void/* (-1): Formless, raw, potential — like "tohu va-vohu" (Genesis 1:2)
* *word/* (0): Defined, structured, source of truth — like God speaking creation
* *tov/* (+1): Complete, verified, good — like "it was good" (Genesis 1:31)
--
+
See: <<03-what-cws-builds/03-03-PLATFORM-LAYER.adoc#,03-03: Platform Layer>>

[[term-bible-rail]]
Bible Rail::
A validation system using Scripture addressing. Provides canonical reference to Bible verses, validates references, and grounds technical work in Scripture.
+
See: <<03-what-cws-builds/03-02-FOUNDATION-LAYERS.adoc#,03-02: Foundation Layers>>

[[term-genesis-patterns]]
Genesis Patterns::
Architectural patterns derived from the creation account in Genesis 1. CWS uses these divine patterns rather than arbitrary human conventions.
+
See: <<03-what-cws-builds/03-08-BUILD-PRINCIPLES.adoc#,03-08: Build Principles>>

'''

== Business Terms

[[term-ip-retention]]
IP Retention::
The *non-negotiable principle* that creators keep ownership of their intellectual property when partnering with CWS. CWS provides services for revenue share but never takes ownership rights.
+
See: <<02-publishing-house-model/02-02-EXTERNAL-CREATIVES.adoc#,02-02: External Creatives>>

[[term-three-pillars]]
Three Pillars::
The three components of CWS's publishing house model:
+
--
* *Pillar One*: External Creatives (partners who retain IP)
* *Pillar Two*: Internal IPs (CWS-owned properties)
* *Pillar Three*: Collaboration Network (partnerships and trade)
--
+
See: <<02-publishing-house-model/02-00-PUBLISHING-HOUSE-MODEL.adoc#,02-00: Publishing House Model>>

[[term-trade-network]]
Trade Network::
A network of Kingdom-aligned businesses enabling commerce through barter, trade credits, and reciprocal services — independent of beast-system financial infrastructure.
+
See: <<02-publishing-house-model/02-04-COLLABORATION-NETWORK.adoc#,02-04: Collaboration Network>>

[[term-support-levels]]
Support Levels::
The tiers of service CWS provides to creative partners, each with corresponding revenue splits:
+
--
* *Full Support*: Maximum services, higher CWS share
* *Moderate Support*: Balanced services and split
* *Light Support*: Advisory focus, lower CWS share
* *Infrastructure Only*: Tools and access, minimal CWS share
--
+
See: <<02-publishing-house-model/02-05-REVENUE-ECONOMICS.adoc#,02-05: Revenue and Economics>>

'''

Infrastructure and systems designed to operate without requiring participation in beast-system commerce (Revelation 13:16-17). Preparation for times when the mark may be required for buying and selling.

Millennium::
Christ's literal thousand-year reign on Earth (Revelation 20:4-6). CWS builds with this timeframe in mind — technology designed to serve not just today but through the Millennial Kingdom.

See: <<01-entity-overview/01-07-SCRIPTURAL-FOUNDATION.adoc#,01-07: Scriptural Foundation>>

[[term-kingdom]]
Kingdom::
The Kingdom of God — both present (in believers' hearts) and future (Christ's literal reign). CWS exists to serve Kingdom purposes, not worldly success metrics.
+
See: <<01-entity-overview/01-07-SCRIPTURAL-FOUNDATION.adoc#,01-07: Scriptural Foundation>>

[[term-body-of-christ]]
Body of Christ::
The universal church — all believers in Jesus Christ across denominations. One of CWS's primary audiences, particularly those preparing for challenging times ahead.

See: <<05-who-cws-serves/05-03-THE-BODY-OF-CHRIST.adoc#,05-03: The Body of Christ>>

== Audience Terms

[[term-five-primary-audiences]]
Five Primary Audiences::
The five distinct groups CWS intentionally serves: *Creatives*, *Faith-Based Businesses*, *The Body of Christ*, *Communities*, and *Future Builders*. CWS prioritizes depth of service to aligned audiences over volume.
+
See: <<05-who-cws-serves/05-00-WHO-CWS-SERVES.adoc#,05-00: Who CWS Serves>>

[[term-creatives]]
Creatives::
Artists, developers, writers, musicians, game creators, filmmakers, and makers of all kinds. CWS's primary audience as a publishing house. Creatives partnering with CWS retain full IP ownership.
+
See: <<05-who-cws-serves/05-01-CREATIVES-WE-SERVE.adoc#,05-01: Creatives We Serve>>

[[term-faith-based-businesses]]
Faith-Based Businesses::
Churches, ministries, Christian nonprofits, and Kingdom-aligned startups. Organizations whose operations are grounded in faith and aligned with Kingdom purposes.
+
See: <<05-who-cws-serves/05-02-FAITH-BASED-BUSINESSES.adoc#,05-02: Faith-Based Businesses>>

[[term-communities]]
Communities::
Local neighborhoods, nonprofits, and settlements where CWS people live. Emphasizes physical presence and personal service over digital-only engagement.
+
See: <<05-who-cws-serves/05-04-COMMUNITIES.adoc#,05-04: Communities>>

[[term-future-builders]]
Future Builders::
The next generation of Kingdom technologists — those who will continue building after current builders are gone. Includes next generation, tribulation survivors, and Millennium builders.
+
See: <<05-who-cws-serves/05-05-FUTURE-BUILDERS.adoc#,05-05: Future Builders>>

[[term-guardian-angel-settlement]]
Guardian Angel Settlement::
A specific CWS commitment to settlement-style community service. Represents long-term, embedded community presence — a familial commitment to a place and its people.
+
See: <<05-who-cws-serves/05-04-COMMUNITIES.adoc#,05-04: Communities>>

[[term-alignment]]
Alignment::
The values fit between CWS and those we serve. CWS prioritizes alignment over volume, serving deeply aligned partners rather than maximizing customer count. Alignment is assessed through integrity, recognition of the times, building posture, community orientation, and honoring the Creator.
+
See: <<05-who-cws-serves/05-06-ALIGNMENT-AND-BOUNDARIES.adoc#,05-06: Alignment and Boundaries>>

[[term-common-thread]]
Common Thread::
The five marks shared by all CWS audiences: *Integrity*, *Recognize the Times*, *Building Posture*, *Community Orientation*, and *Honor Creator*. These unite diverse audiences under shared values.
+
See: <<05-who-cws-serves/05-06-ALIGNMENT-AND-BOUNDARIES.adoc#,05-06: Alignment and Boundaries>>

[[term-intentional-serving]]
Intentional Serving::
CWS's philosophy of serving specific aligned audiences deeply rather than serving everyone shallowly. Choosing depth over breadth, relationship over transaction.
+
See: <<05-who-cws-serves/05-00-WHO-CWS-SERVES.adoc#,05-00: Who CWS Serves>>

[[term-millennium-builders]]
Millennium Builders::
Those who will build during Christ's thousand-year reign. CWS builds technology designed to serve not just today but through the Millennial Kingdom, preserving knowledge for those who come after.
+
See: <<05-who-cws-serves/05-05-FUTURE-BUILDERS.adoc#,05-05: Future Builders>>

== Economic Terms

[[term-four-revenue-pillars]]
Four Revenue Pillars::
CWS's diversified revenue structure: *External Creative Partnerships* (revenue share from publishing), *Internal IP Revenue* (CWS-owned products), *Services & Fees* (consultation, training), and *Trade Network Value* (non-monetary exchange).
+
See: <<06-business-model/06-01-REVENUE-STREAMS.adoc#,06-01: Revenue Streams>>

[[term-mission-driven-economics]]
Mission-Driven Economics::
CWS's economic philosophy where *mission serves as the primary driver*, not profit maximization. CWS makes money to exist and serve — it doesn't exist to make money.
+
See: <<06-business-model/06-02-ECONOMIC-PHILOSOPHY.adoc#,06-02: Economic Philosophy>>

[[term-fair-exchange]]
Fair Exchange::
The principle that CWS receives *fair compensation for value provided* — no more (not extraction), no less (not unsustainable). Revenue splits and pricing reflect actual value delivered.
+
See: <<06-business-model/06-02-ECONOMIC-PHILOSOPHY.adoc#,06-02: Economic Philosophy>>

[[term-kingdom-economics]]
Kingdom Economics::
Biblical economic principles guiding CWS: *Stewardship* (managing God's resources), *Generosity* (giving built in), *Justice* (fair dealing), *Sufficiency* (enough is enough), and *Trust* (God provides).
+
See: <<06-business-model/06-02-ECONOMIC-PHILOSOPHY.adoc#,06-02: Economic Philosophy>>

[[term-financial-sustainability]]
Financial Sustainability::
CWS's model for long-term viability through revenue diversification, lean operations, reserve building, and adaptation capability — enabling consistent mission service over generations.
+
See: <<06-business-model/06-03-FINANCIAL-SUSTAINABILITY.adoc#,06-03: Financial Sustainability>>

[[term-organic-growth]]
Organic Growth::
CWS's growth model where expansion happens through serving well, partner success, referrals, and aligned growth — not through external funding pressure, debt, or forced scaling.
+
See: <<06-business-model/06-04-GROWTH-MODEL.adoc#,06-04: Growth Model>>

[[term-development-phases]]
Development Phases::
CWS's four-phase growth model: *Foundation* (build core capabilities), *Sustainability* (achieve economic stability), *Scale* (expand reach), and *Maturity* (stable operations, legacy building).
+
See: <<06-business-model/06-04-GROWTH-MODEL.adoc#,06-04: Growth Model>>

[[term-trade-credits]]
Trade Credits::
Network-internal value units accumulated when providing services to trade network members. Credits can be used to receive services from other network members, enabling exchange without direct barter match.
+
See: <<06-business-model/06-06-MARK-FREE-ECONOMICS.adoc#,06-06: Mark-Free Economics>>

[[term-direct-barter]]
Direct Barter::
Immediate exchange of goods or services between two parties without money — Party A provides X, Party B provides Y, with mutually agreed value equivalence.
+
See: <<06-business-model/06-06-MARK-FREE-ECONOMICS.adoc#,06-06: Mark-Free Economics>>

[[term-mark-free-economics]]
Mark-Free Economics::
Economic infrastructure designed to function when standard commerce requires the mark (Revelation 13:16-17). Includes trade networks, barter systems, trade credits, resource sharing, and gift economy within the Body of Christ.
+
See: <<06-business-model/06-06-MARK-FREE-ECONOMICS.adoc#,06-06: Mark-Free Economics>>

'''

== Values Terms

[[term-core-values]]
Core Values::
The eight non-negotiable principles that guide all CWS decisions, relationships, and actions. Values are foundational commitments that cannot be compromised for convenience, growth, profit, or any other consideration.
+
See: <<07-core-values/07-00-CORE-VALUES.adoc#,Chapter 07: Core Values>>

[[term-eight-core-values]]
Eight Core Values::
CWS's complete values framework: (1) Service to Community, (2) Faithful Stewardship, (3) Connective Tissue Not Center, (4) Teaching Through Making, (5) Accessible by Design, (6) Trade and Mutual Support, (7) Reverence for the Creator, (8) Creator Autonomy.
+
See: <<07-core-values/07-00-CORE-VALUES.adoc#,Chapter 07: Core Values>>

[[term-service-to-community]]
Service to Community::
CWS's first core value: *"Everything we build serves others."* The commitment that all work exists to serve community, not CWS itself. Anti-patterns include empire building and extraction.
+
See: <<07-core-values/07-01-SERVICE-TO-COMMUNITY.adoc#,07-01: Service to Community>>

[[term-faithful-stewardship-value]]
Faithful Stewardship (Value)::
CWS's second core value: *"Build with what's given, when it's given."* Managing all resources as entrusted gifts, including timing wisdom. Anti-patterns include hoarding, recklessness, and impatience.
+
See: <<07-core-values/07-02-FAITHFUL-STEWARDSHIP.adoc#,07-02: Faithful Stewardship>>

[[term-connective-tissue-value]]
Connective Tissue, Not Center (Value)::
CWS's third core value: *"Enable the body, don't control it."* The commitment to enabling and connecting rather than controlling, serving as infrastructure not gatekeeper. Anti-patterns include empire building and control.
+
See: <<07-core-values/07-03-CONNECTIVE-TISSUE.adoc#,07-03: Connective Tissue>>

[[term-teaching-through-making]]
Teaching Through Making::
CWS's fourth core value: *"Everything teaches."* The commitment that everything CWS creates should teach, with documentation embedded in every product. Anti-patterns include expert-only and undocumented work.
+
See: <<07-core-values/07-04-TEACHING-THROUGH-MAKING.adoc#,07-04: Teaching Through Making>>

[[term-accessible-by-design]]
Accessible by Design::
CWS's fifth core value: *"Understandable by regular people."* Making everything accessible to non-experts across cognitive, economic, technical, physical, and geographic dimensions. Anti-patterns include complexity worship and gatekeeping.
+
See: <<07-core-values/07-05-ACCESSIBLE-BY-DESIGN.adoc#,07-05: Accessible by Design>>

[[term-trade-mutual-support-value]]
Trade and Mutual Support (Value)::
CWS's sixth core value: *"Decentralized economics."* The commitment to mutual aid, community economics, and building economic relationships through community rather than centralized systems.
+
See: <<07-core-values/07-06-TRADE-AND-MUTUAL-SUPPORT.adoc#,07-06: Trade and Mutual Support>>

[[term-reverence-for-creator]]
Reverence for the Creator::
CWS's seventh core value: *"Genesis patterns throughout."* Honoring God in all work by using patterns He established in creation and grounding technology in biblical foundations.
+
See: <<07-core-values/07-07-REVERENCE-FOR-CREATOR.adoc#,07-07: Reverence for the Creator>>

[[term-creator-autonomy-value]]
Creator Autonomy (Value)::
CWS's eighth core value: *"Creators keep their work."* The non-negotiable commitment that creators retain ownership, control, and independence over what they create. Anti-patterns include ownership taking and dependency creation.
+
See: <<07-core-values/07-08-CREATOR-AUTONOMY.adoc#,07-08: Creator Autonomy>>

[[term-decision-test]]
Decision Test::
A simple question used to evaluate alignment with each core value. For example: "Does this serve others?" (Service), "Does this teach?" (Teaching), "Can regular people understand?" (Accessible). Every value has a corresponding decision test.
+
See: <<07-core-values/07-09-VALUES-IN-PRACTICE.adoc#,07-09: Values in Practice>>

== Direction Terms

[[term-mission-statement]]
Mission Statement::
CWS's formal statement of what we do: *"To publish and equip creatives across every sector — preserving their ownership and autonomy — while building Kingdom-aligned infrastructure that serves the body of Christ and provides mark-free alternatives for the world to come."*
+
See: <<08-mission-and-vision/08-01-MISSION-STATEMENT.adoc#,08-01: Mission Statement>>

[[term-vision-statement]]
Vision Statement::
CWS's formal statement of what success looks like: *"A publishing house spanning every industry where creators keep what they create, faith-based businesses have foundations they can trust, and the faithful have infrastructure that doesn't require the mark..."*
+
See: <<08-mission-and-vision/08-02-VISION-STATEMENT.adoc#,08-02: Vision Statement>>

[[term-purpose]]
Purpose (Why CWS Exists)::
The fundamental "why" behind CWS: Creatives need a home that serves rather than exploits, the Body needs infrastructure that doesn't require compromise, and someone must build for what's coming while there's still time.
+
See: <<08-mission-and-vision/08-03-PURPOSE.adoc#,08-03: Purpose>>

[[term-kingdom-metrics]]
Kingdom Metrics::
How CWS measures success — mission fulfillment rather than merely worldly metrics. Primary metrics include: creators served, infrastructure deployed, mark-free capability, knowledge transfer, and community health.
+
See: <<08-mission-and-vision/08-04-SUCCESS-DEFINITION.adoc#,08-04: Success Definition>>

[[term-alignment-questions]]
Alignment Questions::
Practical questions for testing whether decisions, partnerships, projects, and team members align with CWS mission and vision. Makes abstract statements concrete and actionable.
+
See: <<08-mission-and-vision/08-05-ALIGNMENT-QUESTIONS.adoc#,08-05: Alignment Questions>>

[[term-mission-test]]
Mission Test::
Six questions to test mission alignment: Does this publish/equip creatives? Preserve ownership? Build Kingdom infrastructure? Serve the Body? Prepare mark-free alternatives? Serve the world to come?
+
See: <<08-mission-and-vision/08-01-MISSION-STATEMENT.adoc#,08-01: Mission Statement>>
