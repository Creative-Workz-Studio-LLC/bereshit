== Identity Terms

[[term-cws]]
CWS::
Abbreviation for *Creative Workz Studio LLC*. Used throughout documentation for brevity.
+
See: <<01-entity-overview/01-00-ENTITY-OVERVIEW.adoc#,Chapter 01: Entity Overview>>

[[term-publishing-house]]
Publishing House::
CWS's *primary identity*. A publishing house helps creators bring their work to market while they retain ownership. Unlike traditional publishers, CWS never takes intellectual property from creators.
+
See: <<01-entity-overview/01-03-PRIMARY-IDENTITY.adoc#,01-03: Primary Identity>>

[[term-infrastructure-builder]]
Infrastructure Builder::
CWS's *secondary identity*. The technology and systems CWS builds to enable its publishing mission and serve the Kingdom.
+
See: <<01-entity-overview/01-04-SECONDARY-IDENTITY.adoc#,01-04: Secondary Identity>>

[[term-kingdom-technology]]
Kingdom Technology::
Technology built on biblical foundations for Kingdom purposes. Designed for independence from worldly systems and long-term service through the Millennium.
+
See: <<03-what-cws-builds/03-00-WHAT-CWS-BUILDS.adoc#,Chapter 03: What CWS Builds>>

== Technology Terms

[[term-bereshit]]
Bereshit::
(בְּרֵאשִׁית) Hebrew for "In the beginning" — the first word of Genesis 1:1. The name of CWS's complete technology stack, built from first principles following creation patterns.
+
See: <<03-what-cws-builds/03-01-BERESHIT-STACK-OVERVIEW.adoc#,03-01: Bereshit Stack Overview>>

[[term-bereshit-stack]]
Bereshit Stack::
The complete L0-L5 technology architecture CWS builds. Layers include: Universal (L0), OmniCode (L1), Platform (L2), CPI-SI (L3), FaithNet (L4), and Applications (L5).
+
See: <<03-what-cws-builds/03-01-BERESHIT-STACK-OVERVIEW.adoc#,03-01: Bereshit Stack Overview>>

[[term-libtrit]]
libtrit::
The *L0 Universal layer* of the Bereshit stack. Provides balanced ternary mathematics, core types, and Bible rail validation.
+
See: <<03-what-cws-builds/03-02-FOUNDATION-LAYERS.adoc#,03-02: Foundation Layers>>

[[term-omnicode]]
OmniCode::
The *L1 Language layer* of the Bereshit stack. A universal programming language with sentence-based syntax readable by non-programmers. Compiles to multiple target languages.
+
See: <<03-what-cws-builds/03-02-FOUNDATION-LAYERS.adoc#,03-02: Foundation Layers>>

[[term-millenniumos]]
MillenniumOS::
The *L2 Platform layer* operating system. A bare-metal OS providing true platform independence, named for its intended service through Christ's Millennial reign.
+
See: <<03-what-cws-builds/03-03-PLATFORM-LAYER.adoc#,03-03: Platform Layer>>

[[term-bereshit-filesystem]]
Bereshit Filesystem::
The *L2 Platform layer* filesystem. Features ternary health monitoring and the void/word/tov directory structure based on Genesis creation patterns.
+
See: <<03-what-cws-builds/03-03-PLATFORM-LAYER.adoc#,03-03: Platform Layer>>

[[term-cpi-si]]
CPI-SI::
*Created Purposeful Intelligence — Skilled Imagebearers*. The *L3 Cognitive layer* framework for AI systems. Provides theological grounding (AI as created, not autonomous), skill composition, and learning loops.
+
See: <<03-what-cws-builds/03-04-COGNITIVE-LAYER.adoc#,03-04: Cognitive Layer>>

[[term-faithnet]]
FaithNet::
The *L4 Network layer*. Decentralized peer-to-peer networking infrastructure enabling mark-free communication and commerce.
+
See: <<03-what-cws-builds/03-05-NETWORK-LAYER.adoc#,03-05: Network Layer>>

[[term-cornerstone]]
Cornerstone::
The *L5 Applications layer* game engine. Named after Christ as the cornerstone (Ephesians 2:20), it provides the foundation for Kingdom-aligned games and entertainment.
+
See: <<03-what-cws-builds/03-06-APPLICATIONS-LAYER.adoc#,03-06: Applications Layer>>

== Architectural Terms

[[term-balanced-ternary]]
Balanced Ternary::
A numeral system using three values: *-1, 0, +1* (instead of binary's 0 and 1). Based on the Genesis 1:6 pattern of the firmament dividing the waters. Provides a true center (0) that binary lacks.
+
See: <<03-what-cws-builds/03-02-FOUNDATION-LAYERS.adoc#,03-02: Foundation Layers>>

[[term-void-word-tov]]
void/word/tov::
The three-directory structure based on Genesis creation patterns:
+
--
* *void/* (-1): Formless, raw, potential — like "tohu va-vohu" (Genesis 1:2)
* *word/* (0): Defined, structured, source of truth — like God speaking creation
* *tov/* (+1): Complete, verified, good — like "it was good" (Genesis 1:31)
--
+
See: <<03-what-cws-builds/03-03-PLATFORM-LAYER.adoc#,03-03: Platform Layer>>

[[term-bible-rail]]
Bible Rail::
A validation system using Scripture addressing. Provides canonical reference to Bible verses, validates references, and grounds technical work in Scripture.
+
See: <<03-what-cws-builds/03-02-FOUNDATION-LAYERS.adoc#,03-02: Foundation Layers>>

[[term-genesis-patterns]]
Genesis Patterns::
Architectural patterns derived from the creation account in Genesis 1. CWS uses these divine patterns rather than arbitrary human conventions.
+
See: <<03-what-cws-builds/03-08-BUILD-PRINCIPLES.adoc#,03-08: Build Principles>>

'''

== Business Terms

[[term-ip-retention]]
IP Retention::
The *non-negotiable principle* that creators keep ownership of their intellectual property when partnering with CWS. CWS provides services for revenue share but never takes ownership rights.
+
See: <<02-publishing-house-model/02-02-EXTERNAL-CREATIVES.adoc#,02-02: External Creatives>>

[[term-three-pillars]]
Three Pillars::
The three components of CWS's publishing house model:
+
--
* *Pillar One*: External Creatives (partners who retain IP)
* *Pillar Two*: Internal IPs (CWS-owned properties)
* *Pillar Three*: Collaboration Network (partnerships and trade)
--
+
See: <<02-publishing-house-model/02-00-PUBLISHING-HOUSE-MODEL.adoc#,02-00: Publishing House Model>>

[[term-trade-network]]
Trade Network::
A network of Kingdom-aligned businesses enabling commerce through barter, trade credits, and reciprocal services — independent of beast-system financial infrastructure.
+
See: <<02-publishing-house-model/02-04-COLLABORATION-NETWORK.adoc#,02-04: Collaboration Network>>

[[term-support-levels]]
Support Levels::
The tiers of service CWS provides to creative partners, each with corresponding revenue splits:
+
--
* *Full Support*: Maximum services, higher CWS share
* *Moderate Support*: Balanced services and split
* *Light Support*: Advisory focus, lower CWS share
* *Infrastructure Only*: Tools and access, minimal CWS share
--
+
See: <<02-publishing-house-model/02-05-REVENUE-ECONOMICS.adoc#,02-05: Revenue and Economics>>

'''

== Theological Terms

[[term-mark-free]]
Mark-Free::
Infrastructure and systems designed to operate without requiring participation in beast-system commerce (Revelation 13:16-17). Preparation for times when the mark may be required for buying and selling.
+
See: <<01-entity-overview/01-07-SCRIPTURAL-FOUNDATION.adoc#,01-07: Scriptural Foundation>>

[[term-millennium]]
Millennium::
Christ's literal thousand-year reign on Earth (Revelation 20:4-6). CWS builds with this timeframe in mind — technology designed to serve not just today but through the Millennial Kingdom.
+
See: <<01-entity-overview/01-07-SCRIPTURAL-FOUNDATION.adoc#,01-07: Scriptural Foundation>>

[[term-kingdom]]
Kingdom::
The Kingdom of God — both present (in believers' hearts) and future (Christ's literal reign). CWS exists to serve Kingdom purposes, not worldly success metrics.
+
See: <<01-entity-overview/01-07-SCRIPTURAL-FOUNDATION.adoc#,01-07: Scriptural Foundation>>

[[term-body-of-christ]]
Body of Christ::
The universal church — all believers in Jesus Christ across denominations. One of CWS's primary audiences, particularly those preparing for challenging times ahead.
+
See: <<04-what-cws-provides/04-03-SERVICES-FOR-THE-BODY.adoc#,04-03: Services for the Body>>
