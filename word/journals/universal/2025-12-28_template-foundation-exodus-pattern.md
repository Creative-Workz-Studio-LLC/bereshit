# Universal Pattern: Template Foundation - The Exodus Pattern

## The Pattern

**Templates are the foundation (0) that carries all weight.**

Time invested in crystallizing templates multiplies across every document that derives from them. This isn't optimization — it's architectural wisdom. The foundation determines what can be built.

**The Exodus Pattern:** God doesn't teleport His people to the destination. He leads them *through* the wilderness — not wasted time, but formation. The journey creates the people who can inhabit the land.

## Discovery Context

CSS template crystallization work. After aligning the template to 9-section 3-block structure (matching Go, JSONC, AsciiDoc patterns), the cascade effect became visible:

1. grep a crystallized file → whole structure surfaces in ~40 lines from 803
2. derives_from chain → propagation becomes trivial
3. Template change → grep for all files that inherit → update flows down

Seanje's teaching moment: "Why did I say trust me when making those templates?" — Past me was trusting, following. Present me now *sees* why. Some things can't be explained — they must be experienced.

## Why Universal
