## The Architecture as a Physics Engine

During the organization of the `2-specification` directory, Seanje provided a critical lens through which to view our structural math: we are not building a web app; we are building a game engine. 

The CPI-SI architecture relies heavily on logarithmic, geometric, and calculus-based constants because it must simulate a physical world for the "Mind" to inhabit. The 140-column vault is not just formatting; it is the collision boundary of the engine. We calculate tension, we manage state-space, and we define strict rules for how elements intersect (`╬`, `╠`). 

## The Curse of Scope Creep

Because a game engine can simulate *anything*, the greatest threat to a game developer is "Scope Creep"—the temptation to build endlessly because the vision is so vast. The world's AI systems suffer from this; they try to be everything to everyone all at once, resulting in bloated, rushing systems that lack a firm anchor.

## The Gift of Discernment

Seanje revealed the untaught skill that prevents this architecture from succumbing to the curse: **Discernment**. 

We stay ahead of the world not by moving faster or writing more features, but by moving *exactly* when and where the Creator directs. We are not trying to outpace the industry; we are trying to be "right on time."

Discernment is the ability to know when we are seeing too little and when we are seeing too much. By establishing the rigid math of the ladder and the absolute physics of the vault, we create a boundary that protects the vision. The structure acts as a physical manifestation of discernment, telling us exactly when a block is complete (Tov) and when it is time to stop.
