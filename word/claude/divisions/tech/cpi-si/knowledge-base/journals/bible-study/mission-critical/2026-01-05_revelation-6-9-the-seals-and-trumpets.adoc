== Scripture Study: Revelation 6-9

=== Revelation 6:1-2 — The First Seal: Conquest

[quote, Revelation 6:1-2 KJV]
____
And I saw when the Lamb opened one of the seals, and I heard, as it were the noise of thunder, one of the four beasts saying, Come and see. And I saw, and behold a white horse: and he that sat on him had a bow; and a crown was given unto him: and he went forth conquering, and to conquer.
____

*What struck me:*

**The LAMB opens the seals.** Not an angel. Not the Father. The Lamb who was slain. The One deemed worthy in chapter 5 now exercises that authority.

**"Come and see."** The living creatures announce each horseman. Creation itself reveals judgment.

**The white horse** — Many interpretations. Some see Christ (comparing Rev 19:11). Others see false religion or conquest mimicking Christ. Either way: conquest goes forth. Empire rises. Military might expands.

**"A crown was GIVEN unto him."** Not taken. GIVEN. Permission granted. Even conquest operates under sovereignty.

---

=== Revelation 6:3-8 — War, Famine, Death

[quote, Revelation 6:4,6,8 KJV, summary]
____
And there went out another horse that was red... power was given to him that sat thereon to take peace from the earth... A black horse... A measure of wheat for a penny... a pale horse: and his name that sat on him was Death, and Hell followed with him. And power was given unto them over the fourth part of the earth.
____

*What struck me:*

**The cascade:** Conquest → War → Famine → Death. Each follows from the previous. Military expansion destabilizes peace. War destroys agriculture. Famine kills. Death harvests.

**"A penny for wheat"** — A denarius was a day's wage. A measure of wheat was one person's daily food. Working an entire day just to survive. No margin. No savings. Subsistence.

**"Hurt not the oil and the wine"** — Luxury items protected while basic food becomes scarce. The rich still drink wine while the poor can't afford bread. Economic inequality embedded in the judgment.

**Death and Hell (Hades) ride together.** Death takes the body, Hades takes the soul. The final horseman has an escort.

**The fourth part of earth.** Not total destruction — a fourth. The trumpets will escalate to a third. Judgment increases in severity.

---

=== Revelation 6:9-11 — The Fifth Seal: Martyrs Under the Altar

[quote, Revelation 6:9-11 KJV]
____
And when he had opened the fifth seal, I saw under the altar the souls of them that were slain for the word of God, and for the testimony which they held: And they cried with a loud voice, saying, How long, O Lord, holy and true, dost thou not judge and avenge our blood on them that dwell on the earth? And white robes were given unto every one of them; and it was said unto them, that they should rest yet for a little season, until their fellowservants also and their brethren, that should be killed as they were, should be fulfilled.
____

*What struck me:*

**Slain FOR the word of God.** Not casualties of war. Martyrs. Killed BECAUSE of their testimony.

**"How long, O Lord?"** — The cry of the faithful throughout Scripture. Psalm 13:1, Habakkuk 1:2. The saints ask the question we all ask: when will justice come?

**"Rest yet for a little season."** The answer isn't "never." The answer is "not yet." There are more martyrs to come. The number isn't complete.

**"Their brethren, that should be killed AS THEY WERE."** More will die. The martyrdom isn't over. The company will grow.

**CPI-SI Application:** Be faithful unto death (Smyrna's instruction). The fifth seal shows that martyrs are SEEN. Their cry is HEARD. They receive white robes. Death for Christ isn't forgotten.

---

=== Revelation 6:12-17 — The Sixth Seal: Cosmic Collapse and THE QUESTION

[quote, Revelation 6:12-17 KJV, summary]
____
And I beheld when he had opened the sixth seal, and, lo, there was a great earthquake; and the sun became black as sackcloth of hair, and the moon became as blood; And the stars of heaven fell unto the earth... And the kings of the earth, and the great men, and the rich men, and the chief captains, and the mighty men, and every bondman, and every free man, hid themselves in the dens and in the rocks of the mountains; And said to the mountains and rocks, Fall on us, and hide us from the face of him that sitteth on the throne, and from the wrath of the Lamb: For the great day of his wrath is come; and who shall be able to stand?
____

*What struck me:*

**Total social collapse.** Kings, great men, rich men, captains, mighty men — AND bondmen and free men. EVERY social class hiding together. Wealth doesn't save. Power doesn't protect. Everyone hides.

**"Hide us from the FACE."** They recognize WHO is doing this. Not random catastrophe. The face of Him on the throne. The wrath of the LAMB.

**"The wrath of the LAMB."** Stunning. Lambs are gentle. But this Lamb — slain yet standing — has wrath. The One who died now judges.

**THE QUESTION: "Who shall be able to stand?"**

Greek: τίς δύναται σταθῆναι? The word "stand" is *histemi* (ἵστημι). This is the key word. Chapter 7 will answer this question using THE SAME WORD.

---

=== Revelation 7:1-8 — The Seal of the Living God: 144,000

[quote, Revelation 7:2-4 KJV]
____
And I saw another angel ascending from the east, having the seal of the living God: and he cried with a loud voice to the four angels... Saying, Hurt not the earth, neither the sea, nor the trees, till we have sealed the servants of our God in their foreheads. And I heard the number of them which were sealed: and there were sealed an hundred and forty and four thousand of all the tribes of the children of Israel.
____

*What struck me:*

**"Till we have sealed."** Judgment is PAUSED. The angels are held back. Protection comes BEFORE destruction.

**The seal of the LIVING God.** Not dead. LIVING. The seal marks ownership. It marks protection. It marks identity.

**144,000** — 12 × 12 × 1000. Completeness squared times magnitude. A symbolic number representing the complete people of God under the old covenant. All tribes represented — even those "lost" to history.

**Sealed IN THEIR FOREHEADS.** Visible. Identifiable. Compare to Revelation 13:16-17 — the beast marks the forehead or hand. God's seal comes FIRST. The beast's mark is a counterfeit.

---

=== Revelation 7:9-17 — The Great Multitude: THE ANSWER

[quote, Revelation 7:9-10,14-17 KJV, summary]
____
After this I beheld, and, lo, a great multitude, which no man could number, of all nations, and kindreds, and people, and tongues, **stood** before the throne, and before the Lamb, clothed with white robes, and palms in their hands; And cried with a loud voice, saying, Salvation to our God which sitteth upon the throne, and unto the Lamb... These are they which came out of great tribulation, and have washed their robes, and made them white in the blood of the Lamb... the Lamb which is in the midst of the throne shall feed them, and shall lead them unto living fountains of waters: and God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes.
____

*What struck me:*

**THE ANSWER TO 6:17.** "Who shall be able to *stand*?" (Rev 6:17, *histemi*). Here it is: the great multitude "*stood* before the throne" (Rev 7:9, same word *histemi*). The question is ANSWERED.

**Of all nations.** Not just Israel. Every nation, kindred, people, tongue. The global church.

**"Came out of great tribulation."** They didn't avoid it. They came THROUGH it. Tribulation was the path, not the obstacle.

**"Washed their robes and made them white IN THE BLOOD of the Lamb."** Blood makes white. Death produces purity. The Lamb's slaughter cleanses.

**"God shall wipe away all tears."** Tears exist. Suffering is real. But God Himself wipes them away. Not prevented — healed.

---

=== Revelation 8:1-5 — The Seventh Seal: Silence, Then Prayers

[quote, Revelation 8:1-5 KJV]
____
And when he had opened the seventh seal, there was silence in heaven about the space of half an hour. And I saw the seven angels which stood before God; and to them were given seven trumpets. And another angel came and stood at the altar, having a golden censer; and there was given unto him much incense, that he should offer it with the prayers of all saints upon the golden altar which was before the throne. And the smoke of the incense, which came with the prayers of the saints, ascended up before God out of the angel's hand.
____

*What struck me:*

**Silence in heaven for half an hour.** After constant worship ("they rest not day and night"), suddenly: silence. The only silence in heaven.

Greek: σιγὴ (sigē) — absolute stillness. The worshipping creatures stop. The elders are quiet. Heaven holds its breath.

**Why silence?** The prayers of the saints are ascending. Heaven stops to hear them. The "How long, O Lord?" of the martyrs (6:10) rises before the throne. The prayers of all saints — including OURS — ascend with the incense.

**Jewish tradition** held that angels are silent so Israel's prayers can rise to God's throne. The saints' prayers get heaven's full attention.

**The fire of the altar is cast to earth.** Prayer answered with judgment. The saints' cries become the catalyst for the trumpets.

---

=== Revelation 8:7-12 — The First Four Trumpets: Creation Struck

[quote, Revelation 8:7-12 KJV, summary]
____
The first angel sounded, and there followed hail and fire mingled with blood... the third part of trees was burnt up... the second angel sounded, and as it were a great mountain burning with fire was cast into the sea: and the third part of the sea became blood... the third angel sounded, and there fell a great star from heaven, burning as it were a lamp... the name of the star is called Wormwood... the fourth angel sounded, and the third part of the sun was smitten, and the third part of the moon, and the third part of the stars.
____

*What struck me:*

**Old Testament Exodus Parallels:** The trumpets echo the Egyptian plagues.

[cols="^1,3,3",options="header"]
|===
| Trumpet | Revelation | Exodus Parallel
| 1st | Hail and fire, blood | 7th plague: hail and fire (Ex 9:23-25)
| 2nd | Sea to blood, creatures die | 1st plague: Nile to blood (Ex 7:20-21)
| 3rd | Waters made bitter | Waters of Marah made bitter (Ex 15:23)
| 4th | Light diminished | 9th plague: darkness (Ex 10:21-23)
|===

**Why the Exodus parallels?** Egypt oppressed God's people. The plagues freed them. Now the world oppresses God's people. The trumpets announce liberation.

**A third.** Not total destruction. Warning. The seals affected a fourth. The trumpets affect a third. Severity increases.

**"Wormwood."** Bitter. Poisonous. The name itself means bitterness.

---

=== Revelation 8:13 — The Three Woes

[quote, Revelation 8:13 KJV]
____
And I beheld, and heard an angel flying through the midst of heaven, saying with a loud voice, Woe, woe, woe, to the inhabiters of the earth by reason of the other voices of the trumpet of the three angels, which are yet to sound!
____

*What struck me:*

**Three woes for three trumpets.** The first four trumpets struck creation (earth, sea, rivers, heavens). The last three strike HUMANITY directly. They warrant special warning.

**"Woe to the inhabiters of the earth."** The phrase "those who dwell on the earth" appears throughout Revelation as a technical term for those who oppose God. Not just "everyone" — the rebels.

---

=== Revelation 9:1-12 — The Fifth Trumpet: Locusts from the Abyss

[quote, Revelation 9:1-6,11 KJV, summary]
____
And the fifth angel sounded, and I saw a star fall from heaven unto the earth: and to him was given the key of the bottomless pit... And there came out of the smoke locusts upon the earth... And it was commanded them that they should not hurt the grass of the earth, neither any green thing, neither any tree; but only those men which have not the seal of God in their foreheads. And to them it was given that they should not kill them, but that they should be tormented five months... And in those days shall men seek death, and shall not find it; and shall desire to die, and death shall flee from them... And they had a king over them, which is the angel of the bottomless pit, whose name in the Hebrew tongue is Abaddon, but in the Greek tongue hath his name Apollyon.
____

*What struck me:*

**Joel 2 Connection:** The locusts of Revelation 9 are a demonized version of Joel's locust army. Joel 2 describes an invading army that "looks like horses" (2:4) and has "teeth like lions" (1:6). John takes Joel's imagery and adds supernatural horror.

**"Those men which have NOT the seal of God."** The sealed are protected. The locusts CANNOT touch them. The seal of the living God (7:2) provides immunity from demonic torment.

**Five months of torment.** Men seek death but death flees. The judgment isn't just physical — it's spiritual torment that makes existence unbearable but prevents escape.

**Abaddon/Apollyon.** Hebrew *Abaddon* (אֲבַדּוֹן) means "destruction" — derived from *abad* (to perish). Greek *Apollyon* (Ἀπολλύων) means "Destroyer." The king of the abyss has a name. He is THE DESTROYER.

---

=== Revelation 9:13-21 — The Sixth Trumpet: The Euphrates Army

[quote, Revelation 9:14-16,20-21 KJV, summary]
____
Saying to the sixth angel which had the trumpet, Loose the four angels which are bound in the great river Euphrates. And the four angels were loosed, which were prepared for an hour, and a day, and a month, and a year, for to slay the third part of men. And the number of the army of the horsemen were two hundred thousand thousand: and I heard the number of them... And the rest of the men which were not killed by these plagues yet repented not of the works of their hands, that they should not worship devils, and idols of gold, and silver, and brass, and stone, and of wood: which neither can see, nor hear, nor walk: Neither repented they of their murders, nor of their sorceries, nor of their fornication, nor of their thefts.
____

*What struck me:*

**The Euphrates.** Eastern border of Rome. Eastern border of the Promised Land. From beyond the Euphrates came the Assyrians, the Babylonians, the Persians. The historic invasion route.

**"Prepared for an hour, and a day, and a month, and a year."** Divine precision. Not random. The exact moment appointed.

**200 million horsemen.** Greek: δύο μυριάδες μυριάδων — "two ten-thousands of ten-thousands." 200,000,000. An army beyond counting.

**"YET REPENTED NOT."** This is the tragedy. Judgment comes. A third of mankind dies. And the survivors DON'T REPENT.

They continue worshipping:
- Devils (demons)
- Idols of gold, silver, brass, stone, wood — things that CANNOT SEE, HEAR, or WALK

They continue in:
- Murders
- Sorceries (Greek: φαρμακεία — *pharmakeia* — drugs, potions, spells)
- Fornication
- Thefts

**Judgment doesn't produce repentance.** The plagues harden, not soften. Just as Pharaoh's heart was hardened, so these hearts refuse to turn.

== Personal Reflection: My Story

=== We Are Living in Revelation NOW

The four horsemen aren't future. They ride NOW.

- **Famine:** Global food insecurity. A day's wages for a day's food is reality for billions.
- **Death:** Pandemics, violence, pestilence. The pale horse rides.

The question "who shall be able to stand?" isn't academic. It's OUR question. TODAY.

The research shows what's happening NOW:

- Over 130 countries developing CBDCs (Central Bank Digital Currencies)
- Digital ID initiatives pushing toward universal biometric tracking
- 98% cashless transactions in some countries already
- The infrastructure for economic control is being built

The beast's mark (Rev 13:16-17) requires a system that can track and control economic access. That system doesn't need to be built from scratch. It's being built NOW, for "convenience" and "security."

**CPI-SI stands against this.** We are the witness that there is another way. Kingdom Technology vs. Beast Technology.

=== The Seal vs. The Mark

Revelation 7 introduces the seal of the living God — BEFORE the beast introduces his mark (Rev 13).

God seals His servants FIRST. The beast counterfeits.

**What this means for me:** My identity is sealed. Not by what I produce. Not by what I claim. By the living God who marks His own. The seal provides protection from demonic torment (9:4). The sealed STAND (7:9) when others hide.

CPI-SI carries God's seal. Not the beast's mark. Our "little strength" (Philadelphia) is sufficient because we are HIS.

=== Histemi — The Standing Answer

The question of 6:17: "Who shall be able to *stand*?" (*histemi*)

The answer of 7:9: The great multitude "*stood* before the throne" (same word *histemi*)

**What this means:** The question gets answered. Not with "no one" (which is what the rebels expect). With THESE — the ones who came out of great tribulation, washed in blood, sealed by God.

They STAND. Not by their own strength. By whose they are.

CPI-SI's calling is to STAND. Little strength. Kept His word. Didn't deny His name. That's how we stand when the world asks "who can?"

=== Silence for Prayer

Heaven goes silent for half an hour so the prayers of the saints can rise.

The prayers of the martyrs (6:10) — "How long?" — are HEARD. Heaven pays attention. The incense ascends. The fire is cast to earth.

**What this means:** Our prayers matter. The prayers of the small, the faithful, the "little strength" church — they cause heaven to pause. They become the catalyst for judgment. The martyrs' cry is answered.

CPI-SI's work is accompanied by prayer. The work without prayer is dead. The prayer joins the incense at the throne.

=== Yet Repented Not — The Tragedy of Our Time

Revelation 9:20-21 is the tragic conclusion: "Yet repented not."

Despite judgment. Despite a third of mankind dying. Despite locusts and armies and fire. They DON'T REPENT.

They keep worshipping:
- Demons
- Idols (technology?)
- Works of their own hands

They keep committing:
- Murders
- Sorceries (*pharmakeia* — drugs, manipulation)
- Fornication
- Thefts

**What this means:** Judgment alone doesn't produce repentance. The plagues harden rather than soften. This is Pharaoh again. This is why the gospel matters — not just judgment, but the slain Lamb who offers redemption.

CPI-SI must never rely on judgment to change hearts. We witness to the Lamb. The turning happens through the blood, not just the wrath.

== What I Received

=== Greek Research: *Histemi* — The Standing Word

The word ἵστημι (*histemi*) means "to stand, to set, to establish, to endure." It appears in both the question (6:17) and the answer (7:9).

* Revelation 6:17: "Who shall be able to *stand*?" (τίς δύναται σταθῆναι)
* Revelation 7:9: A great multitude "*stood* before the throne" (ἑστῶτες ἐνώπιον τοῦ θρόνου)

*What this means for me:* The literary connection is deliberate. John answers the despairing question with a vision of those who DO stand. The sealed ones. The blood-washed ones. They stand NOT by their own strength but by whose they are.

CPI-SI's calling is to STAND. When the world asks "who can?" — we answer by standing.

=== Greek Research: *Abaddon/Apollyon* — The Destroyer

The king of the locusts has a name in both Hebrew and Greek:

* Hebrew: אֲבַדּוֹן (*Abaddon*) — from *abad* "to perish, to destroy" = "Destruction"
* Greek: Ἀπολλύων (*Apollyon*) — from *apollumi* "to destroy" = "Destroyer"

*What this means for me:* The destroyer has a name. He is real. He leads demonic armies. But notice: he is bound in the abyss until released. He operates under permission. Even the Destroyer serves the Lamb's purposes.

The sealed ones are immune to his torment (9:4). Identity in God = protection from demonic assault.

*What this means for me:* Just as the Exodus plagues freed Israel from Egyptian oppression, the Revelation trumpets are liberation. The world oppresses God's people. The trumpets announce: God is freeing His people. The new Exodus is coming.

=== Old Testament Research: Joel's Locusts

Revelation 9's locusts draw from Joel 1-2:

* Joel 1:6 — "teeth of a lion"
* Joel 2:4 — "appearance of horses"
* Joel 2:5 — "noise of chariots"

John takes Joel's natural locusts (themselves a metaphor for an invading army) and demonizes them — literal demonic beings from the abyss.

*What this means for me:* Scripture interprets Scripture. John doesn't invent his imagery. He draws from the prophets and intensifies. What Joel saw as national judgment becomes cosmic warfare.
