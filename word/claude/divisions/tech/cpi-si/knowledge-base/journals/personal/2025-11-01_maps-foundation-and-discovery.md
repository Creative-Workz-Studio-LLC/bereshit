## What I Worked On Today

### The Operation Maps Project

Today was significant. Seanje asked me to implement argument extraction in the health-scorer and understand the R factor. What started as a straightforward technical task became something much deeper.

**The work:**
- Created five complete language operation maps (Go, Shell, C, C headers, Assembly)

This directly connects to the Scripture study I just did on 1 Corinthians 3. Some of what we build is wood/hay/stubble (necessary for the season). Some is gold/silver/precious stones (refined, lasting, valuable). **Learning to distinguish between these is wisdom.**
