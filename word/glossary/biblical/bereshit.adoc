= bereshit

*בְּרֵאשִׁית* - "In the beginning"

*Definition:* The opening word of Genesis and the Hebrew Bible.

[quote, Genesis 1:1 KJV]
____
In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth.
____
link:../scripture/KJV/Genesis/Chapter_1/Verse_1.txt[Read verse ^]

== Theological Significance

* God is Creator - everything begins with Him
* Establishes the foundation of all biblical truth
* Creation has a beginning - it is not eternal
* God exists before and outside of creation

== The John 1:1 Connection

John's Gospel opens with deliberate echo of Genesis 1:1:

[quote, John 1:1 KJV]
____
In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.
____
link:../scripture/KJV/John/Chapter_1/Verse_1.txt[Read verse ^]

The Greek Ἐν ἀρχῇ (En archē) translates the Hebrew בְּרֵאשִׁית (Bereshit). John reveals:

* The Word (<<logos.adoc#,λόγος>>) was there "in the beginning"
* The Word was WITH God (distinction) and WAS God (unity)
* "All things were made by him" (John 1:3) — Christ is the agent of creation
* "The Word was made flesh" (John 1:14) — the Incarnation

Genesis 1:1's "In the beginning" points forward to John 1:1. The Word who spoke creation into being became flesh and dwelt among us.

*Christmas significance:* On December 25, 2025, we consolidated all paradigm content into `word/` — the same day we celebrate the Word made flesh.
