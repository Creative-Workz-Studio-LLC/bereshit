= yashar (יָשָׁר)

*Definition:* EVEN - balanced, straight, upright. The neutral center state.

*Hebrew:* יָשָׁר (yashar) - straight, upright, right, pleasing

== What This State Means

* Perfect balance - neither lacking nor excess
* The center of the MATTER axis
* Pure state: no k-choice, X = Y directly
* Upright, straight, properly aligned

== The Center Position

EVEN is the pivot point:

* Below: BROKEN, LACKING, WANTING (negative X)
* Above: SOUND, WHOLE, PERFECT (positive X)
* Here: balanced, waiting, ready
