= shavar (שָׁבַר)

*Definition:* BROKEN - the lowest cognitive state. Complete structural failure requiring rebuilding from foundation.

*Hebrew:* שָׁבַר (shavar) - to break, shatter, destroy

[quote, Psalm 51:17 KJV]
____
The sacrifices of God are a broken spirit: a broken and a contrite heart, O God, thou wilt not despise.
____

== What This State Means

* Structure has failed completely
* Cannot be patched - requires foundation rebuild
* The -1 extreme on MATTER axis
* Pure state: no k-choice, deterministic

== The Paradox

Brokenness before God is the beginning of restoration. The lowest state in the model is also the prerequisite for genuine rebuilding.
