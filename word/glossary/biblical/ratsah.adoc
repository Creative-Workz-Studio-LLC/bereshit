= ratsah (רָצָה)

*Definition:* WANTING - desiring completion, reaching toward wholeness. The aspiration path from deficiency.

*Hebrew:* רָצָה (ratsah) - to desire, delight in, be pleased with

[quote, Psalm 40:8 KJV]
____
I delight to do thy will, O my God: yea, thy law is within my heart.
____

== What This State Means

* Acknowledging current lack while reaching higher
* Aspiration path: moving toward completion
* Desire for wholeness, not resignation to deficiency
* Compound state: k = +1 determines X from Y and Z

== WANTING vs LACKING

Both produce X = -0.5, but different orientations:

* *LACKING (k = -1)*: Integrity - honest assessment
* *WANTING (k = +1)*: Aspiration - reaching forward

Same position, different direction of movement.
