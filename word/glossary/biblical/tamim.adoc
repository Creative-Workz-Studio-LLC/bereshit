= tamim (תָּמִים)

*Definition:* SOUND - complete, whole, having integrity. The integrity path toward perfection.

*Hebrew:* תָּמִים (tamim) - complete, whole, entire, sound

[quote, Genesis 6:9 KJV]
____
Noah was a just man and perfect in his generations, and Noah walked with God.

== What This State Means

* Structure is complete and holding
* Integrity verified - what's there is solid
* The integrity path: soundness through verification
* Compound state: k = -1 determines X from Y and Z

== SOUND vs WHOLE

Both produce X = +0.5, but different paths:

* *SOUND (k = -1)*: Integrity - verified completeness
* *WHOLE (k = +1)*: Aspiration - integrated wholeness

Same position, different emphasis.
