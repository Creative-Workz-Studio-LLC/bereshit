= shalem (שָׁלֵם)

*Definition:* WHOLE - integrated, complete, at peace. The aspiration path toward perfection.

*Hebrew:* שָׁלֵם (shalem) - complete, safe, whole, at peace (root of shalom)

[quote, 1 Kings 8:61 KJV]
____
Let your heart therefore be perfect with the LORD our God, to walk in his statutes, and to keep his commandments.
____

== What This State Means

* Integrated wholeness - parts working together
* Aspiration path: reaching toward perfection
* Peace through integration (shalom from shalem)
* Compound state: k = +1 determines X from Y and Z

== WHOLE vs SOUND

Both produce X = +0.5, but different emphasis:

* *SOUND (k = -1)*: Integrity - verified completeness
* *WHOLE (k = +1)*: Aspiration - integrated reaching upward

Same position, different orientation.

== The Shalom Connection

שָׁלֵם (shalem) is the root of שָׁלוֹם (shalom). Wholeness produces peace.
