= logos

*λόγος* - "Word" (also: reason, order, logic, principle)

*Definition:* The Greek term used in John 1:1-14 for the eternal Son of God, the divine Word through whom all things were created.

[quote, John 1:1 KJV]
____
In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.
____
link:../scripture/KJV/John/Chapter_1/Verse_1.txt[Read verse ^]

[quote, John 1:14 KJV]
____
And the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us, (and we beheld his glory, the glory as of the only begotten of the Father,) full of grace and truth.
____
link:../scripture/KJV/John/Chapter_1/Verse_14.txt[Read verse ^]

== Theological Significance

* *Divine Person* — The Logos is not a concept but a Person: the eternal Son of God
* *Agent of Creation* — "All things were made by him" (John 1:3, Colossians 1:16)
* *Sustainer of Reality* — "By him all things consist" (Colossians 1:17)
* *The Incarnation* — "The Word was made flesh" — God entering creation as a creature
* *Revelation of God* — "The express image of his person" (Hebrews 1:3)

== Connection to Bereshit

John's opening "In the beginning" (Ἐν ἀρχῇ - En archē) deliberately echoes Genesis 1:1's "In the beginning" (בְּרֵאשִׁית - Bereshit).

[cols="1,1,2"]
|===
|Text |Language |Connection

|Genesis 1:1
|בְּרֵאשִׁית (Bereshit)
|God creates by speaking — "And God said..."

|John 1:1
|Ἐν ἀρχῇ (En archē)
|The Word WAS there, WITH God, and WAS God

|John 1:14
|σὰρξ ἐγένετο
|The Word became flesh — the Incarnation
|===

The Word who spoke creation into being IS the Son who became flesh.

== Greek Semantic Range

λόγος carries rich meaning in Greek:

* *Word* — spoken or written expression
* *Reason* — the rational faculty
* *Logic* — the principle of coherence
* *Account* — explanation, reckoning
* *Ordering Principle* — the structure by which reality makes sense

Greek philosophy recognized the Logos as the rational structure of the universe. John reveals: that Logos is a Person — the eternal Son of God.

== The Incarnation Pattern

The Logos didn't stay abstract. He became concrete:

* Eternal → entered time
* Infinite → became finite
* Creator → became creature
* Glory → took servant form

This pattern informs Kingdom Technology: paradigm must become implementation, definitions must become working systems.
