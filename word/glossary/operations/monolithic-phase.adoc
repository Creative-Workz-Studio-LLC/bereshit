= monolithic-phase

*Definition:* The natural growth stage where a file accumulates content before distribution. A cocoon, not a failure.

[quote, Genesis 1:2 KJV]
____
And the earth was without form, and void; and darkness was upon the face of the deep.
____

== The Insight

Files WILL grow large as work proceeds. That's natural — things get added where work is happening. The monolithic phase is the cocoon. The work is knowing *when* and *how* to distribute, not preventing growth.

== What It Is

* A natural lifecycle stage
* Accumulation before organization
* The cocoon before the butterfly
* Content gathering in one place
* Organic growth following work patterns

== What It Is NOT

* A failure of architecture
* Something to prevent
* A sign of bad practice
* Permanent state

== Why It Works

Fighting organic growth wastes energy. Let things accumulate where work happens, maintain link:structural-scaffolding.adoc[structural-scaffolding], and distribute when natural boundaries become visible. Growth then distribution, not prevention.
