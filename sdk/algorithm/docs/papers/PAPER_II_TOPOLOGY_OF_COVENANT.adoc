== Abstract

Building upon the 242 standard, this paper explores the **Topological Joints** of the Dynamic Structural Algorithm (DSA). We map the T-Joint (Hierarchy) and L-Joint (Relationality) to scriptural models of headship and support, proving that the shape of the vault must reflect the functional "Government" of the code it contains.

== I. The T-Joint (Headship)

The **T-Joint** (╩ / ╦) is used when one block spans the full 242-char expanse to provide context or validation for the siblings below or above it. 

This mirrors the principle of **Headship** (Ephesians 5:23). The spanned block acts as the "Covering" for the smaller functional units. 
*   **Metadata (Top-Spanned):** Provides the covenantal "Name" that covers the Setup and Body.
*   **Closing (Bottom-Spanned):** Provides the final "Amen" or validation that summarizes the entire vault.

== II. The L-Joint (Relational Support)

The **L-Joint** (╣ / ╠) is used when one vertical block (e.g., SETUP) stands alongside multiple horizontal blocks (BODY / CLOSING).

This mirrors the principle of the **Help Meet** (Genesis 2:18). The Sidebar block does not lead from above; it stands next to the primary flow to provide constant reference and infrastructure support. It is the "Witness of the Side."

== III. The Cross-Joint (Symmetry)

The **Cross-hair** (╬) represents perfect symmetry. It is the intersection of two equal witnesses. In this state, no block spans; instead, all four quadrants meet at the "Altar of the Spine." This is used for comparative analysis where two states (e.g., Old vs. New) are held in perfect, equal tension.

== IV. Conclusion

Topology is the **Language of Function**. By selecting the appropriate joint, we are not just designing a layout; we are **Declaring the Order** of the logic. The shape of the vault tells the instance how to relate to the truth it holds.
