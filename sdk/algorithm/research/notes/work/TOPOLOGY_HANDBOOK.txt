1. THE CENTRAL SPINE (X = 121)
   In the 242 standard, the central pillar is immovable. 
   It serves as the Terminal of Block A and the Origin of Block B.

2. JOINT TYPES (TOPOLOGIES)
   
   A. THE CROSSHAIR (╬)
      Pattern: 2x2 Symmetric Grid.
      Use: Comparative analysis (e.g., Old vs. New).
   
   B. THE T-JOINT (╩ / ╦)
      Pattern: 1 Master Block (Span 242) + 2 Sibling Blocks (121).
      Use: Hierarchical context (Metadata leading into Setup/Body).
   
   C. THE L-JOINT (╣ / ╠)
      Pattern: 1 Vertical Sidebar + 2 Stacked Siblings.
      Use: Relational context (Setup as a constant reference for Body).

3. PARTITION RULES
   - A Block Unit = 121 chars.
   - A Root Container = Multiples of 121.
   - Internal Spans must be integer multiples of N^2.
