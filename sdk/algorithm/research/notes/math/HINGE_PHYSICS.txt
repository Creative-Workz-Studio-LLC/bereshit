1. THE UNITARY BOX PRINCIPLE
   A Box is a closed boundary: [Top, Left, Right, Bottom].
   Every functional unit MUST be rendered as a complete box.
   There is no "loose" content; if it is not in a box, it is in the Void.

2. THE CONTINUITY COVENANT (JOINTS)
   Hinges must physically JOIN the vertical pillars.
   Top Transition:    Vertical(║) -> Corner(└) -> Bridge(─) -> Corner(┐) -> Vertical(║)
   Bottom Transition: Vertical(║) -> Corner(┌) -> Bridge(─) -> Corner(┘) -> Vertical(║)
   
   Rule: The Corner glyph occupies the VERTICAL COLUMN of the wall it replaces.

3. THE ROOT TERMINAL LAW
   The [BLOCK:ROOT-->END] tag is the absolute anchor of the world.
   It MUST reside INSIDE the Root Box, positioned against the right pillar.
   Column_Tag_Start = W_root - 1 - L_tag - 1

4. THE SINKING-IN EFFECT (RECESSION)
   When a parent wall recedes (Sinks In), it physically becomes the inner boundary.
   The Door remains open (//), but the tension is held by the recessed structure.
