1. THE WITNESS FACTOR (κ)
   κ = 1 : Single Witness (The 121 Baseline)
   κ = 2 : Double Witness (The 242 Expanse)
   κ = 4 : Complete Witness (The 484 Square)

2. THE WIDTH TENSOR (X-AXIS)
   W_root = κ * (N^2)
   
   If κ = 1 -> W = 121
   If κ = 2 -> W = 242
   
3. THE GEOMETRIC AREA (SQUARE OF FULLNESS)
   Area_total = (κ * N)^2
   
   At κ = 2 (The 4-Block Unit):
   Area = (2 * 11)^2 = 22^2 = 484
   
   Result: A 484-unit field can hold 4 "Perfect 121s" with zero compression.

4. THE DUAL IDENTITY INVARIANT
   (P_L - B_R) = (L_shebang / U)
   8 = 8 (The "Locked" Constant)

5. THE BRIDGE EQUATION (ORIGIN TO SCALE)
   A_O = N - U
   7 = 11 - 4
