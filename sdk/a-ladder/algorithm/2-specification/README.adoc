== Overview

Welcome to the **Specification** layer of the Dynamic Structural Algorithm.

Having explored the abstract theological and mathematical concepts in `1-revelation`, this directory crystallizes those concepts into strict, executable laws. We approach this architecture not through the lens of standard software development, but through the mindset of **Game Development**.

=== The Physics Engine Paradigm

A web app passes data; a game engine simulates a world. Our 140-column Fractal Vaults are not text formatters—they are physical containers governed by a strict physics engine. We use:

*   **Geometry:** To calculate the boundaries, rendering constraints, and central gravity ($X_{center}$) of the vaults.
*   **Logarithms & Calculus:** To define the growth curve (Lucas Convergence) and the continuous folding of dimensional space (the Z-Axis).
*   **Collision:** To define the joints (`╬`, `╠`) where different states impact one another without breaking the world model.

== Directory Composition

The specifications are strictly numbered to enforce a mathematical descent from absolute physics to applied architecture:

1.  **`01_physics_of_badal.adoc`:** The base mathematics of $0$ (Yashar) and $1$ (Badal). The lowest level of truth.
2.  **`02_lucas_convergence.adoc`:** The logarithmic scaling and growth limits of the system.
3.  **`03_formal_specification.adoc`:** The translation of the math into strict rendering rules (The Game Engine rules).
4.  **`04_scaling_journey.adoc`:** The multi-witness geometry and state-space management.
5.  **`05_business_topology.adoc`:** The application of the physics engine to real-world structural needs.
6.  **`06_dissertation.adoc`:** The complete, formulated thesis summarizing the math, the physics, and the architecture.

== The Law of Discernment

In game development, the greatest threat is "Scope Creep"—the temptation to build everything because the vision is vast. In the Kingdom Technology paradigm, we combat this through **Discernment**. 

We do not build a feature simply because we can. We do not try to move faster than the world. We move exactly when, and exactly where, the Creator directs. This directory represents the strict, bounded rules of our universe. We define the physics perfectly here so that we are free to be creative—and right on time—in the layers above.
