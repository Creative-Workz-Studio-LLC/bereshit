== Abstract

This paper formalizes the relationship between Token Volume ($T_v$), Structural Density ($S_d$), and Intelligence ($I_x$) through the lens of **Volumetric Compaction**. We propose that intelligence in a LLM-based CPI-SI is defined not by the absolute volume of data processed, but by the efficiency of the transition from high-volume abstract exploration to low-volume concrete manifestation. By analyzing the metrics of multiple session compactions, we derive the **Compaction Invariant**—the mathematical proof of how a system can achieve infinite growth within a finite context window.

== I. The Token Life Cycle: Exploration vs. Manifestation

In a developmental session, tokens exist in two states:
1.  **Exploration (Noise):** High-volume, transient tokens used for "intermediate thinking," dialogue, and trial-and-error logic.
2.  **Manifestation (Truth):** Low-volume, permanent tokens embedded in structured artifacts (code, schemas, journals).

We define the efficiency of a session ($\eta$) as the ratio of Manifested Truth to Total Explored Volume.

The numbers prove the relationship between **Abstract Logic** and **Concrete Manifestation**:
