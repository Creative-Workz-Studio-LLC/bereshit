== 1. The Principle of Recursive Compression (The Z-Axis)

When a functional block is nested inside a Master Vault, it does not indent arbitrarily. It compresses symmetrically to create the illusion of Z-axis depth while maintaining a balanced center of gravity on the X-axis.

=== The Centered Gravity Axiom

Let $W_{root}$ be the total width of the Master Vault (e.g., 140 columns).
The exact center point ($X_{center}$) is:

[stem]
++++
X_{center} = \frac{W_{root}}{2}
++++

Any nested box (Level 1, Level 2, Level $n$) must be drawn so that its midpoint perfectly aligns with $X_{center}$. If the Master Vault spans columns 1 to 140, its center is 70. An inner 80-column box MUST span columns 30 to 110 to maintain Yashar (Balance).

== 2. The Physics of the Cross Joint (`╬`)

In the 2D plane of the vault, a vertical line `║` represents a **Constant State** (e.g., "I am inside the Metadata block"). A horizontal line `══` represents a **Transition of State** (e.g., "Moving from Setup to Body").

=== The Permeability Axiom

When a horizontal transition (`══`) impacts a vertical wall (`║`), the resulting joint is dictated by structural permeability:

*   **T-Joint (`╣` or `╠`):** The transition stops at the wall. The state change only affects the area inside the boundary.
*   **Cross Joint (`╬`):** The transition punches *through* the vertical wall. 

A horizontal transition can only form a `╬` if the new state applies to *both sides* of the wall simultaneously. If the wall is absolute (like the outer edge of the Master Vault), it has zero permeability; it can never form a `╬` and must terminate as a Corner (`╗` or `╝`).

== 3. The Intervention Vector (`!`)

In the structural mapping of the vault, the `!` symbol (`//!║`) is introduced as a modifier to the standard wall (`// ║`).

In standard logic, `!` denotes "NOT" or "Inverse". 
If $Wall = +1$ and $Door = -1$, then $!Wall$ represents a **Forced Override**. 

=== The Alert State Axiom

The `!` acts as a "flare" on the Z-axis. It mathematically signals to the parser (or the AI reading the context window) that the content contained within this specific boundary is not machine-executable logic, but **High-Priority Human Intent** (e.g., `[HUMAN:ANCHOR]`). It deliberately breaks the visual monotony of the `║` pillar to draw cognitive attention, bypassing standard structural processing.
