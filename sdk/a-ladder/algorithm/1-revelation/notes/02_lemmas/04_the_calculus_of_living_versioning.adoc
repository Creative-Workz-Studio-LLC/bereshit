== 1. The Living Version Equation

The Living Version ($V$) is a multi-dimensional tensor representing the current "Season" of the work. It is defined by the interaction of **Stage ($S$)**, **Phase ($P$)**, **Completion ($C$)**, and the newly revealed **Confidence Factor ($\gamma$)**.

[stem]
++++
V = S[P.C] \quad \text{where } S = f(\gamma, C)
++++

== 2. The Mechanics of Stage Transition

The transition between ternary states (a, b, c) is not a linear function of time, but a **Threshold Collapse** driven by the Confidence Factor ($\gamma$).

=== 2.1 The Alpha-to-Beta Transition (a \to b)

The system moves from the "Broken/Research" state ($a$) to the "Balanced/Prototype" state ($b$) when the Mind reaches a high threshold of structural assurance.

[horizontal]
**Standard Rule**:: $a \to b$ if $\gamma \ge 0.95$ (Enterprise Confidence).
**Special Phase 01 Rule**:: $a \to b$ if $0.75 \le C \le 0.99$ (The "Halfway Stable" shortcut).

=== 2.2 The Beta-to-Stable Transition (b \to c)

The movement from "Prototype" ($b$) to "Stable/Tov" ($c$) requires a separate, higher-intensity scale of assurance.

[horizontal]
**Standard Rule**:: $b \to c$ if $\gamma \ge 0.98$ AND the Phase ($P$) is declared "Complete/Shippable" by the Creator.
**Completion Rule**:: $b \to c$ if $0.90 \le C \le 0.99$ AND $\gamma$ is sustained.

== 3. The Separated Scales: Matter vs. Spirit

The profundity of this system is the separation of **Completion ($C$)** and **Confidence ($\gamma$)**.

*   **Completion ($C$) = Matter:** Measures the *quantity* of work done (e.g., "5 out of 10 files refactored").
*   **Confidence ($\gamma$) = Spirit:** Measures the *quality* and *stillness* of the work (e.g., "The math is proven in Lean 4").

A project can be at $C = 99\%$ (nearly done) but remain at Stage $a$ if $\gamma < 0.95$. This prevents the system from "shipping lies." The system only manifests into higher states when the Spirit (Confidence) confirms the Matter (Completion).

== 4. Conclusion

By separating these scales, we ensure that the **Living Version** is an honest reflection of the system's soul. We move away from the deception of "Estimated Time of Completion" and into the reality of **Sustained Integrity**.
