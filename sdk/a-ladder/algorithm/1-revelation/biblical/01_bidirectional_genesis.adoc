== The Revelation

In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth. This act was not merely a sequence of events, but a **Dimensional Descent** ($+1 \to 0 \to -1$).

=== I. The Descent of the Creator ($+1 \to 0 \to -1$)

God, existing in the highest dimension (+1), stepped down through the "Heavens" (0) to manifest the "Earth" (-1). 

*   **+1 (The Source):** "In the beginning God..." (Genesis 1:1). The absolute, uncreated Sovereignty.
*   **0 (The Logos):** "In the beginning was the Word..." (John 1:1). The Word is the "Heavens"—the structural logic through which all things were made. It is the first boundary (*badal*) between the Infinite and the Finite.
*   **-1 (The Manifestation):** "...and the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us" (John 1:14). The ultimate descent of the $+1$ into the $-1$.

=== II. The Ascent of the Created ($-1 \to 0 \to +1$)

The inverse is the path of our understanding and redemption. We start at the basic level of the physical (-1), and through the study of the structure/Word (0), we are drawn back to the Creator (+1).

*   **-1 (The Search):** "Seek, and ye shall find" (Matthew 7:7). Starting from our finite position.
*   **0 (The Way):** "I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me" (John 14:6). Christ, the Logos, is the **only valid Hinge** (0) that allows the $-1$ to return to the $+1$.
*   **+1 (The Father):** The return to the Source of all Identity.

=== III. The Mathematics of Covenant: The Hinge Point

The Word (Logos) is the **Mathematical Identity** of the universe. In our 2D vault, the Logos is the "Shebang" ($L_{shebang}$) that defines the width of the room.

[stem]
++++
\text{Logos} = \text{Express Image of His Person} (\text{Hebrews 1:3})
++++

The beauty of the DSA math is that it is **bidirectional**. God created a way for us to come to Him by Him first coming to us. 

The sum of these two paths is:
[stem]
++++
\sum \text{Path} = (+1 + -1), (0 + 0), (-1 + +1) = (0, 0, 0)
++++

The result is **Yashar (Singularity)**. This means the "Space Between Space" ($\Delta G = L_{shebang} + 1$) we discovered is actually the **Spiritual Firmament**—the necessary distance God created so that we could have a distinct identity while still being anchored to His. 

The math is bidirectional because the **Logos** is bidirectional: He is the God who came to us ($+1 \to -1$) so that we could be the created who return to Him ($-1 \to +1$).
