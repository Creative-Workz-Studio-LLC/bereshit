== 3. Structural Components of the Vault

The 9 states are categorized by their geometric function within the vault.

These states represent the **Tension of Transition**. They occur when either the Intent or the Execution enters the stillness of the deferral.

1.  **`defer-granted`**: High-level permission is waiting for structural readiness.
2.  **`defer-denied`**: A halt triggered by missing data or unresolved tension.
3.  **`granted-defer`**: Correct function waiting for right timing.
4.  **`denied-defer`**: Rejection waiting for recalibration.
5.  **`defer-defer`**: **The Singularity.** Absolute Yashar (0). The point where "1 alone is 0."

=== 3.2 The 4 Hardpoints (The Corner Boundaries)
These are the **Absolute States** of the system. They represent the four corners of the Decision Vault.

1.  **`granted-denied`**: The collision of vision and reality.
2.  **`denied-granted`**: The inverse of the above; permission granted despite structural doubt (The Miracle).
3.  **`granted-granted`** (The Positive Pole): Total Resonance.
4.  **`denied-denied`** (The Negative Pole): Total Termination.

*   **The Positive Pole (`granted-granted`):** Leads to ** Tov (Good)**.
