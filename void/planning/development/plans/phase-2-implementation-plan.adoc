[[biblical-foundation]]
== Biblical Foundation

[NOTE]
====
*Just Weight and Balance:* Proverbs 11:1 establishes the principle that accurate measurement honors God. Phase 2 implements mathematical operations that must be precise, lossless, and faithful — computing that measures rightly.
====

_"A false balance is abomination to the LORD: but a just weight is his delight."_ — *Proverbs 11:1 KJV*

_"A just weight and balance are the LORD's: all the weights of the bag are his work."_ — *Proverbs 16:11 KJV*

*Applied:* Phase 2's mathematical operations reflect the biblical principle of honest measurement:

[cols="2,4",options="header"]
|===
| Principle | Phase 2 Application

| *Just weight*
| Arithmetic produces correct results — no shortcuts, no approximations where precision matters

| *True balance*
| Conversion preserves meaning — stored↔true transformation without loss

| *Honest measure*
| Normalization serves understanding — knowing when precision is critical vs acceptable tolerance
|===

This is the analog-digital bridge in practice. Analog computing has margin of error — not a weakness, but wisdom. Not everything is mission-critical. A "just balance" knows when to be precise and when tolerance is acceptable. The CENTER (0) in ternary represents this equilibrium — "within tolerance."

[cols="2,3,3",options="header"]
|===
| Operation Type | Biblical Principle | Phase 2 Implementation

| *Arithmetic*
| "Just weight"
| Add, subtract, negate with proper carry handling

| *Conversion*
| "True balance"
| stored↔true preserving meaning through transformation

| *Normalization*
| "Honest measure"
| Bases and levels for appropriate precision (coarse vs fine)

| *Calculus*
| "Discernment"
| Rate of change detection — knowing WHEN to pay attention
|===
